Fiji Travel Weather Guide

Fiji's historical weather data (2021–2025) shows a clear split between a hot, wet season and a cooler, drier one. January is the standout month to avoid if you dislike rain: it averages 29.6 rain days per month, meaning almost constant downpours, alongside highs near 26°C. By contrast, June and July offer the most reliable conditions, with highs around 22°C and only 11.8–14.4 rain days per month. Practically, this means planning outdoor activities, island hops and beach days around the June–August window, while the December–March period suits travelers who accept frequent showers in exchange for lush scenery and warm temperatures.

Practical tips

  • Target June–August for the most dependable weather: highs around 22°C and the fewest rain days (11.8–15.6), making this the best window for beaches, hiking and island transfers.
  • Avoid January if rain bothers you — with 29.6 rain days per month, almost every day sees measurable precipitation, so build in indoor activities and flexible transport.
  • Pack for both heat and rain year-round: temperatures stay warm (highs 21.6–26.1°C), but rain days remain high in every month, so a light waterproof layer is essential.
  • Expect peak crowds and higher prices in July, August and December–January; shoulder months like June and September offer good conditions with fewer visitors.
  • Use the cooler, drier months for outdoor adventures and the warmer wet months for cultural activities, spa time and short dry-window excursions.
