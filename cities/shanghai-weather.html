Shanghai Travel Weather Guide

The data shows Shanghai's most comfortable travel windows fall in spring and autumn. April and October stand out with highs around 22–24°C, lows of 12–17°C, and moderate rain (9.8 and 8.0 days respectively), making long days of walking and sightseeing practical. The hardest months are July and August, when highs of 32.7–33.6°C combine with lows near 26°C and 12–17 rain days — expect oppressive humidity, sudden downpours, and heavy tourist crowds. January and December are the driest (4–5 rain days) but chilly, with lows near 1–3°C, so pack layers if visiting in winter.

Practical tips

  • Aim for April–May or October–November for the best balance of comfortable temperatures and manageable rain.
  • June–September brings frequent downpours and high humidity — pack a compact umbrella, waterproof shoes, and quick-dry clothing.
  • Winter (December–February) is dry but cold, with lows near 1–3°C; a warm coat and layers are essential, and crowds are lowest.
  • Rain days peak in June and July (17+ days), so build indoor options like museums and malls into your itinerary.
  • Book accommodation and major attractions early for July–August and public holidays, when crowds hit their seasonal high.
