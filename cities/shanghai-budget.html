Shanghai is China's most cosmopolitan city — a dazzling mix of historic longtang alleys, futuristic skylines, and world-class dining. While it can be an expensive destination by mainland Chinese standards, it's entirely possible to experience Shanghai on a backpacker's budget or in five-star luxury. This guide breaks down realistic daily costs across three travel styles — budget, mid-range, and luxury — covering accommodation, food, transport, and activities. All prices are in Chinese Yuan (CNY / ¥) with approximate USD equivalents (1 USD ≈ 7.2 CNY). Costs are per person per day unless otherwise noted.

Detailed Cost Breakdown

🏨 Accommodation

  • Hostels: Dorm beds ¥80–150/night. Private hostel rooms ¥250–400. Best areas: Jing'an, the French Concession (Xuhui), and near People's Square.
  • Mid-range hotels: ¥400–800/night for a clean 3–4★ room. International chains (Holiday Inn, Novotel) and well-rated local boutiques are good value.
  • Luxury: ¥1,500–4,000+/night. The Bund and Lujiazui (Pudong) command the highest rates; book well in advance for skyline views.
  • Alternative: Serviced apartments (¥500–1,000/night) suit longer stays and families.
