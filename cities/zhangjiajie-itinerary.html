Zhangjiajie 3-Day Itinerary: The Complete Travel Guide

Zhangjiajie, in Hunan Province, China, is home to the UNESCO-listed Wulingyuan Scenic Area, famous for its towering quartz-sandstone pillars that inspired the floating mountains in Avatar. The region combines three main zones—Zhangjiajie National Forest Park (Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain), Tianmen Mountain, and the Grand Canyon with its glass bridge—plus the ancient town of Fenghuang within reach. This guide lays out a practical three-day itinerary with morning, afternoon, and evening schedules, restaurant suggestions, and tips to help you navigate tickets, crowds, and transport efficiently.

Day 1: Zhangjiajie National Forest Park — Yuanjiajie and Golden Whip Stream

Time Activity Details
Morning (7:00–12:00) Enter the park and hike Golden Whip Stream Enter via the Forest Park gate (森林公园门票站) and walk the flat 7.5 km Golden Whip Stream (金鞭溪) trail. It takes 2–3 hours at a relaxed pace and passes dramatic pillars, macaques, and clear water. Arrive at the park entrance by 7:00–7:30 to beat tour groups.
Afternoon (12:00–17:00) Bailong Elevator up to Yuanjiajie Take the shuttle to the Bailong Elevator (百龙天梯), a 326 m glass lift built into the cliff, then explore Yuanjiajie (袁家界): the First Bridge Under Heaven (天下第一桥) and the Hallelujah Mountain viewpoint. Shuttles connect viewpoints; expect queues at the elevator mid-day.
Evening (17:00 onward) Descend and dine in Wulingyuan town Take the shuttle and elevator or cable car down before closing (park shuttles stop around 18:00–19:00 depending on season). Stay overnight in Wulingyuan town near the Wulingyuan gate for easy access the next morning.

Restaurant recommendations (Day 1): In Wulingyuan town, try Laozhangjia Sanxiaguo (老张家三下锅) for the local "three-pot" stew, or Hunan Flavor Restaurant near the Wulingyuan gate for classic Hunan dishes like stir-fried pork with chili. For a quick lunch inside the park, bring snacks or eat at the simple canteens near Bailong Elevator.

Day 2: Tianzi Mountain and Tianmen Mountain

Time Activity Details
Morning (7:00–11:30) Tianzi Mountain (天子山) Enter via the Wulingyuan gate, take the shuttle to Tianzi Mountain, and ride the cable car or walk up. Key viewpoints: Imperial Writing Brush Peaks (御笔峰) and Warrior Taming Horse. Mornings offer the best chance of clear views and fewer crowds.
Midday (11:30–13:00) Transfer to Tianmen Mountain Exit the park and drive or take a taxi (~40–50 minutes) to Tianmen Mountain cable car station in Zhangjiajie city. Buy tickets in advance; the cable car is the world's longest passenger cableway at 7,455 m.
Afternoon (13:00–17:30) Tianmen Mountain (天门山) Ride the cable car up, walk the cliff-hanging glass walkway, visit Tianmen Cave (Heaven's Gate), and optionally take the 999-step stairway. The 99-bend road is visible from the summit. Allow 3–4 hours.
Evening (17:30 onward) Dinner and rest in Zhangjiajie city Stay overnight in Zhangjiajie city (Yongding district) to be close to transport links and restaurants.

Restaurant recommendations (Day 2): In Zhangjiajie city, Hu Shifu Sanxiaguo (胡师傅三下锅) is a well-known spot for the signature three-pot dish. Qin Ma Ma serves home-style Hunan food. For a lighter option, try a local noodle shop for mifeng (rice noodles) with braised beef.

Day 3: Grand Canyon Glass Bridge and Fenghuang (or Baofeng Lake)

Time Activity Details
Morning (8:00–11:30) Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon and Glass Bridge The glass-bottomed bridge spans 430 m across the canyon at 300 m height. Book timed tickets in advance (entry is limited and often sells out). Walk the bridge, then explore the canyon trail and optional boat ride. Allow 2–3 hours.
Midday (11:30–13:00) Lunch and transfer Eat near the Grand Canyon or return toward the city. If continuing to Fenghuang, depart by early afternoon (bus or private car, ~2.5–3 hours).
Afternoon (13:00–17:00) Option A: Fenghuang Ancient Town / Option B: Baofeng Lake Option A: Explore Fenghuang's stilt houses along the Tuo River, ancient city walls, and East Gate Tower. Option B: Stay local and take a boat on Baofeng Lake (宝峰湖), a scenic reservoir surrounded by cliffs, with Tujia folk songs performed on boats.
Evening (17:00 onward) Sunset and dinner In Fenghuang, enjoy the illuminated riverside at night and dinner at a riverside restaurant. If staying local, return to Zhangjiajie city for dinner and departure prep.

Restaurant recommendations (Day 3): In Fenghuang, try Laoying Restaurant for blood duck (血粑鸭) and sour fish. Near the Grand Canyon, simple farmhouse restaurants serve local smoked pork and wild vegetables. In Zhangjiajie city, Xibu Street (溪布街) area has many casual eateries and snack stalls.

Practical Tips

  • Tickets: The Zhangjiajie National Forest Park ticket (valid 4 days) covers park entry and shuttles but not cable cars or the Bailong Elevator, which are charged separately. Buy Tianmen Mountain and Glass Bridge tickets online in advance—timed entries sell out, especially in peak season.
  • Best time to visit: April–June and September–November offer mild weather and clearer views. July–August is hot and crowded; winter can be icy but quiet and atmospheric.
  • Crowds: Start early (before 7:30) to avoid tour groups. Weekdays are calmer than weekends and Chinese public holidays.
  • Weather: Mist and fog are common and can obscure viewpoints; build flexibility into your plan and check forecasts. Bring a light rain jacket.
  • Footwear: Expect thousands of steps and steep stairs—wear sturdy, broken-in hiking shoes.
