Yangshuo: A Perfect 3-Day Itinerary

Yangshuo, set amid the dramatic karst peaks of Guangxi along the Li River, is one of China's most scenic destinations. This three-day itinerary balances iconic landscapes, outdoor activity, and local culture, with morning, afternoon, and evening schedules plus restaurant recommendations and practical tips. It assumes you are based in Yangshuo town, with Day 1 focused on the Li River and town, Day 2 on the Yulong River and countryside, and Day 3 on hiking and departure.

Day 1: Li River, West Street, and the Impression Show

Time Activity Notes
Morning Li River cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo (or a shorter Xingping section cruise) The classic Guilin–Yangshuo cruise takes about 4–5 hours; book ahead. The Xingping–Nine Horse Fresco Hill section is shorter and captures the scenery on the 20-yuan note.
Afternoon Check in, then stroll West Street (Xi Jie) and the riverside West Street is touristy but lively; the quieter riverside paths offer better views and fewer crowds.
Evening Watch "Impression Sanjie Liu" (Liu Sanjie) outdoor light show Staged on the Li River with karst peaks as backdrop; book tickets in advance and arrive early for good seats.

Restaurants: Lucy's Place (Western and Chinese, popular with travelers), Rosewood Cafe (riverside setting), and local beer fish (pijiu yu) at any reputable riverside restaurant.

Day 2: Yulong River and Countryside by Bike

Time Activity Notes
Morning Bike or e-bike the Yulong River loop Rent from town; the route passes Moon Hill, bamboo rafts, and rice paddies. Allow 3–4 hours.
Afternoon Bamboo raft on the Yulong River; optional Moon Hill climb Rafts are calmer and less crowded than the Li River. Moon Hill has about 800 steps to a natural arch viewpoint.
Evening Dinner in town and a cooking class or tai chi session (optional) Many guesthouses arrange cooking classes featuring local dishes.

Restaurants: Pure Lotus Vegetarian (near West Street), Giggling Tree (countryside, near Moon Hill), and farmhouse restaurants along the Yulong River serving fresh river fish and vegetables.

Day 3: Xianggong Hill, Xingping, and Departure

Time Activity Notes
Morning Sunrise hike at Xianggong Hill (about 20–30 minutes up) Famous panorama over the Li River bend; go early for mist and light.
Afternoon Explore Xingping old town and the 20-yuan note viewpoint Short walk from town; combine with a riverside stroll.
Evening Return to Yangshuo or transfer onward to Guilin Buses and private transfers run regularly; allow buffer time.

Restaurants: Xingping local eateries for river fish and rice noodles, and Mei You Cafe in Yangshuo for coffee and light meals before departure.

Practical Tips

  • Best time to visit: April–October for green scenery and rafting; May–June can be rainy, and July–August is hot and busy. Autumn offers clear skies.
  • Getting there: Fly or train to Guilin, then bus or private transfer to Yangshuo (about 1.5 hours). The Li River cruise is a scenic alternative.
  • Getting around: Rent bikes or e-bikes for the countryside; taxis and ride-hailing are limited outside town, so plan returns.
  • Booking: Reserve Li River cruises, the Impression show, and popular guesthouses in advance, especially in peak season.
  • Money and connectivity: Carry cash for small vendors; mobile payment (WeChat/Alipay) is widely used. Buy a local SIM or eSIM for maps and translation apps.
  • Safety and etiquette: Wear sturdy shoes for hikes, use sun protection, and respect local villages and farmland. Bargain politely at markets.
