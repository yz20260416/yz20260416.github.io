```html Vienna Travel Cost Guide

Vienna consistently ranks among the world's most livable cities, and while it is not the cheapest European capital, it offers excellent value for travelers who plan ahead. Prices vary significantly depending on your travel style — from hostel-hopping backpackers to luxury-seeking visitors — and understanding the cost structure of accommodation, food, transport, and activities will help you build a realistic budget. This guide breaks down typical daily and per-trip costs across three travel styles: budget, mid-range, and luxury, with all figures in euros (€) per person unless otherwise noted. Prices reflect 2024–2025 averages and should be treated as planning estimates.

Accommodation

Vienna's accommodation ranges from well-run hostels to grand historic hotels. Booking 4–8 weeks in advance and avoiding major event periods (e.g., ball season in January–February, the Christmas market peak in December) yields the best rates. The inner districts (1–9) are priciest; districts 10–20 offer better value with quick U-Bahn connections to the center.

  • Budget Hostel dorms: €25–45/night; private hostel rooms: €60–90.
  • Mid-Range 3–4★ hotels and pensions: €90–160/night; aparthotels and Airbnb-style apartments: €80–140.
  • Luxury 5★ properties (e.g., near the Ringstrasse or Opera): €250–600+/night; suites and palace hotels can exceed €1,000.

Food & Drink

Vienna is famous for its coffeehouse culture and hearty cuisine. Lunch specials (Mittagsmenü) at restaurants typically cost €10–15 and are one of the best value meals in the city. Street food (sausages at a Würstelstand, kebabs) keeps costs low, while traditional Beisl taverns and Michelin-starred dining anchor the mid and high ends.

  • Budget Supermarket meals and self-catering: €5–10/day; Würstelstand sausage: €4–6; lunch specials: €10–15; coffeehouse coffee: €4–5.
  • Mid-Range Sit-down dinners at traditional restaurants: €15–25 per main; a three-course dinner with wine: €45–80/day total.
  • Luxury Fine dining (tasting menus): €120–250+ per person; top coffeehouse pastries and wine-bar evenings add €30–60.

Transport

Vienna's public transport network (U-Bahn, trams, buses) is excellent, safe, and affordable. A single ticket costs €2.60, but passes deliver far better value. The city is also highly walkable and bike-friendly (WienMobil bike sharing is available).

  • 24-hour pass: €8.00
  • 48-hour pass: €14.10
  • 72-hour pass: €17.10
  • Weekly pass: €17.10 (Monday–Sunday validity)
  • Vienna City Card: from ~€29 (24h) — includes transport plus museum discounts
  • Airport transfer: CAT train €14.90; S-Bahn (S7) €4.60; taxi
