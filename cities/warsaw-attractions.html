Warsaw Top 10 Attractions

Warsaw is a phoenix city — rebuilt from rubble into a vibrant European capital where history meets modernity. From the meticulously restored Old Town (a UNESCO site) to world-class museums and lush parks, the city offers a wealth of experiences for every traveler. Below are the top 10 attractions, with essential details on ticket prices and practical tips to help you plan a smooth visit.

# Attraction Description Ticket price (approx.)
1 Old Town (Stare Miasto) UNESCO-listed heart of Warsaw, painstakingly reconstructed after WWII. Cobblestone lanes, colorful facades, the Royal Castle, and vibrant market square. Free to wander; castle entry is extra. Free (Royal Castle: 30–70 PLN)
2 Royal Castle (Zamek Królewski) Baroque palace on Castle Square, former residence of Polish kings. Rich interiors, art collections, and the famous Canaletto Room. Book online to skip lines. 70 PLN (adult); 50 PLN reduced
3 POLIN Museum of the History of Polish Jews Modern, award-winning museum on the site of the former Warsaw Ghetto. Powerful 1,000-year history of Polish Jews, stunning architecture. Allow 3+ hours. 45 PLN (adult); 30 PLN reduced
4 Warsaw Uprising Museum Immersive museum dedicated to the 1944 uprising. Atmospheric exhibits, original artifacts, a replica bomber, and a moving film. A must for history buffs. 35 PLN (adult); 30 PLN reduced
5 Łazienki Park & Palace on the Isle Warsaw’s largest park (76 ha) with a neoclassical palace, amphitheater, and free-roaming peacocks. Chopin concerts in summer. Perfect for a leisurely stroll. Park free; Palace: 40 PLN (adult); 20 PLN reduced
6 Palace of Culture and Science (PKiN) Stalin-era skyscraper, iconic symbol of Warsaw. 30th-floor terrace offers panoramic views. Includes theaters, museums, and a swimming pool. A classic photo stop. Viewing terrace:
