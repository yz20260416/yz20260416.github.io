Bali Travel Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Planning

The data shows a striking split. The most comfortable months are January and December, when highs sit around 25–26°C, lows are a mild 14–15°C, and rain is rare (just 1.6–2.4 rain days). In practice, that means light layers, easy outdoor sightseeing, and little need for a rain plan. The hardest months are July, August, and September: highs of 31–32°C combine with 27–29 rain days per month, so expect heat, humidity, and near-daily downpours. April and May are transitional — hot (35–37°C) with moderate rain — while June and October straddle the extremes. Time your trip around these patterns and pack accordingly.

Practical tips

  • Target January, February, November, or December for the driest, most comfortable conditions — lows of 14–19°C mean you'll want a light jacket for evenings.
  • Avoid July–September if rain bothers you: 27–29 rain days per month means near-daily downpours, so pack waterproofs, dry bags, and shoes with grip.
  • April is the hottest month (36.9°C highs) — schedule outdoor activities for early morning and carry plenty of water.
  • Book early for January, July, August, and December, when crowds peak; shoulder months like February and November offer better value.
  • Always pack a rain layer — even in "Low" rainfall months, a few wet days are possible, and humidity stays high year-round.
