Best Time to Visit London

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are April and June. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

London's weather is famously changeable, but the 2021–2025 averages reveal clear patterns worth planning around. July is the warmest month (average high 23.0°C, low 14.1°C), making it ideal for parks, markets and long walks — though it also brings 10.6 rain days and peak crowds, so book accommodation early. August offers nearly identical warmth (22.8°C high) with the fewest rain days of the year (6.8), arguably the best overall balance of weather and comfort. The least appealing months are January (high 7.4°C, low 1.4°C) and December (high 9.2°C), when short daylight, cold mornings and frequent rain (11+ days) make outdoor sightseeing less enjoyable — pack layers, waterproofs and plan indoor attractions.

Practical tips

  • Pack layers and a compact waterproof jacket year-round — even the driest month (August, 6.8 rain days) sees regular showers.
  • Target late spring (April) or late summer (August) for the best mix of mild temperatures and fewer rain days.
  • Book accommodation and major attractions well in advance for June–August and December, when crowds peak.
  • In the wettest months (October, March, November), build indoor backups into each day — museums, galleries and theatres.
  • Expect cold mornings in winter (January lows near 1.4°C) — a warm coat, gloves and scarf are essential.
