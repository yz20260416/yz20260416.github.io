Hanoi Top 10 Attractions

Hanoi, Vietnam’s thousand-year-old capital, blends French colonial elegance with traditional Vietnamese soul. From the serene Hoan Kiem Lake to the bustling Old Quarter, the city offers a treasure trove of cultural and historical sites. Whether you’re a history buff, a foodie, or a wanderer, these ten attractions capture the essence of Hanoi. Below you’ll find descriptions, current ticket prices (in VND and approximate USD), and practical tips to make the most of your visit.

Note: Prices are as of early 2025 and may change; always check official sources before you go. Many sites accept cash only, so carry small notes.

Attraction Description Ticket price Visiting tips
1. Hoan Kiem Lake & Ngoc Son Temple Heart of Hanoi, this picturesque lake is perfect for a morning stroll. The red Huc Bridge leads to Ngoc Son Temple (Temple of the Jade Mound), dedicated to Tran Hung Dao. Legend says the lake is where Emperor Le Loi returned his magic sword to the Golden Turtle God. 30,000 VND (~$1.20) for temple entry (lake free) Go early (6–8am) to see locals doing tai chi and avoid crowds. The temple is small; combine with a walk around the lake. Weekend evenings the area becomes a pedestrian zone.
