Yangshuo Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Travel Planning

Yangshuo's climate rewards careful timing. Based on 2021–2025 averages, October and November are the standout months: highs of 28.1°C and 22.8°C, lows of 18.5°C and 13.7°C, and only about 8 rain days each—ideal for cycling the Yulong River and hiking among the karst peaks. In contrast, June is the wettest month with roughly 24 rain days, and July is the hottest with highs near 33.5°C. Practically, this means spring and early summer bring lush scenery but require rain gear and flexible plans, while autumn offers the most reliable conditions for outdoor activities.

Practical Tips

  • Time your trip for autumn: October and November combine comfortable temperatures with the lowest rain-day counts outside winter, making them the safest bet for outdoor plans.
  • Pack for rain in spring and early summer: April through June averages 20+ rain days per month, so bring a waterproof jacket, dry bag, and quick-dry footwear.
  • Beat the summer heat: In July and August, schedule cycling, rafting, and hikes for early morning, and reserve air-conditioned accommodation in advance.
  • Layer for winter: January and December lows drop near 7°C; mornings can feel damp and cold, so pack warm layers even though daytime highs reach the mid-teens.
  • Check river conditions: Heavy rain can raise water levels and affect bamboo rafting and boat trips—confirm with local operators the day before.
