Florence is one of Italy's most visited cities, and its compact historic center means most travelers can explore on foot. While accommodation and dining costs vary widely, careful planning allows a range of budgets to enjoy the city's art, architecture, and cuisine. The breakdown below reflects typical daily costs per person, based on double occupancy for mid-range and luxury categories and shared dorm accommodation for backpackers. Prices are approximate and can rise significantly during peak season (May–September) and major events.

Key Cost Details

  • Accommodation: Hostels cluster around Santa Maria Novella station. Mid-range hotels in the center book out early in summer; staying in Oltrarno or near the station can reduce rates. City hotel tax is typically €4–7 per person per night depending on star rating.
  • Food: A stand-up espresso at the bar costs about €1.20–1.50, while sitting at a table can triple the price. A panino from a street vendor runs €5–8. Cover charges (coperto) of €2–4 per person are common in sit-down restaurants.
  • Transport: The historic center is walkable. A single ATAF bus ticket costs €1.70 (valid 90 minutes); a 24-hour pass is about €5. Taxis from the airport run €22–25 flat rate to the center.
  • Activities: Uffizi and Accademia tickets are about €25 each; booking fees apply online. The Duomo complex pass is around €30. Many churches are free, and the first Sunday of each month offers free entry to state museums (expect crowds).

Practical Tips

  • Book museum tickets online in advance to skip long queues, especially for the Uffizi and Accademia (Michelangelo's David).
  • Travel in shoulder season (April, October–November) for lower accommodation rates and thinner crowds.
  • Drink from public water fountains (fontanelle) to save on bottled water.
  • Look for menù turistico fixed-price lunches at trattorias away from the Duomo for better value.
  • Students and EU citizens under 18 often qualify for reduced or free museum entry—carry ID.
  • Avoid restaurants with photo menus and touts near major sights; walk a few blocks for better prices and quality.
  • If arriving by train, the Santa Maria Novella station is a 10–15 minute walk from most central sights.
