This guide breaks down realistic daily costs across three travel styles—Budget, Mid-Range, and Luxury—so you can plan your trip with confidence. All figures are in US dollars (USD) and reflect typical 2024–2025 prices; local currency is the Vietnamese đồng (VND), roughly 1 USD ≈ 25,000 VND.

Detailed Cost Breakdown by Category

🏨 Accommodation

  • Hostels & dorms: $6–12/night in the Old Quarter; private hostel rooms $15–25.
  • Guesthouses & budget hotels: $15–30/night with A/C and private bathroom.
  • Mid-range hotels: $35–70/night, often including breakfast and rooftop views.
  • Boutique & luxury hotels: $100–300+/night (e.g., Sofitel Metropole, Apricot Hotel).
  • Serviced apartments: $500–1,200/month for long stays; popular in Tay Ho district.

🍜 Food & Drink

  • Street food: Phở $1.20–2, bánh mì $0.80–1.50, bún chả $2–3.
  • Local restaurants: $3–6 per dish; family-style meals $5–10/person.
  • Cafés: Vietnamese coffee $1–2; egg coffee $2–3.
