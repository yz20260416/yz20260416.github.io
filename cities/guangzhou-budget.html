Guangzhou Travel Budget Guide: Backpacker, Mid-Range & Luxury Costs

Guangzhou (Canton) is one of China's most affordable major cities for visitors, with a wide spread of options from dorm beds in Liwan to five-star hotels in Zhujiang New Town. Costs below are per person per day in Chinese yuan (CNY), with approximate USD equivalents at roughly 7.2 CNY = 1 USD. Prices are typical for central areas such as Tianhe, Yuexiu, and Haizhu; expect modest savings in outer districts and higher costs during the Canton Fair (April and October), when hotel rates can double or triple.

Transport Costs

  • Metro: 2–7 CNY per ride; a stored-value Yangchengtong card or mobile QR payment is convenient. Day passes are limited, so pay-per-ride is standard.
  • Bus: 2 CNY flat fare in most areas.
  • Taxi / DiDi: flag fall around 12 CNY; cross-city trips typically 30–80 CNY. Airport to city center by taxi runs roughly 120–150 CNY.
  • Airport express / metro to Baiyun Airport: about 7–20 CNY by metro; airport shuttle buses around 20–30 CNY.
  • Bike sharing: 1–2 CNY per 30 minutes.

Practical Tips

  • Avoid Canton Fair periods (mid-April and mid-October) unless attending; hotel prices surge and availability tightens.
  • Pay by phone: Alipay and WeChat Pay are accepted almost everywhere; set up an international card link before arrival. Carry some cash for small vendors.
  • Eat where locals eat: neighborhood dim sum halls and roast-meat shops (siu mei) offer excellent value, especially at breakfast.
  • Stay near a metro line: the network is cheap, fast, and covers nearly all major sights, making it the best budget transport choice.
  • Book attractions ahead: free museums and Canton Tower tickets often require online reservations, particularly on weekends.
  • Budget for summer heat: June–September is hot and humid; plan indoor activities midday and keep hydration and taxi costs in mind.
