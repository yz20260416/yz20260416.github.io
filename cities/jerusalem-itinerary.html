Jerusalem 3-Day Itinerary

Jerusalem is one of the world's most layered cities — sacred to Jews, Christians, and Muslims alike, and home to more than 4,000 years of history packed into a compact, walkable core. Three days is enough to experience the essential contrasts: the golden Dome of the Rock and the Western Wall in the Old City, the modern energy of Mahane Yehuda Market, the solemnity of Yad Vashem, and the desert vistas of the Mount of Olives. This itinerary balances major holy sites with neighborhoods, food, and downtime, and is designed to be walked (with taxis or the light rail for longer hops). Note that many sites close on Shabbat (Friday afternoon through Saturday evening), so the schedule below is ordered to account for that.

Day 1 — The Old City: Four Quarters in One Day

Time Activity Notes & Food
8:00 AM Enter through Jaffa Gate; walk the ramparts (Ramparts Walk) toward the Armenian Quarter. Arrive early to beat crowds. Grab coffee and a bourekas pastry near the gate.
9:30 AM Tower of David (Citadel) — museum and panoramic rooftop views. Allow ~1.5 hours. Great orientation to the city's history.
11:30 AM Wander the Cardo and Jewish Quarter; visit the Western Wall. Modest dress required at the Wall; men and women have separate sections.
1:00 PM Lunch in the Jewish Quarter. Try Lina (hummus, near the Via Dolorosa) or Amigo Emil for a sit-down meal.
2:30 PM Via Dolorosa and the Church of the Holy Sepulchre. Walk the 14 Stations of the Cross; the church is busiest mid-afternoon — go early if possible.
4:30 PM Muslim Quarter and a view of the Dome of the Rock from the Temple Mount approach (note: non-Muslim entry hours are limited and subject to change). Check current visiting hours in advance; security screening applies.
6:30 PM Exit via Damascus Gate; stroll the Muslim Quarter market for spices and sweets. Try kanafeh at a local sweets shop.
8:00 PM Dinner in the Old City or nearby West Jerusalem. Abu Shukri (hummus, Muslim Quarter) or Machneyuda (reserve ahead, Mahane Yehuda).

Day 2 — Mount of Olives, City of David & Modern Jerusalem

Time Activity Notes & Food
7:30 AM Sunrise walk to the Mount of Olives; panoramic view over the Old City. Best light of the day. Wear comfortable shoes — steep, uneven paths.
9:00 AM Garden of Gethsemane and Church of All Nations. Quiet, atmospheric; allow 45 minutes.
10:30 AM Descend to Kidron Valley; visit City of David and walk the Hezekiah's Tunnel (water tunnel). Book tunnel tickets online; bring water shoes and a flashlight (or use provided).
1:00 PM Lunch near the Old City / Silwan. Abu Shukri again if you missed it, or a falafel stand near Dung Gate.
2:30 PM Yad Vashem Holocaust Remembrance Center (West Jerusalem). Take a taxi or bus; allow 2–3 hours. Free entry; closed Saturdays and Jewish holidays.
5:30 PM Walk Mahane Yehuda Market as it transitions from produce to bars and restaurants. Sample halva, rugelach, and fresh juice; the market is liveliest Thursday and Saturday nights.
8:00 PM Dinner at the market or in the Nachlaot neighborhood. Machneyuda (book ahead), Azura (Iraqi/Kurdish, lunch-focused), or Crazy Noodle for casual eats.
