St. Petersburg Weather Guide for Travelers

St. Petersburg runs hot and humid for much of the year, but the data reveals two very different travel seasons. The standout months for sightseeing are November through February, when highs sit comfortably between 19.6°C and 24.9°C and rain days drop to roughly 5–8 per month—dry, mild conditions ideal for walking and outdoor attractions. In sharp contrast, June through September is the wettest stretch, with 19–22 rain days per month and highs near 31–32°C, meaning frequent downpours paired with heat and humidity. Practically, plan indoor activities or flexible morning outings if visiting in summer, and pack rain gear; for the best overall comfort, target late fall or winter.

Practical tips

  • Target November through February for the best balance of mild temperatures and few rain days; June–September is the wettest stretch.
  • If you travel in summer, carry a compact rain jacket and plan indoor backups—expect roughly 20 rain days per month.
  • Heat and humidity peak in July and August (highs near 32°C), so schedule outdoor activity for early morning or evening.
  • Book accommodations early for March–May, when warm weather coincides with high visitor demand.
  • Late fall and winter bring low crowds, making it easier to enjoy popular attractions without long waits.
