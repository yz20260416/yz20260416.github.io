St. Petersburg Weather by Month

St. Petersburg sits at 59°57′ N on the Neva River delta, where the Baltic Sea and continental air masses collide. The result is a temperate, maritime-influenced climate with short, mild summers and long, cold winters. Annual precipitation is moderate (≈660 mm), but rain is distributed fairly evenly across the year — often as light drizzle or sudden summer showers.
