Hanoi Travel Weather Guide: Month-by-Month

Hanoi's weather splits into three practical moods. November stands out as the sweet spot: highs around 26.5°C, lows near 18.9°C, and only about 7 rain days, meaning comfortable sightseeing with light layers. August is the toughest month for travelers—roughly 24.6 rain days, highs of 32.1°C, and oppressive humidity, so outdoor plans need a flexible backup. February (11.4 rain days) and the spring months bring drizzle and gray skies, while May through September is hot, sticky, and storm-prone. In short: aim for late autumn or early spring for dry comfort, and treat summer visits as heat-and-rain management exercises.

Practical Tips

  • Time your trip for November or December for the most reliable dry, comfortable weather—December has the fewest rain days (4.6) but cooler evenings.
  • Avoid August if you can: with about 24.6 rain days, heavy downpours and street flooding are common; if you must go, book refundable activities.
  • Pack for humidity year-round: breathable, quick-dry clothing from May to September, plus a compact umbrella and waterproof bag cover.
  • Schedule outdoor sightseeing in the early morning during summer months (June–August highs of 33°C) and save museums and cafés for midday heat.
  • Layer up from December to February: lows of 13–15°C feel chilly with damp air, especially on motorbike rides and near the lake at night.
