Dubai Top 10 Attractions

Dubai blends record-breaking architecture, desert adventure, and rich Arabian heritage into one unforgettable destination. From the world's tallest building to traditional souks and man-made islands, the city offers experiences for every type of traveler. Below are the ten attractions that should be on every Dubai itinerary, complete with current ticket guidance and practical visiting tips to help you plan efficiently and save money.

# Attraction Description Ticket Price (approx.) Visiting Tips
1 Burj Khalifa The world's tallest building (828 m) with observation decks on levels 124–125 and 148. Panoramic views stretch across the skyline, desert, and Gulf — spectacular at sunset. At the Top (124/125): from AED 149 (off-peak) / AED 179 (peak)
At the Top SKY (148): from AED 399 		Book online in advance for the cheapest slots; off-peak weekday mornings are least crowded. Combine with the Dubai Mall and Fountain show.
2 Dubai Mall & Dubai Fountain One of the world's largest malls with 1,200+ shops, an indoor aquarium, ice rink, and the free Dubai Fountain show — a choreographed water-and-light spectacle set to music. Mall entry: Free
Dubai Aquarium: from AED 145
Fountain show: Free 		Fountain shows run every 30 minutes from early evening; arrive 15–20 minutes early for a good spot on the boardwalk. Avoid Friday evenings if you dislike crowds.
3 Palm Jumeirah & The View at The Palm Iconic palm-shaped man-made island lined with resorts and beaches. The View observation deck on level 52 of The Palm Tower offers 360° views of the island and skyline. The View (Level 52): from AED 100
Level 54 (Next Level): from AED 175 		Visit near sunset for the best photos. Combine with a walk along the Palm's boardwalk or a beach day at one of the island's public beaches.
4 Dubai Marina & JBR Walk A glamorous waterfront district with skyscraper-lined promenades, al fresco dining, yacht cruises, and the lively Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR) beachfront. Entry: Free
Marina dhow cruise dinner: from AED 150–250 		Stroll the Marina Walk in the evening when temperatures drop. Book a dhow cruise for a different perspective; verify inclusions (dinner, transfers) before booking.
