```html Los Angeles 3-Day Itinerary — Travel Guide

Los Angeles is a sprawling, sun-soaked metropolis where world-famous beaches, legendary film studios, cutting-edge art museums, and diverse culinary scenes collide. With only three days, the key is to cluster attractions by neighborhood to minimize time in traffic. This itinerary balances the must-see icons — Hollywood, Santa Monica, and Downtown — with authentic local experiences and standout dining, so you can experience the best of L.A. without burning out.

Day 1 Hollywood & the Hills

Start with classic L.A. — the Hollywood sign, the Walk of Fame, and the star-studded hills.

Time Activity Notes & Details
8:00 AM Breakfast at Republique (624 S La Brea Ave) Acclaimed French-inspired bakery in a stunning historic building; try the pastries and shakshuka.
9:30 AM Griffith Observatory & Griffith Park Free entry; best morning views of the Hollywood Sign and city skyline. Arrive early for parking. Hike the short Charlie Turner Trail if time allows.
12:00 PM Hollywood Walk of Fame & TCL Chinese Theatre Stroll Hollywood Blvd between Highland and Vine. See the celebrity handprints at the Chinese Theatre forecourt.
1:00 PM Lunch at Grand Central Market (317 S Broadway) — or Eggslut Historic market with dozens of vendors; great for groups with varied tastes. (Note: Grand Central Market is downtown — swap for Musso & Frank Grill if staying in Hollywood.)
2:30 PM Hollywood Hills hike — Runyon Canyon or Lake Hollywood Park Runyon offers a moderate 2–3 mile loop with skyline views; Lake Hollywood Park gives a close-up sign photo op.
5:00 PM Sunset at Griffith Observatory (return visit) or Mulholland Drive overlook The observatory lawn is a prime sunset spot; planetarium shows run in the evening (tickets required).
7:30 PM Dinner at Providence (5955 Melrose Ave) — or Guelaguetza (3014 W Olympic Blvd) Providence: Michelin-starred seafood tasting menu (reserve ahead). Guelaguetza: beloved Oaxacan mole and mezcal in Koreatown — more casual and budget-friendly.

Day 2 Westside: Santa Monica, Venice & Malibu

Head west for beach culture, ocean air, and the quintessential California coastline.

Time Activity Notes & Details
8:00 AM Breakfast at Gjusta (320 Sunset Ave, Venice) Artisan bakery/deli; grab a pastry and coffee before the beach crowds arrive.
9:00 AM Venice Beach Boardwalk & Muscle Beach People-watching paradise — street performers, skate park, and the famous canals nearby (walk the Venice Canal Historic District).
10:30 AM Santa Monica Pier & Third Street Promenade Ride the historic Pacific Wheel ferris wheel; stroll the pier and beach. Promenade is great for shopping.
12:30 PM Lunch at Cassia (1314 7th St, Santa Monica) — or Bay Cities Italian Deli Cassia: Southeast Asian–inspired, excellent for a sit-down meal. Bay Cities: legendary "Godmother" sandwich — cash-friendly, casual.
