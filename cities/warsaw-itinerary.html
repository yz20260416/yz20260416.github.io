Warsaw 3-Day Itinerary

Warsaw is a city of remarkable resilience and reinvention. Almost entirely rebuilt after the devastation of World War II, Poland's capital blends meticulously restored Old Town charm with striking socialist-era architecture, world-class museums, and a thriving modern food and cultural scene. This 3-day itinerary is designed to help you experience the best of Warsaw at a comfortable pace — from the historic heart of the Old Town and the Royal Route to the moving history of the Warsaw Uprising and the sobering memory of the former Jewish Ghetto, plus plenty of excellent Polish cuisine along the way. Each day balances major sights with time to wander, eat well, and soak up the local atmosphere.

Day 1 — Old Town & the Royal Route

Morning

  • 9:00 AM — Castle Square (Plac Zamkowy): Start at the heart of historic Warsaw. Admire the Sigismund's Column and the colorful facades of the reconstructed Old Town.
  • 9:30 AM — Royal Castle (Zamek Królewski): Tour the former residence of Polish kings, including the richly decorated Royal Apartments and the Great Assembly Hall. Allow about 1.5–2 hours. Book tickets online in advance during peak season.
  • 11:30 AM — Old Town Market Square (Rynek Starego Miasta): Stroll through the UNESCO-listed square with its pastel townhouses and the Warsaw Mermaid statue. Explore the narrow surrounding streets.

Afternoon

  • 12:30 PM — Lunch: Try U Barssa (traditional Polish, near the Market Square) for pierogi and żurek, or Zapiecek for casual, budget-friendly Polish dumplings.
  • 2:00 PM — Barbican & City Walls: Walk the surviving fragments of the medieval fortifications toward the New Town (Nowe Miasto).
  • 2:45 PM — Krakowskie Przedmieście: Follow the elegant Royal Route south past the Presidential Palace, churches, and university buildings toward Nowy Świat.
  • 4:00 PM — Łazienki Park (Lazienki Królewskie): Take a short bus or taxi ride to Warsaw's largest park. Visit the Palace on the Isle, the Chopin Monument (free outdoor Chopin concerts on summer Sundays), and the peacock-filled gardens.

Evening

  • 7:00 PM — Dinner: Dine at Stary Dom (classic Polish cuisine in a rustic setting) or Kieliszki na Próżnej for refined Polish small plates and wine.
  • 9:00 PM — Optional: Enjoy a drink on Nowy Świat or in the trendy Powiśle district along the river.

Day 2 — History & Jewish Heritage

Morning

  • 9:30 AM — POLIN Museum of the History of Polish Jews: One of Europe's finest museums, located in Muranów on the site of the former ghetto. The core exhibition traces 1,000 years of Jewish life in Poland. Allow 2.5–3 hours. Closed on Tuesdays.
  • 12:30 PM — Monument to the Ghetto Heroes & Umschlagplatz: Visit the powerful memorials just outside the museum, including the square from which Jews were deported to Treblinka.
