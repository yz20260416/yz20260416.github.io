Best Time to Visit Cairo

Cairo is Egypt's sprawling capital on the Nile, home to the Giza pyramids, medieval Islamic quarters, and the Egyptian Museum. Visit from October to April for mild, sunny days ideal for sightseeing, while summers are hot and hazy.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx — The last surviving ancient wonder, with three monumental pyramids and the Great Sphinx on the city's western edge.
  2. Egyptian Museum — Home to a vast collection of pharaonic antiquities, including royal mummies and treasures from Tutankhamun's tomb.
  3. Khan el-Khalili Bazaar — A labyrinthine medieval market selling spices, lanterns, jewelry, and souvenirs in the heart of Islamic Cairo.
  4. Saladin Citadel and Muhammad Ali Mosque — A hilltop fortress offering panoramic views and the striking alabaster domes of the 19th-century mosque.
  5. Coptic Cairo — A historic quarter with early Christian churches, including the Hanging Church, and the Ben Ezra Synagogue.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Egyptian Pound (EGP, E£)
  • Language: Arabic (English is widely understood in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: EET (UTC+2)
  • Visa: Most international visitors need a visa, available as an e-visa or on arrival at Cairo airport for many nationalities.

FAQ

Is Cairo safe for tourists?
Yes, major tourist areas are generally safe, though visitors should watch for petty crime and aggressive vendors and avoid unlicensed guides.
How do I get around Cairo?
Use ride-hailing apps like Uber and Careem, metered taxis, or the metro for cheap, efficient transport; traffic is heavy so plan extra time.
What should I wear in Cairo?
Modest, lightweight clothing is recommended, with shoulders and knees covered, especially when visiting mosques and religious sites.
