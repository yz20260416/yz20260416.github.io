Rio de Janeiro Travel Weather Guide

Rio de Janeiro's weather data reveal a tropical climate with warm temperatures year-round but sharply uneven rainfall. The standout months for travelers are July and August, when rain days drop to just 6.0 and 8.6 respectively and highs hover around 24–25°C — dry, comfortable conditions ideal for beach days and sightseeing. In contrast, December and January are the wettest, with 16.0 and 15.2 rain days and highs near 29–30°C, meaning frequent afternoon downpours and high humidity. Practically, this means planning outdoor activities for mornings during summer, and packing rain protection if visiting around the New Year.

Practical tips

  • Visit in July or August for the driest, most comfortable conditions — highs of 24–25°C and only 6–9 rain days per month.
  • Avoid December and January if you dislike rain; these months average 15–16 rain days and coincide with peak holiday crowds.
  • Even in wet months, rain often falls in short afternoon bursts — schedule beaches, hikes, and viewpoints for the morning.
  • Pack a compact umbrella and quick-dry clothing year-round; humidity stays high even in the cooler dry season.
  • Temperatures rarely drop below 17°C, so light layers suffice — but bring a jacket for cooler July evenings.
