Cairo 3-Day Itinerary

Cairo is a city of superlatives – home to the last surviving wonder of the ancient world, a medieval Islamic quarter that feels frozen in time, and a bustling modern metropolis that never sleeps. With three days, you can experience the iconic Pyramids of Giza, dive into the Egyptian Museum’s treasures, wander through historic bazaars, and savor the flavors of Egyptian cuisine. This itinerary balances the must-see highlights with authentic local experiences, ensuring you make the most of your time in this captivating capital. Below is a day-by-day plan with schedules, activities, and hand-picked restaurant recommendations.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day Time Activity Restaurant / Food Recommendation
Day 1
Pyramids & Ancient Wonders		 8:00 – 12:30 Pyramids of Giza & Sphinx – Arrive early to beat the heat and crowds. Explore the Great Pyramid (optional interior entry), the Queens' Pyramids, and the Sphinx. Consider a camel or horse ride for a different perspective (agree on price beforehand). Breakfast: Felfela (downtown) for ful medames and falafel; or grab a quick feteer meshaltet from a local bakery.
12:30 – 14:00 Lunch near Giza – Relax and refuel after the morning exploration. Andrea El Mariouteya (Giza) – garden setting, famous for grilled chicken and Egyptian mezze.
Alternative: Abou Shakra (multiple branches) for classic kebab and kofta.
14:30 – 17:00 Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) – The new mega-museum near the pyramids (check opening status; timed tickets recommended). Home to the complete Tutankhamun collection and thousands of artifacts. —
19:00 – 21:30 Dinner & evening stroll – Head to Zamalek island for a relaxed evening along the Nile. Sequoia (Zamalek) – Nile-side dining, excellent mezze and grilled fish.
Budget-friendly: Koshary Abou Tarek (downtown) – the ultimate Egyptian street food
