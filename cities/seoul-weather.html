Seoul Travel Weather Guide

Seoul's weather data reveals a sharply seasonal city where timing your visit matters enormously. The most comfortable months are April, May and October, when highs sit between 18°C and 22°C, lows stay mild, and rain days are relatively few — ideal for walking palaces, markets and riverside parks without heavy gear. By contrast, July and August are the toughest: highs near 30°C combine with roughly 18 rain days per month, meaning oppressive humidity and frequent downpours tied to the East Asian monsoon. January is the coldest month (highs of just 1.9°C, lows of -7°C), so winter travellers should pack serious insulation for sightseeing.

Practical tips

  • Best windows: April–May and October offer the most comfortable temperatures with manageable rain — book accommodation well ahead for these peak periods.
  • Avoid or prepare for July–August: with roughly 18 rain days per month and highs around 30°C, carry a compact umbrella, breathable clothing and plan indoor backups.
  • Winter insulation: January and December bring lows of -7°C to -5.5°C; pack thermal layers, gloves and a proper coat, as wind chill makes it feel colder.
  • Rain strategy: rain days climb sharply from May onward; a foldable umbrella and waterproof shoes are worthwhile from spring through autumn.
  • Layering pays off: spring and autumn swing widely between highs and lows (10°C+ daily range), so dress in layers you can add or remove.
