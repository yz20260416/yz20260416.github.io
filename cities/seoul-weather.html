Seoul Weather by Month

Seoul sits in the heart of the Korean Peninsula and experiences a humid continental climate with four sharply defined seasons. Summers are hot and rainy, winters are cold and dry, and the shoulder seasons — spring and autumn — are famously beautiful. Understanding the monthly weather patterns will help you pack correctly, choose the best time for outdoor sightseeing, and avoid the extremes of monsoon downpours or freezing temperatures. Below is a month-by-month breakdown of average temperatures and rainfall, followed by guidance on the best times to visit and practical travel tips.

Monthly Weather Overview

January -1 / 30 -9 / 16 ~20 Cold, dry, clear; occasional snow
February 3 / 37 -6 / 21 ~25 Still cold, gradually warming
March 10 / 50 0 / 32 ~45 Cool, breezy; early spring

May 23 / 73 12 / 54 ~100 Warm, pleasant; green and blooming

November 12 / 54 2 / 36 ~45 Cool, clear; late autumn color fades
December 4 / 39 -6 / 21 ~20 Cold, dry; festive lights in the city

Note: Figures are long-term averages and vary year to year. July and August can see intense localized downpours, while winter precipitation often falls as light snow.

Seasonal Highlights

  • Spring (March–May): Mild and increasingly sunny. Cherry blossoms peak in early-to-mid April, followed by azaleas and lush greenery in May. Crowds rise around blossom season.
  • Summer (June–August): Hot, humid, and rainy, with the jangma monsoon dumping the bulk of the year's rain in July. Pack breathable clothing and a sturdy umbrella.
  • Autumn (September–November): Widely regarded as the finest season — clear skies, comfortable temperatures, and spectacular foliage in October. Ideal for hiking and palace visits.
  • Winter (December–February): Cold and dry with bright, crisp days. Snow is possible but usually light; dress in layers and enjoy fewer crowds and festive illuminations.

Best Months to Visit

The best overall months are April, May, September, and October, when temperatures are comfortable (roughly 15–25°C / 59–77°F), rainfall is moderate, and outdoor sightseeing is at its best. October stands out for clear skies and autumn color, while April is unbeatable for cherry blossoms. If you want to avoid both heat and cold, aim for these shoulder seasons. Budget-conscious travelers may find November and March appealing for lower prices and thinner crowds, though weather is cooler.

Practical Tips

  • Pack for the season: Lightweight, breathable layers in summer; a warm coat, hat, and gloves in winter; a versatile jacket and layers in spring and autumn.
  • Carry an umbrella year-round: Summer monsoons bring sudden heavy rain, and spring/autumn showers are common.
  • Beat the summer heat: Schedule outdoor activities for early morning or evening, stay hydrated, and use air-conditioned cafés and museums midday.
  • Winter comfort: Korean winters are dry and windy — moisturizer and lip balm help, and heated floors (ondol) make indoor stays cozy.
  • Check air quality: Spring occasionally brings yellow dust (hwangsa) and fine dust; check forecasts and consider a mask on poor-air days.
  • Book ahead in peak seasons: Cherry blossom season (April) and autumn foliage (October) draw domestic and international crowds — reserve hotels and popular attractions early.
  • Layer for temperature swings: Day–night temperature differences can be large in spring and autumn, so dress in removable layers.
