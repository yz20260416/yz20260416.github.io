Beijing Travel Cost Guide

Beijing is a sprawling metropolis where ancient imperial grandeur meets modern ambition. As one of the world’s most influential capitals, it offers a wide spectrum of travel experiences — from backpacker hostels in hutong alleys to five-star luxury in Wangfujing. Understanding the real costs is essential for planning a trip that matches your budget and style. This guide breaks down estimated daily expenses for three common travel styles: budget, mid-range, and luxury. All prices are in Chinese Yuan (CNY), with approximate USD equivalents (1 USD ≈ 7.2 CNY) for reference. Use these figures as a flexible framework — Beijing can be remarkably affordable if you eat local and use public transport, or as lavish as you desire.
