Best Time to Visit Bali

Bali is an Indonesian island province of temples, rice terraces, surf beaches and volcanic peaks, drawing travellers for both culture and coastline. Visit in the dry season from April to October for sunshine and clear diving, or in the greener wet months for lower prices and lush landscapes.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Tanah Lot Temple — A sea temple perched on a tidal rock that is spectacular at sunset.
  2. Ubud Monkey Forest — A sacred forest sanctuary home to hundreds of long-tailed macaques and mossy temples.
  3. Mount Batur — An active volcano whose pre-dawn summit hike rewards with sweeping sunrise views.
  4. Tegallalang Rice Terraces — Steep emerald rice paddies near Ubud sculpted by traditional subak irrigation.
  5. Uluwatu Temple — A clifftop temple above the surf with nightly kecak fire dances.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR, Rp)
  • Language: Indonesian (English is widely spoken in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: WITA (UTC+8)
  • Visa: Most international visitors can enter visa-free for 30 days or buy a visa on arrival, but check your nationality's current rules before travel.

FAQ

What is the best time to visit Bali?
The dry season from April to October offers the sunniest weather, while May, June and September balance good conditions with fewer crowds.
How do I get around Bali?
Ride-hailing apps, private drivers and scooter rentals are the most practical options, since public transport is limited and traffic can be heavy.
Is Bali safe for travellers?
Bali is generally safe, but watch for strong surf and rip currents, respect temple dress codes, and be cautious of scooter accidents and petty theft.
