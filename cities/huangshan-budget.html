Huangshan Travel Budget Guide: Costs for Backpackers, Mid-Range, and Luxury Travelers

Huangshan (Yellow Mountain) in Anhui Province is one of China's most celebrated natural attractions, famous for its granite peaks, sea-of-clouds vistas, and twisted pine trees. While the mountain itself is the main draw, most travelers base themselves in the nearby towns of Tangkou (at the base) or Tunxi (Huangshan City, about an hour away). Costs vary widely depending on whether you sleep on the mountain, how you get there, and your comfort level. Below is a complete budget breakdown across three travel styles, with all figures in Chinese yuan (CNY) unless noted, based on typical 2024–2025 prices. A typical visit is 2–3 days.

Summary Budget Table (Per Person, 2–3 Day Trip)

Category Backpacker Mid-Range Luxury
Accommodation (per night) 60–150 CNY (hostel dorm or budget guesthouse) 300–600 CNY (3–4 star hotel or private guesthouse room) 900–2,500+ CNY (mountain-top luxury hotel or upscale resort)
Food (per day) 50–100 CNY (street food, convenience stores, simple noodle shops) 150–300 CNY (sit-down local restaurants, hotel breakfast included) 400–800+ CNY (hotel restaurants, fine dining, imported goods)
Transport (arrival + local) 100–200 CNY (public bus, shared shuttle, train hard seat) 250–500 CNY (high-speed rail, taxi, scenic area shuttle) 600–1,500+ CNY (private car/driver, high-speed rail first class, transfers)
Activities (tickets, cable cars) 230–350 CNY (park entry + one cable car, self-guided hiking) 350–500 CNY (park entry + both cable cars, guided day hike) 500–1,200+ CNY (park entry, cable cars, private guide, hot springs)
Estimated total (2 nights, 3 days) ~500–1,000 CNY ~1,500–3,000 CNY ~4,000–9,000+ CNY

Detailed Cost Breakdown

Accommodation

Type Location Typical Price (per night) Notes
Hostel dorm bed Tangkou / Tunxi 60–120 CNY Cheapest option; book ahead in peak season
Budget guesthouse (double) Tangkou 150–250 CNY Simple rooms near the park entrance
Mid-range hotel (double) Tangkou / Tunxi 300–600 CNY Includes breakfast; Tunxi has more choice
Mountain-top hotel (standard) On Huangshan 800–1,500 CNY Essential for sunrise viewing; book months ahead
Luxury mountain-top hotel On Huangshan 1,500–3,000+ CNY Premium views, limited availability
Upscale resort (Tunxi) Huangshan City 800–2,000 CNY Spa, hot springs nearby

Food

Style Daily Cost What to Expect
Backpacker 50–100 CNY Noodle shops, baozi breakfasts, convenience-store snacks, instant noodles
Mid-Range 150–300 CNY Local Anhui cuisine (stinky mandarin fish, bamboo shoots), hotel breakfast
Luxury 400–800+ CNY Hotel restaurants, multi-course meals, imported drinks

Note: Food on the mountain is expensive (a simple meal can cost 60–120 CNY) because everything is carried up by porters. Many travelers bring snacks and water from the base.
