Lijiang Travel Weather Guide

Lijiang's real 2021–2025 averages show a high-altitude climate with mild, sunny winters and a pronounced summer monsoon. April is arguably the sweet spot: highs near 22°C, lows around 7°C, and only about six rain days mean comfortable sightseeing with low disruption. September is the worst month for rain, with roughly 25 wet days—practically, expect daily showers, slippery old-town lanes, and backup indoor plans. The rainy season (May–October) brings 15–25 rain days per month, so pack waterproof layers and schedule outdoor excursions for mornings. Winter days are sunny but cold after dark, with lows near or below freezing in January and February.

Practical tips

  • Pack layers year-round: highs reach 22–24°C in spring and summer, but lows drop near or below freezing in December–February.
  • Visit April or November for the best combination of mild temperatures and low rain days; avoid September if you dislike daily showers.
  • During May–October, carry a waterproof jacket and plan outdoor sights for mornings before afternoon rain.
  • Use high-factor sunscreen and sunglasses even in winter—Lijiang's altitude makes sunny days intense.
  • Bring sturdy, water-resistant footwear for the old town's stone lanes, especially during the wet months.
