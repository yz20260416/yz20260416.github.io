Top Attractions in Las Vegas

Las Vegas is far more than casinos and neon—it is a city of immersive art, record-breaking thrill rides, and quirky off-Strip landmarks. From interactive alternate-reality supermarkets to a 829-foot controlled freefall, the city rewards curious travelers who venture beyond the slots. This guide highlights ten attractions most likely to appeal to an international visitor, blending iconic sights with one-of-a-kind experiences.

  1. Omega Mart — An interactive art installation by Meow Wolf inside the AREA15 complex, where visitors explore a surreal supermarket that opens into hidden rooms and a sprawling narrative. It features contributions from over 300 artists and designers and functions as an alternate reality game.
  2. SkyJump Las Vegas — A commercial decelerator at The Strat that holds the Guinness World Record for the highest commercial decelerator descent at 829 feet. It offers a controlled, guided plunge from the tower's edge.
  3. X-Scream — A ride atop The Strat SkyPod at approximately 866 feet, making it the world's third-highest amusement ride. Its name is a play on the word "extreme," and it delivers a dramatic teeter over the Strip.
  4. FlyOver in Las Vegas — A flying theater attraction on the Las Vegas Strip next to the Hard Rock Cafe, opened in September 2021. It is the third FlyOver location operated by Pursuit and simulates flight over sweeping landscapes.
  5. Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas Sign — The city's most photographed landmark, featured among Las Vegas's public artworks. A quintessential photo stop that captures the classic mid-century spirit of the Strip.
  6. Big Edge by Nancy Rubins — A large-scale public sculpture included in Las Vegas's public art collection, known for its dramatic assemblage of boat forms. A striking free landmark for art lovers exploring the city.
  7. Bliss Dance by Marco Cochrane — A monumental public artwork listed among Las Vegas's installations, celebrated for its expressive depiction of the human figure. An easy, free cultural stop during a Strip stroll.
  8. Paintbrush Gateway — A playful public art installation in the Las Vegas public art roster, serving as a colorful photo-friendly landmark. It reflects the city's growing commitment to accessible street art.
  9. Double Down Saloon — A dive bar and live music venue founded by P Moss in 1992, known for its "Shut Up and Drink" motto, graffiti-covered walls, and specialties like "Ass Juice" and the Bacon Martini. It is an off-Strip landmark of the city's independent nightlife scene.
  10. Universal Horror Unleashed — A self-guiding, walk-through haunted attraction spanning 110,000 square feet within the AREA15 entertainment complex. Announced in January 2023, it brings Universal's horror-themed experience to Las Vegas.

Practical tips

  • Book Omega Mart and Universal Horror Unleashed tickets online in advance, especially on weekends, and allow at least two hours for Omega Mart's narrative exploration.
  • Visit The Strat's SkyJump and X-Scream together to save time; check weather conditions and ride operating hours before buying tickets.
  • Arrive early at the Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas Sign for shorter photo lines—midweek mornings are quietest.
  • Cluster AREA15 attractions (Omega Mart and Universal Horror Unleashed) in a single visit since they share the same complex.
  • Explore off-Strip spots like Double Down Saloon in the evening for live music; note that some venues are 21+ only.
