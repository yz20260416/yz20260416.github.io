Fiji Travel Cost Guide

Fiji is a South Pacific archipelago of more than 330 islands, famous for its turquoise lagoons, coral reefs, and warm Bula hospitality. While it has a reputation as a luxury honeymoon destination, Fiji can be surprisingly affordable if you plan wisely. Costs vary dramatically depending on whether you stay on the main island of Viti Levu, hop between remote islands, or choose between resorts, guesthouses, and homestays. This guide breaks down typical daily expenses across three travel styles — budget, mid-range, and luxury — so you can plan a trip that fits your wallet.

Detailed Cost Notes by Category

Accommodation

  • Budget: Hostels on Viti Levu and the Yasawas start around FJD 30–50 per dorm bed. Village homestays (a culturally rich option) run roughly FJD 60–90 including meals.
  • Mid-Range: Three- to four-star hotels in Nadi, Denarau, or Coral Coast range from FJD 150–350 per night. Boutique island lodges sit at the higher end.
  • Luxury: Private-island resorts (e.g., in the Mamanucas) start around FJD 600 and climb steeply; overwater villas and exclusive lodges can exceed FJD 2,500 per night. Note that many luxury resorts are all-inclusive, bundling meals and activities.
  • Money-saver tip: Prices spike during Australian and New Zealand school holidays (December–January, June–July) and around Easter. Book early or travel in shoulder seasons (May, October–November).

Food

  • Local eateries, market stalls, and lovo (earth-oven) feasts are inexpensive — a hearty meal can cost FJD 10–20.
  • Resort restaurants are pricey: expect FJD 40–80 for a main course. If staying at a resort, consider a meal plan.
  • Self-catering (markets in Nadi, Suva, and Lautoka) can cut food costs to FJD 20–30 per day.
  • Alcohol adds significantly to costs; resort cocktails run FJD 15–30 each.
