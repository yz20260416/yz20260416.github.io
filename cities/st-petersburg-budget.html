St. Petersburg Travel Cost Guide

St. Petersburg is one of Europe's most magnificent cities — a showcase of imperial palaces, world-class museums, and romantic canals — yet it remains surprisingly affordable compared to Western European capitals. Whether you're a backpacker counting every ruble, a mid-range traveler seeking comfort and culture, or a luxury visitor indulging in five-star experiences, this guide breaks down realistic daily and weekly costs for accommodation, food, transport, and activities. Prices are given in Russian rubles (RUB) with approximate US dollar equivalents (at roughly 90 RUB = 1 USD; exchange rates fluctuate, so check current rates before you go).

Prices are highest during the White Nights (late May–early July) and major holidays; winter (November–March, excluding New Year) offers the best value.

Accommodation in Detail

  • Hostels: Dorm beds range from 700–1,500 RUB ($8–$17) per night. Private rooms in hostels start around 2,500 RUB ($28). The best-value hostels cluster around Nevsky Prospekt and the city center.
  • Budget hotels & guesthouses: Simple private rooms with shared or private bathrooms run 3,000–5,000 RUB ($33–$56) per night.
  • Mid-range hotels (3–4 star): Expect 5,000–10,000 RUB ($56–$111) per night for a comfortable double in the center. Book well ahead for White Nights.
