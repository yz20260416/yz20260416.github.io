Melbourne's climate rewards flexible travellers. The data shows February as the standout month — warm highs of 25.7°C paired with just 5.2 rain days, making it the driest and most reliable window for outdoor plans, beaches and the Australian Open season. July is the toughest month: highs of only 13.2°C, lows near 6.4°C and 12.6 rain days mean you should pack layers, waterproofs and plan around indoor attractions. Spring and autumn sit in between, but note that October (14.8 rain days) and September (12.0) are surprisingly wet despite pleasant temperatures — carry an umbrella year-round, because Melbourne's weather can turn within an hour.

Practical tips

  • Pack for four seasons in one day: even in summer, lows drop to 12–15°C, so bring layers you can add and remove.
  • Always carry a compact umbrella or rain shell: rain days exceed 8 in every month except February, and October peaks at 14.8.
  • Target February for the best odds of dry, warm weather (5.2 rain days); avoid July if you dislike cold and wet conditions.
  • Book early for December–February: summer crowds are high, especially around major events and holidays.
  • Use rainy months (May–October) for indoor attractions — museums, galleries, laneway cafés and shopping — and save coastal trips for summer.
