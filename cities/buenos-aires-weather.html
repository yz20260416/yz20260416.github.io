Buenos Aires Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Travel Planning

Buenos Aires has a humid subtropical climate, and the data shows a clear split between a hot, wet summer and a mild, drier winter. November stands out as the wettest month (9.2 rain days), while April and October offer the best balance of comfortable temperatures (highs of 21–22°C) and fewer wet days. Practically, this means spring and autumn are the sweet spots for sightseeing, while January–February bring heat near 29°C highs plus frequent storms — plan indoor activities or early mornings then. June–August are coolest (highs around 14–16°C) but relatively dry, making winter a good bet for budget travelers who don't mind a jacket.

Practical tips

  • Best months for weather: April and October combine comfortable highs (21–22°C) with moderate rain — ideal for walking tours and outdoor sightseeing.
  • Rainiest months: March (10.2 days) and November (9.2 days) — pack a compact umbrella or waterproof jacket and keep indoor backup plans.
  • Hottest period: January–February (highs near 28–29°C). Schedule outdoor activities for mornings, use sunscreen, and stay hydrated.
  • Coolest, driest stretch: June–August (highs 14–16°C, around 5 rain days). Bring warm layers for evenings; crowds and prices are at their lowest.
  • General packing: Layers work year-round in Buenos Aires — even hot summer days can turn stormy, and winter mornings are chilly.
