Zanzibar Travel Cost Guide

Zanzibar — the legendary Spice Island off the coast of Tanzania — offers turquoise waters, powder-white beaches, historic Stone Town, and a rich cultural tapestry. But how much does a trip here actually cost? Whether you're a backpacker chasing sunsets on a shoestring, a mid-range traveler seeking comfort and local flavor, or a luxury seeker indulging in private villas and fine dining, this guide breaks down real-world costs for accommodation, food, transport, and activities across all three travel styles. All prices are in US dollars (USD) and reflect 2024–2025 averages, with Tanzanian shillings (TZS) noted where helpful (roughly 2,500–2,600 TZS = 1 USD).
