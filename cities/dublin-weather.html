Dublin Travel Weather Guide

Dublin's weather is famously mild and changeable, and the data confirms it: no month averages above 20°C, and every single month sees at least 11 rain days. The best window for travelers is May through June, when highs climb to 15–18°C with lows of 9–11°C, making outdoor sightseeing, coastal walks, and day trips genuinely comfortable. The toughest months are October (16.6 rain days — the wettest of the year) and the dark, damp stretch of December through February, when highs hover around 8–10°C and rain is frequent. Practically, this means packing waterproof layers year-round and favoring indoor attractions like museums and pubs on wet days, while keeping summer plans flexible enough to seize the drier breaks between showers.

Practical tips

  • Expect rain in every month — no period averages fewer than 11 rain days, so a waterproof jacket and shoes are essential year-round.
  • Pack layers rather than heavy coats: highs range only from about 8°C in January to 19.5°C in July, and conditions change quickly.
  • Target May–June for the best balance of mild temperatures (15–18°C) and manageable crowds before the July–August peak.
  • Avoid October if rain bothers you — at 16.6 rain days it is the wettest month; if visiting then, build in indoor backups.
  • Carry a small umbrella or hooded raincoat daily, and plan outdoor activities around shorter daylight hours from November to January.
