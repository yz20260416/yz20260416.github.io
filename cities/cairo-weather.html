Cairo Travel Weather Guide

Cairo's weather data tells a clear story: the sweet spot for visitors is November through March, when highs sit comfortably between 20–27°C and lows hover around 10–16°C. January (20.4°C high / 10.1°C low) and December (21.9°C / 12.0°C) are especially pleasant for sightseeing. The worst time is July–August, when highs near 39°C and lows stay around 24°C — meaning even nights offer little relief. Practically, that means planning outdoor attractions like the Pyramids and Islamic Cairo for early morning or late afternoon in summer, and packing layers for cool winter evenings. Rain is negligible year-round, so an umbrella is rarely needed.

Practical tips

  • Schedule outdoor sights such as the Pyramids and Citadel for early morning or late afternoon, especially May–September when highs exceed 34°C.
  • Pack light, breathable layers for summer and a warm jacket for winter evenings, when lows can drop to around 10°C.
  • Rain is rare all year (at most ~1.6 rain days per month), so an umbrella is optional — prioritize sun protection instead.
  • Carry water and plan air-conditioned breaks during midday in June–August, when highs approach 39°C.
  • For the most comfortable sightseeing, target November–March and book accommodation early, as these months draw the biggest crowds.
