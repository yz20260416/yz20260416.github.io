Nairobi Travel Weather Guide

Nairobi enjoys a mild, spring-like climate year-round, but rainfall patterns vary dramatically. The data shows July is the driest and most reliable month (just 3.8 rain days, high 23.1°C), making it ideal for safaris and outdoor sightseeing. In sharp contrast, April is the wettest (25.2 rain days, high 25.2°C), when long rains bring near-daily downpours and muddy parks. February is the warmest and driest combination (27.9°C high, 5.8 rain days). Practically, plan major outdoor activities for January–March or June–September, and expect afternoon showers, slippery trails, and reduced visibility if you visit in April, November, or December.

Practical tips

  • Target June–September for the driest, most dependable weather; July is the standout month.
  • Avoid April if possible — with 25 rain days, outdoor plans and park roads are frequently disrupted.
  • Even in dry months, mornings and evenings are cool (lows 12–15°C), so bring a light jacket or fleece.
  • In rainy months (April, May, November, December), schedule activities for mornings and keep afternoons flexible.
  • Pack a compact rain jacket and sturdy shoes year-round — short showers can occur even in drier periods.
