Best Time to Visit Hangzhou

Hangzhou is the capital of Zhejiang province, set where the Grand Canal meets Hangzhou Bay and home to the celebrated West Lake. It is one of China's Eight Ancient Capitals, having served as the seat of the Wuyue Kingdom and later the Southern Song dynasty. Visit in spring or autumn for mild weather and the lake at its best.

Local Character

Hangzhou's identity is bound to West Lake and to the engineers and poets who shaped it. In the Tang dynasty, the poet Bai Juyi, appointed governor, found the old dyke collapsed and farmland parched; he ordered a stronger, taller dyke with a dam to control water flow, easing the drought for local farmers, then planted willows along it and visited almost daily for leisure. Visitors still walk a causeway named for him, and knowing this explains why the lake's embankments are both irrigation works and promenades. The city also gave its name to the Liangzhu culture, a jade-carving neolithic society whose type site lies just northwest of the present city and whose ruins are a UNESCO World Heritage Site — worth a detour for travelers interested in China's deep prehistory. Hangzhou's written history begins with Yuhang, the first of its neighborhoods to appear in records and a name that likely preserves an old Baiyue word, a reminder that the city predates its imperial founding as Qiantang county in 222 BC.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. West Lake — The city's defining landscape, a UNESCO World Heritage Cultural Landscape ringed by causeways, temples, and hills.
  2. Grand Canal (Hangzhou section) — The southern terminus of the canal that reaches Beijing, a UNESCO World Heritage Site best explored on foot or by boat.
  3. Archaeological Ruins of Liangzhu City — A UNESCO World Heritage Site northwest of the city showing the neolithic jade-carving Liangzhu culture.
  4. Bai Juyi Causeway — The willow-lined dyke built by the Tang poet-governor Bai Juyi to control West Lake's water and ease local drought.
  5. Wulin area — The historic district known as Wulin under the Han dynasty, among the earliest recorded parts of the present city.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit Hangzhou?
April, May, and October offer the mildest weather and the lake at its best, though crowds are high then.
How many UNESCO World Heritage Sites does Hangzhou have?
Three: the West Lake Cultural Landscape, the Grand Canal, and the Archaeological Ruins of Liangzhu City.
What is the historical significance of West Lake's dykes?
The Tang governor and poet Bai Juyi built a stronger dyke and dam to control lake water for nearby farmland, easing drought and creating a promenade still enjoyed today.
