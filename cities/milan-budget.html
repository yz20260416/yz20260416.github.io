Milan Travel Budget Guide: Backpacker, Mid-Range & Luxury Costs

Milan is one of Europe's most expensive cities, but costs vary dramatically depending on how you travel. This guide breaks down realistic daily and per-item expenses for three budget styles — backpacker (hostels, street food, public transport), mid-range (3-star hotels, trattorias, occasional taxis), and luxury (4–5 star hotels, fine dining, private transfers). Prices are in euros and reflect typical 2024–2025 rates; peak periods such as Milan Fashion Week, Salone del Mobile, and the Christmas season can push accommodation costs significantly higher.

Practical Tips

  • Book ahead during trade fairs: Salone del Mobile (April) and Fashion Week (Feb/Sep) can double or triple hotel prices; reserve months in advance.
  • Use the ATM transit network: Tickets cover metro, trams, and buses; buy passes at machines or via contactless tap-and-go.
  • Eat like a local: Standing at the bar, pizza al taglio, and aperitivo buffets keep food costs low without sacrificing quality.
  • Reserve Last Supper tickets early: Slots sell out weeks ahead; official booking is essential.
  • Stay slightly outside the center: Neighborhoods like Lambrate, Porta Romana, and Isola offer lower rates with good metro access.
  • Watch for coperto and servizio: Cover charges (€2–5) and service fees are common at sit-down restaurants.
  • Free sights: Duomo exterior, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, Parco Sempione, and the Navigli canals cost nothing to explore.
  • Carry some cash: Small cafés and markets may prefer it, though cards are widely accepted.
