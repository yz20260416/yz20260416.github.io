Miami Weather by Month

Miami enjoys a tropical monsoon climate, which means warm temperatures year-round, a distinct wet season from May through October, and a drier, more comfortable season from November through April. Average highs hover between the upper 70s°F (25–26°C) in winter and the low 90s°F (32–33°C) in summer, while humidity can make summer feel considerably hotter. Rainfall is highly seasonal: the wet months can bring intense but short afternoon thunderstorms, while winter is mostly sunny and dry. The best time to visit for sightseeing and beach days is generally December through April, when temperatures are pleasant, rain is scarce, and humidity is lower—though this is also peak tourist season, so expect higher prices and crowds. Summer offers lower hotel rates and warm ocean water but comes with heat, humidity, and the heart of hurricane season (June–November, peaking August–October).

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall (weather + comfort): December through April — dry, sunny, and mild, ideal for beaches, sightseeing, and outdoor dining.
  • Best value: June through September — lower hotel rates and warm water, but expect heat, humidity, daily thunderstorms, and hurricane-season risk.
  • Shoulder sweet spot: Late November and early December offer pleasant weather before peak-season prices and crowds arrive.
  • Busiest and priciest: Late December through March (holidays, winter escapes, spring break).

Practical Tips

  • Pack for humidity: Lightweight, breathable clothing and sunscreen year-round; a light sweater or jacket for cooler winter evenings.
  • Plan around summer storms: In the wet season, schedule outdoor activities for mornings, as thunderstorms typically build in the afternoon.
  • Hurricane season awareness: June–November (peak August–October). If traveling then, buy travel insurance, monitor forecasts, and keep flexible plans.
  • Book early in peak season: Reserve hotels and popular restaurants well in advance for December–April, especially during holidays and major events.
  • Stay hydrated and seek shade: Summer heat indices can exceed 100°F (38°C); take breaks and use AC during midday.
  • Check ocean conditions: Rip currents and jellyfish can occur seasonally; heed beach flags and lifeguard warnings.
  • Rain gear: A compact umbrella or rain jacket is handy May–October for sudden downpours.
