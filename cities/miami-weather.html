Miami's subtropical climate divides sharply into a warm, dry winter and a hot, humid, storm-prone summer. Based on 2021–2025 averages, the best months to visit are January through April, when highs sit at a comfortable 24–28°C and rain days hover around 10–12 per month — ideal for beaches, sightseeing, and outdoor dining. The worst weather arrives from June through September, when rain days climb to 20–24 per month, mostly as intense afternoon thunderstorms, alongside highs of 30–32°C and oppressive humidity. Practically, that means winter visitors should pack light layers for cooler evenings, while summer travelers should plan outdoor activities for mornings and keep afternoons flexible.

Practical tips

  • Time your visit for winter or spring: January–April offers the most reliable beach and sightseeing weather, with highs of 24–28°C and fewer rain interruptions.
  • Summer means afternoon storms: From June to September, expect rain on roughly 20–24 days per month — schedule outdoor plans for the morning and keep indoor backups.
  • Note that "rain days" are high year-round: Even dry-season months average about 10 rain days, so a compact umbrella or rain jacket is worth packing in any month.
  • Pack for humidity in summer: Lightweight, breathable clothing and sun protection are essential when highs reach 30–32°C with lows near 25°C.
  • Watch hurricane season: June–November overlaps with storm season, peaking August–October; consider travel insurance and monitor forecasts if visiting then.
