Guilin, the gateway to the Li River karst landscape and the terraced rice fields of Longji, suits a wide range of budgets. Costs below are per person per day in Chinese yuan (CNY), with approximate USD equivalents (1 USD ≈ 7.2 CNY). They cover accommodation, food, local transport, and activities, and assume double occupancy for mid-range and luxury stays. Prices are typical for Guilin city and nearby Yangshuo; peak seasons (May–October, Chinese public holidays) can push costs 30–50% higher.

Category Backpacker (CNY/day) Mid-Range (CNY/day) Luxury (CNY/day)
Accommodation 60–120 (hostel dorm or basic guesthouse) 250–500 (3–4 star hotel or boutique inn) 900–2,500+ (5-star riverside resort or luxury boutique)
Food 50–90 (street noodles, rice noodles, local eateries) 120–250 (mid-range restaurants, mix of local and Western) 300–800+ (hotel dining, fine dining, imported drinks)
Local transport 15–40 (public bus, shared minivan, bicycle rental) 50–120 (taxis, DiDi, occasional private driver) 150–400 (private car with driver, airport transfers)
Activities 50–150 (hiking, free karst viewpoints, budget Li River cruise ~215) 150–350 (Li River cruise, Longji terraces entry ~80, bike tours) 400–1,000+ (private cruises, guided tours, hot air balloon, spa)
Daily total 175–400 (≈ US$25–55) 570–1,220 (≈ US$80–170) 1,750–4,700+ (≈ US$245–650+)

Key Cost Details

  • Li River cruise (Guilin–Yangshuo): roughly 215–450 CNY depending on boat class; budget travelers often take the bus to Yangshuo (~30 CNY) and do shorter bamboo raft sections instead.
  • Longji Rice Terraces: entry around 80 CNY plus transport (shared van ~50–60 CNY each way); overnight guesthouse stays are 100–200 CNY.
  • Reed Flute Cave & Elephant Trunk Hill: ~90 CNY and ~55 CNY respectively; many travelers pick one.
  • Airport transfer: airport shuttle bus ~20 CNY; taxi 100–120 CNY; private car 150–250 CNY.
  • Yangshuo activities: bamboo rafting 150–250 CNY, cycling rental 20–50 CNY/day, Impression Sanjie Liu show 200–680 CNY.

Practical Tips

  • Book accommodation and Li River cruises in advance during May–October and national holidays; prices and availability worsen sharply.
  • Eat where locals eat—Guilin rice noodles (桂林米粉) cost 6–12 CNY and are the best budget meal in the region.
  • Use DiDi (ride-hailing) in Guilin city; it is cheaper and easier than hailing taxis, especially with limited Chinese.
  • Carry cash for smaller towns and terraced villages; mobile payment (WeChat/Alipay) is dominant but foreign cards can be tricky.
  • Skip peak weekends if possible; mid-week rates in Yangshuo drop noticeably.
  • Budget travelers should base in Yangshuo rather than Guilin city for cheaper stays and easier access to karst scenery.
