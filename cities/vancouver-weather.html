Best Time to Visit Vancouver

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are July and August. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

Vancouver's weather data tells a clear story: July and August are the standout months, with highs near 23°C and only 2.6–5.8 rain days per month, meaning you can plan outdoor activities—hiking, beaches, patio dining—with confidence and pack light. At the other extreme, November through January brings highs of just 6–8°C and 17–21 rain days per month, so expect near-daily drizzle, short daylight, and the need for waterproof layers and indoor backup plans. Spring and fall sit in between: mild but frequently wet. The practical takeaway is to book summer for sightseeing and accept that winter visits demand rain gear and flexibility.

Practical tips

  • Pack a waterproof jacket and shoes year-round—rain days exceed 9 in nine of twelve months, and even July can see occasional showers.
  • Target July or August for the driest, warmest conditions (2.6–5.8 rain days, highs near 23°C), but book lodging early since crowds peak.
  • If visiting November–January, plan around indoor attractions and keep outdoor plans flexible; expect rain on roughly 17–21 days per month.
  • Use layers: summer lows hover around 15°C while winter highs sit near 6°C, so a light mid-layer plus shell works across seasons.
  • Shoulder months like May, June, and September offer mild temperatures with moderate crowds—good value if you don't mind some rain.
