Madrid Travel Weather Guide

Madrid's climate rewards careful timing. The data shows a stark split: July and August are punishing, with average highs near 35°C and almost no rain (July averages just 0.4 rain days), meaning midday sightseeing becomes genuinely uncomfortable and hydration is essential. By contrast, May and October are the sweet spots — May offers 24.6°C highs with only 6.2 rain days, while October brings 22.4°C highs with 8.8 rain days. March stands out as the wettest month (11.4 rain days), so pack a rain jacket if visiting then. Winter is cold but dry, with January lows dipping to 1.4°C — bring layers for crisp, sunny days.

Practical tips

  • Avoid midday heat in summer: July and August highs near 35°C make noon sightseeing draining — shift outdoor plans to early morning and evening.
  • Pack rain gear for spring and autumn: March (11.4 rain days) and October (8.8) are the wettest months; a compact umbrella and waterproof shoes pay off.
  • Layer for winter: January lows average 1.4°C, but days are often sunny and dry — dress in removable layers for cold mornings and mild afternoons.
  • Target May or September–October for balance: these months pair comfortable temperatures (22–25°C highs) with moderate rain and lively city life.
  • Book ahead in peak season: May–July draw the largest crowds, so reserve major attractions and restaurants in advance.
