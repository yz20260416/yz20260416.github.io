Cairo Top 10 Attractions

Cairo is a city where ancient wonders meet vibrant modern life. From the last remaining wonder of the ancient world to bustling bazaars and world-class museums, the Egyptian capital offers an unforgettable journey through 5,000 years of history.

Below you’ll find the 10 must-see attractions, with honest descriptions, current ticket prices (as of 2025, in Egyptian pounds and approximate USD), and practical tips to make the most of your visit. Prices for foreign visitors are higher than for locals — we’ve listed the standard foreign adult ticket unless noted.

# Attraction Description Ticket price (foreign adult) Visiting tip
1 Pyramids of Giza & Sphinx The iconic trio of Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure, plus the enigmatic Sphinx. A bucket-list experience — you can even climb inside the Great Pyramid (separate ticket). EGP 540 (~$11) site entry; Great Pyramid interior +EGP 900 (~$18) Go at 8 AM opening to beat crowds and heat. Bring water, hat, and comfortable shoes. Ignore aggressive vendors — a firm "no, thank you" works.
