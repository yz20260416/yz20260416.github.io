Miami 3-Day Itinerary — Travel Guide

Miami is a vibrant melting pot of cultures, where Art Deco architecture, world-class beaches, Cuban coffee, and Latin rhythms collide with contemporary art and celebrity-chef dining. This carefully balanced 3-day itinerary is designed for first-time visitors who want to experience the best of Miami — from the sun-soaked sands of South Beach and the street art of Wynwood to the tranquil beauty of the Everglades and the historic charm of Little Havana. Each day blends iconic sights with authentic local food, and the schedule is structured to minimize traffic and maximize your time. Whether you're traveling as a couple, with friends, or solo, this plan gives you a practical, memorable, and delicious introduction to the Magic City.

Day 1 — South Beach & Art Deco

Time Activity Details & Tips
8:00 AM Breakfast at Puerto Sagua Classic Cuban diner on Collins Ave. Try the café con leche and a tostada. Arrive early to beat the crowd.
9:30 AM South Beach & Ocean Drive stroll Walk the beach, rent a lounge chair, or swim. Then walk Ocean Drive to admire the pastel Art Deco facades.
11:30 AM Art Deco Historic District walking tour Self-guided or join the Miami Design Preservation League tour (10:30 AM daily). Learn about 1920s–40s architecture.
1:00 PM Lunch at La Sandwicherie Famous French-style sandwiches and fresh juices. Casual, affordable, and a local favorite on 14th St.
2:30 PM Lincoln Road Mall & Española Way Pedestrian shopping street with cafés and galleries. Española Way offers a charming European-style cobblestone stroll.
4:30 PM Beach relaxation or hotel pool Recharge before dinner — Miami evenings are long and lively.
6:30 PM Sunset cocktails at The Broken Shaker Award-winning craft cocktail bar at Freehand Miami. Tropical garden setting; arrive early for a seat.
8:00 PM Dinner at Joe's Stone Crab Miami Beach institution (open Oct–May for stone crab). Reserve ahead. Alternatives: Yardbird (Southern) or Carbone (Italian).
10:00 PM Optional: Ocean Drive nightlife Enjoy people-watching or a nightcap at a beachfront bar. Note: many venues have minimum spends.

Day 2 — Wynwood, Little Havana & Downtown

Time Activity Details & Tips
8:30 AM Breakfast at Zak the Baker Wynwood's beloved bakery — sourdough, pastries, and great coffee. Expect a line; it moves fast.
9:30 AM Wynwood Walls & street art district Open-air museum of world-class murals. Buy tickets online. Wander surrounding streets for free public art.
