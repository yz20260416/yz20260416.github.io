Shangri-La Travel Guide: Top 10 Must-See Attractions

Shangri-La, located in Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture in northwestern Yunnan Province, sits at an elevation of roughly 3,200 meters on the Tibetan Plateau. Renamed from Zhongdian in 2001 to evoke the mythical paradise of James Hilton's novel Lost Horizon, the city serves as a gateway to Tibetan culture, alpine lakes, monasteries, and the dramatic gorges of the Three Parallel Rivers region. The attractions below cover the city's cultural core, nearby natural wonders, and day-trip destinations within Diqing Prefecture. Prices and hours change seasonally, so confirm locally before visiting.

  1. Ganden Sumtseling Monastery (Songzanlin Monastery)

    The largest Tibetan Buddhist monastery in Yunnan and the spiritual heart of Shangri-La, Sumtseling was founded in 1679 under the patronage of the Fifth Dalai Lama and modeled on Lhasa's Potala Palace. The complex houses hundreds of monks and several assembly halls with elaborate murals, gilded statues, and butter-lamp chapels. Morning prayer sessions and the surrounding hillside kora (pilgrimage circuit) are highlights.

    Ticket priceApproximately CNY 90–115 (varies by season and whether a shuttle is included)
    Opening hoursRoughly 07:30–18:30 daily; shorter hours in winter

    Tips: Arrive early to observe morning prayers; dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered); walk the kora path clockwise; photography inside assembly halls is often restricted.

  2. Dukezong Old Town

    Shangri-La's historic Tibetan old town, a warren of cobblestone lanes lined with wooden Tibetan houses, cafés, and shops. Its centerpiece is the Guishan Temple and the giant Prayer Wheel of Great Buddha, which pilgrims spin clockwise. Much of the town was damaged by fire in 2014 and rebuilt, but the atmosphere, evening dancing in the square, and hillside views remain worthwhile.

    Ticket priceFree to enter the old town; small fee (about CNY 30–50) for the prayer wheel platform
    Opening hoursOpen 24 hours; shops typically 09:00–22:00

    Tips: Visit at dusk when locals gather for circle dancing; climb to the prayer wheel for sunset views over the valley; be mindful that some rebuilt areas are quieter than expected.

  3. Pudacuo National Park

    China's first national park (established 2007), Pudacuo protects alpine lakes, marshlands, and virgin forests between roughly 3,500 and 4,200 meters. The park has two main scenic areas: Shudu Lake, with a boardwalk through meadows and rhododendron groves, and Bita Lake, a quieter forest lake reached by shuttle and trail. Wildlife includes black-necked cranes, yaks, and diverse birdlife.

    Ticket priceApproximately CNY 100–140 including mandatory shuttle bus
    Opening hoursAbout 08:00–17:00 (last entry around 15:00–16:00)

    Tips: Altitude here is high — walk slowly and hydrate; bring sun protection and rain gear; the park is about 22 km east of town and reachable by taxi or tour bus.

  4. Songzanlin Grassland and Napa Lake

    Napa Lake, a seasonal wetland about 8 km northwest of the city, floods in summer and dries into pasture in winter, when black-necked cranes and other migratory birds arrive. The surrounding grasslands, dotted with yaks and horses, offer classic plateau scenery and horse-riding opportunities.

    Ticket priceOften free to view from the road; some fenced viewing areas charge CNY 30–60
    Opening hoursAccessible daylight hours; best in early morning and late afternoon

    Tips: Best birdwatching is December–March; summer brings green meadows but afternoon showers; negotiate horse-riding prices in advance.

  5. Balagezong Grand Canyon

    A deep gorge of the Jinsha (Yangtze) River about 50 km northwest of Shangri-La, Balagezong combines dramatic cliffs, a Tibetan village, sacred caves, and a glass viewing platform over the canyon. The scenic area includes shuttle transport between viewpoints and short hikes.

    Ticket priceApproximately CNY 170–220 including shuttle
    Opening hoursAbout 08:00–17:30

    Tips: The site is large — allow a full day including transport; the glass platform is not for those with vertigo; check road conditions in rainy season.

  6. Tiger Leaping Gorge

    One of the world's deepest river canyons, where the Jinsha River surges between the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain and Haba Snow Mountain, with a drop of several thousand meters. The High Trail trek (2–3 days) and shorter descents to the famous rock are popular. The gorge is about 100 km south of Shangri-La, often visited en route to or from Lijiang.

    Ticket priceAbout CNY 45–65 for the scenic area
    Opening hoursRoughly 07:00–18:00; trails accessible with daylight

    Tips: The descent to the Tiger Leaping Stone involves steep stairs — allow 1.5–2 hours round trip; guesthouses along the High Trail make multi-day hikes manageable; carry cash for small fees.

  7. Meili Snow Mountain (Kawagarbo) and Feilai Temple

    The sacred 6,740-meter Kawagarbo, highest peak in Yunnan and unclimbed out of respect for local beliefs, anchors a range of thirteen peaks. The viewing village of Feilai Temple, about 180 km from Shangri-La via Deqin, offers sunrise views of the massif and the Mingyong Glacier. The drive passes the dramatic Baima Snow Pass (4,292 m).

    Ticket priceScenic area pass approximately CNY 150–230 (varies by season and includes viewpoints)
    Opening hoursViewpoints open daylight hours; sunrise viewing is the main draw

    Tips: This is a 2–3 day trip — acclimatize in Shangri-La first; clear views of the peak are most likely October–May; bring warm layers even in summer.

  8. Baishuitai Terraces

    A striking natural formation of white travertine terraces cascading down a hillside, formed by calcium carbonate deposits from spring water. The site is also culturally significant to the Naxi people as a birthplace of Dongba culture. It lies about 100 km southeast of Shangri-La, often combined with a Tiger Leaping Gorge itinerary.

    Ticket priceApproximately CNY 30–50
    Opening hoursAbout 08:00–18:00

    Tips: Do not walk on the terraces — stay on designated paths; the light is best in late afternoon; the site is remote, so arrange transport in advance.
