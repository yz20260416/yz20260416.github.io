Moscow Travel Weather Guide

Moscow's climate divides sharply between a long, cold winter and a brief, warm summer, and the data makes the trade-offs clear. July is the standout month, with highs near 26°C and lows around 16°C — genuinely comfortable for sightseeing, though rain days are frequent (12), so pack a compact umbrella. January is the toughest: highs of -4°C and lows near -9°C, with 12.8 rain/snow days, meaning heavy coats, thermal layers and short outdoor stints are essential. Spring and autumn are transitional and unpredictable, while October and December are the wettest-feeling months by day count. Choose late June to August for the best odds of pleasant weather.

Practical tips

  • Pack for rain year-round: no month averages fewer than about 8 rain days, and October–December exceed 11 — always carry a compact umbrella or waterproof shell.
  • Winter (Dec–Feb) needs serious kit: with lows near -9°C, bring insulated boots, thermal base layers, gloves and a hat; limit long outdoor walks.
  • Target July–August for comfort: highs of 24–26°C make these the best months for walking tours and parks, but book accommodation early due to high crowds.
  • Use shoulder months for value: May–June and September offer mild temperatures with moderate crowds — good balance of weather and cost.
  • Layer for swing days: spring and autumn see large day-night temperature gaps (often 10°C+), so dress in removable layers.
