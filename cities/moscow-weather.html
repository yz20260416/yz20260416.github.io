Moscow Weather by Month

Moscow has a humid continental climate with long, cold winters and short, warm summers. Average temperatures range from about −8 °C (18 °F) in the coldest winter months to roughly +19 °C (66 °F) in July, and precipitation is fairly evenly spread through the year, with slightly wetter summers. The table below gives typical monthly averages for temperature (daytime high / nighttime low) and precipitation, followed by guidance on the best months to visit.

February −3 / 27 −9 / 16 ≈44 Coldest-feeling month; ice and snow persist

November +1 / 34 −3 / 27 ≈58 Gloomy; sleet and early snow; short days
December −3 / 27 −8 / 18 ≈56 Winter sets in; festive lights; snowy streets

Best months to visit:

  • Late May to early September — the most comfortable window for sightseeing, with mild-to-warm temperatures, long daylight hours, and the city's parks, cafés, and riverfront at their best. June–August is peak season.
  • May and September — excellent shoulder-season choices: fewer crowds than midsummer, generally pleasant weather, and lower prices.
  • December to February — ideal for travelers who want a classic snowy Russian winter, festive decorations, and winter sports, but expect short days and a need for serious cold-weather gear.

Practical Tips

  • Pack in layers year-round: Moscow weather can shift quickly, and indoor heating is strong in winter.
  • Winter essentials (Nov–Mar): insulated waterproof boots with good grip, a warm coat, hat, gloves, and a scarf. Expect icy sidewalks and dress accordingly.
  • Summer essentials (Jun–Aug): light clothing plus a compact rain jacket or umbrella — July is the wettest month and afternoon thunderstorms are common.
  • Mind the daylight: days are very short in December (roughly 7 hours of daylight) and very long in June (about 17+ hours), which affects sightseeing schedules.
  • Check the forecast before you go: averages are useful for planning, but Moscow can see notable swings — a late-spring cold snap or an autumn warm spell is not unusual.
  • Shoulder seasons save money: April–May and September–October often offer better hotel rates than the summer peak, though spring streets can be muddy during the thaw.
