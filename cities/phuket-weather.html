Phuket Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Travel Planning

Phuket's tropical climate stays warm year-round, with average highs ranging from 28.9°C to 32.4°C, so the real variable is rain. February is the standout month for a beach holiday, with just 8.4 rain days on average and highs near 31.7°C—expect long stretches of sunshine and the driest conditions of the year. October is the wettest and riskiest, averaging 27.6 rain days, meaning showers or storms are likely on most days and boat trips or beach plans may need flexibility. The practical takeaway: book February for reliable sun, and treat October–November as a gamble best suited to flexible travelers who don't mind indoor time.

Practical tips

  • Book February (or late December–January) for the most reliable sunshine; February averages only 8.4 rain days versus October's 27.6.
  • Temperatures barely vary (lows around 24–25°C, highs 29–32°C), so pack light, breathable clothing and sun protection year-round rather than cold-weather gear.
  • In wet-season months (May–November), schedule boat trips and outdoor activities in the morning, as rain is often heavier later in the day.
  • Travel insurance and flexible bookings are wise for October and November, when rain is likely on most days and sea conditions can cancel tours.
  • Expect peak crowds and higher prices in December–February; May–October offers quieter beaches and discounts if you can tolerate frequent showers.
