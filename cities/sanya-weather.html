Sanya Travel Weather Guide

Sanya's real 2021–2025 averages show a classic tropical monsoon pattern: a dry, comfortable winter and a hot, very wet summer. The standout month is January, with highs of just 24.4°C, lows of 18.1°C, and only 2.4 rain days — ideal for beaches, hiking, and sightseeing without heat stress or washed-out days. By contrast, June is the wettest month at 22.6 rain days with highs of 31.1°C, meaning frequent downpours and high humidity; plan indoor activities, flexible schedules, and rain gear if visiting then. April and November are useful shoulder months — April warms up but rain jumps to 10.2 days, while November stays warm (27.8°C highs) with rain falling back to 6.4 days.

Practical tips

  • Time your trip for dry months: December through March offer the lowest rain-day counts (2.4–4.4 days) and comfortable highs of 24–27°C — the safest bet for beach and outdoor plans.
  • Avoid the wet-season peak: June–October averages 20+ rain days per month, so build flexibility into itineraries and keep indoor alternatives (malls, museums, spas) handy.
  • Pack for heat and rain: Even in dry months, bring sun protection; from April onward add a compact umbrella, quick-dry clothing, and waterproof bags for electronics.
  • Watch typhoon season: August and September carry the highest storm risk — monitor forecasts and choose refundable bookings or travel insurance with weather coverage.
  • Book early for peak season: January, February, and December combine the best weather with the highest crowds and prices, so reserve accommodation and transport weeks in advance.
