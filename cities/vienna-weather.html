Vienna Travel Weather Guide

Vienna's climate rewards travelers who time their visit thoughtfully. The data shows June through September as the sweet spot: highs of 22–27.5°C and lows of 12.7–17.4°C make outdoor sightseeing, café terraces, and Danube walks genuinely pleasant, though you should still pack a light rain layer since rain days hover around 10 per month. January is the toughest month, with highs near 5°C, lows dipping to -1.1°C, and 9.2 rain days — expect cold, damp days that push you toward museums and coffee houses. Practically, late spring and early autumn offer the best balance of comfort and manageable crowds, while midsummer brings peak warmth alongside peak tourism.

Practical tips

  • Rain days are frequent year-round (roughly 6–13 per month), so always pack a compact umbrella and a waterproof layer regardless of season.
  • For the best comfort-to-crowd ratio, aim for March, September, or October — mild temperatures with moderate rain and thinner crowds.
  • In July and August, sightsee in the morning and reserve midday for shaded parks, museums, or air-conditioned cafés.
  • Winter visitors (December–February) should bring insulated, waterproof footwear and plan around indoor attractions and Christmas markets.
  • Rain-day ratings reflect frequency rather than intensity; even "High" months usually offer plenty of dry hours between showers.
