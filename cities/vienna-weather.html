Vienna Weather by Month

Vienna sits in Central Europe's temperate continental climate zone, which means the city experiences four distinct seasons with warm, sometimes humid summers and cold, often grey winters. Rainfall is fairly evenly distributed throughout the year, with modest peaks in late spring and summer. For travelers, this translates into a destination that changes character dramatically month to month—from blooming baroque gardens in April to glittering Christmas markets in December. The best months to visit are generally April through June and September through October, when temperatures are pleasant, crowds are manageable, and the city's outdoor cafés and parks are at their best. July and August are warm but can be crowded and occasionally stormy, while November through February are cold, quiet, and atmospheric—ideal for museum-goers, concert lovers, and anyone seeking a romantic winter experience.

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall: May, June, September, October — Comfortable temperatures (15–24 °C / 59–75 °F), moderate rainfall, and the city at its most photogenic. Gardens are in bloom in spring; autumn brings golden foliage and heuriger (wine tavern) season.
  • Best for budget travelers: January, February, November — Lower hotel rates, fewer crowds, and a chance to enjoy Vienna's indoor cultural riches (museums, opera, coffee houses) without the summer throngs.
  • Best for festive atmosphere: December — Christmas markets (Rathausplatz, Schönbrunn, Spittelberg), mulled wine, and twinkling lights make the cold worthwhile. Pack warm layers.
  • Best for warm-weather lovers: July and August — Long daylight hours, outdoor dining, Danube Island swimming, and open-air concerts. Expect peak prices and crowds, plus occasional afternoon thunderstorms.

Practical Tips

  • Layer up year-round: Even in summer, evenings can cool off, and Vienna's weather is changeable. A light jacket or sweater is always a good idea.
  • Pack an umbrella: Rain falls in every month, though rarely in torrential amounts. A compact umbrella and water-resistant shoes will serve you well, especially in spring and summer.
  • Winter gear is essential from November to March: Temperatures regularly dip below freezing, and wind chill in the city center can feel sharp. Bring a warm coat, gloves, a hat, and waterproof boots for occasional snow.
  • Book accommodation early for peak seasons: May–June and September–October, plus December (Christmas market season) and New Year's Eve, see the highest demand. Reserve hotels and concert tickets well in advance.
  • Check the forecast daily: Vienna's weather can shift quickly, particularly in transitional months (March–April and October–November). A reliable weather app will help you plan outdoor activities.
  • Embrace indoor alternatives: On rainy or bitterly cold days, Vienna's world-class museums, coffee houses, and concert halls are perfect refuges—no visit is ruined by a grey sky.
  • Summer heat tips: July and August afternoons can reach 30 °C+ (86 °F+). Plan sightseeing for mornings and evenings, seek shade in parks like the Prater or Augarten, and stay hydrated.
