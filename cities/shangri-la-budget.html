Shangri-La (Zhongdian), perched at 3,200 meters in Yunnan's Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, blends Tibetan culture with dramatic alpine scenery. Because it sits at high altitude and depends on imported goods, prices run somewhat higher than lowland Yunnan towns like Dali. This guide breaks down realistic daily and per-item costs across three travel styles, based on typical 2024–2025 prices in Chinese yuan (CNY). As a rough reference, CNY 1 ≈ USD 0.14 / EUR 0.13.
