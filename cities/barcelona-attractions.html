Barcelona Top 10 Attractions

Barcelona is a city where Gothic stone meets modernist curves, and the Mediterranean breeze carries the scent of tapas and sea salt. From Gaudí’s surreal masterpieces to lively markets and sun-drenched beaches, the Catalan capital offers an unforgettable mix of culture, architecture, and cuisine. To help you plan, we’ve curated the 10 must-see attractions — complete with descriptions, current ticket prices (2025), and smart visiting tips. Prices are per adult unless noted; booking online often saves time and money.
