Berlin is one of Europe's most affordable major capitals, with a wide spread of prices that rewards both shoestring travelers and those seeking comfort. This guide breaks down realistic daily and per-trip costs for three travel styles, covering accommodation, food, local transport, and activities. Figures are given in euros per person per day unless otherwise noted, and assume a trip of three to five days. Prices are approximate and vary by season, with May through September and the Christmas market period being the most expensive.

Accommodation Breakdown

Type Typical Price (per night) Notes
Hostel dorm bed €15–35 Cheapest in Kreuzberg, Neukölln, and near Alexanderplatz; prices rise sharply on weekends and during trade fairs.
Private hostel room / budget guesthouse €45–80 Good value for couples; shared bathrooms common at the lower end.
Mid-range hotel (3–4 star) €70–130 Wide choice in Mitte, Prenzlauer Berg, and Charlottenburg; breakfast often extra (€10–18).
Apartment rental (short stay) €80–150 Ideal for stays of three nights or more; confirm registration and check-in details in advance.
Luxury hotel (5 star) €200–450+ Properties around Unter den Linden, Potsdamer Platz, and the Ku'damm; suites and peak dates cost considerably more.

Practical Tips

  • Book around trade fairs: Major events such as IFA, ITB, and Berlin Marathon week push hotel and hostel prices up dramatically. Check the city's event calendar before booking.
  • Use multi-day transit passes: A 7-day AB ticket is usually cheaper than several day tickets, and Berlin's network covers nearly every major sight.
  • Carry cash: Many small restaurants, bakeries, and kiosks are cash-only or have card minimums.
  • Eat at lunch: Mid-range restaurants often offer "Mittagstisch" lunch
