Best Time to Visit Guilin

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are December. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

Guilin's subtropical climate is warm and humid year-round, but the real data reveals a clear split. October is the standout month: highs near 27°C, lows around 18°C, and only 6.4 rain days make it the driest and most comfortable window for karst scenery and river cruises. June is the worst for sightseeing — 25.8 rain days and 30°C heat mean persistent downpours, high humidity, and possible flooding along the Li River. Late spring (April–May) is lush but wet, while July–September brings intense heat and typhoon-season downpours. Plan outdoor highlights for October and treat June as a backup month.

Practical tips

  • Target October for the best weather: only 6.4 rain days and highs near 27°C make it the clearest, most comfortable month for Li River cruises and karst viewpoints.
  • Avoid June if you can: 25.8 rain days and 30°C heat mean near-daily downpours, high humidity, and elevated flood risk on the Li River.
  • Pack for rain year-round: every month averages at least 5.8 rain days, so a compact umbrella and quick-dry clothing are always worth carrying.
  • Book cruises early in peak season: October and summer holidays bring high crowds, so reserve Li River and Longji Rice Terrace trips in advance.
  • Use mornings in summer: July–September highs of 32–33°C mean outdoor sightseeing is best before 10am and after 4pm.
