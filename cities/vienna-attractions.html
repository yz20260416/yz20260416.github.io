Vienna Top 10 Attractions

Vienna, the former imperial capital on the Danube, blends grand palaces, world-class museums, and a lively coffeehouse culture into one elegant city. From the gilded halls of Schönbrunn to the cutting-edge KunstHausWien, the city offers a wealth of experiences for every traveler. Below you’ll find the top 10 attractions with essential details on what to see, current ticket prices (as of 2025), and practical tips to make the most of your visit. All prices are in euros and reflect standard adult admission unless noted.
