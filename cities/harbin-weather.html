Harbin's weather splits sharply between a long, brutal winter and a short, warm, wet summer. January is the coldest month (avg high −12.1°C, low −21.2°C) and the reason to visit if you want the Ice and Snow Festival — but you'll need serious cold-weather gear. July is the warmest and wettest (avg high 28.4°C, 16.2 rain days), so pack for heat and frequent showers. The sweet spots are May, September and October: mild highs of 12–22°C with only moderate rain. Avoid late June through August if heavy rain bothers you, and expect deep winter cold from December through February.

Practical tips

  • Visit in May or September for the most comfortable temperatures (highs around 20–22°C) with only moderate rain.
  • For the Ice and Snow Festival, come in January or February and bring thermal layers, insulated boots, gloves and a face covering for lows near −20°C.
  • Avoid July and August if you dislike rain — these months average 14–16 rain days and frequent heavy showers.
  • Pack a compact umbrella and waterproof shoes for any trip between June and September.
  • Expect large crowds during the winter festival and summer holidays; book accommodation and attractions well in advance.
