Best Time to Visit Moscow

Moscow is Russia's vast capital, a city of onion-domed churches, monumental Soviet architecture, world-class museums and a fast-paced modern energy. Visit in late spring or early autumn for mild weather and long daylight, while winter offers a dramatic, snow-covered experience for those who can handle the cold.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Red Square — Moscow's iconic central plaza, flanked by the Kremlin, St Basil's Cathedral and the GUM department store.
  2. Saint Basil's Cathedral — A riotously colourful 16th-century cathedral crowned with onion domes and now a museum.
  3. Moscow Kremlin — The fortified seat of Russian power, home to cathedrals, palaces and the Armoury Chamber's treasures.
  4. Tretyakov Gallery — The world's finest collection of Russian fine art, from medieval icons to 20th-century masterpieces.
  5. Gorky Park — A sprawling riverside park with gardens, skating rinks in winter and open-air cultural events.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Russian Ruble (RUB, ₽)
  • Language: Russian (English is limited outside major tourist sites)
  • Time Zone: MSK (UTC+3)
  • Visa: Most international visitors need a visa arranged in advance, though some nationalities qualify for an e-visa or visa-free entry.

FAQ

Is Moscow safe for tourists?
Yes, central Moscow is generally safe, though pickpocketing occurs in crowded areas, so keep valuables secure and stay aware of your surroundings.
How do I get around Moscow?
The metro is fast, cheap and famously ornate, with stations doubling as attractions; taxis and ride-hailing apps are also widely available.
What should I know about paying for things in Moscow?
International credit cards may not work due to sanctions, so carry enough cash (rubles) or arrange a local payment method before arrival.
