Istanbul Travel Weather Guide

Istanbul's weather data reveals a city of two distinct travel seasons. July and August are the clear standouts: highs near 30°C, lows around 22°C, and only 2.6–3.2 rain days per month make these months ideal for sightseeing, ferry rides, and outdoor cafés with minimal packing concerns. By contrast, December through March is the wettest stretch, with December (12 rain days), March (12.2), and January (11.4) demanding a rain jacket and flexible indoor plans. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer a sweet spot of mild temperatures and moderate rainfall, though crowds peak in high summer.

Practical tips

  • Book July or August for the driest, sunniest weather, but reserve hotels and major attractions well in advance due to peak crowds.
  • Carry a compact umbrella and waterproof layer if traveling December–April, when rain days frequently exceed 10 per month.
  • Choose May, September, or October for the best balance of mild temperatures, moderate rain, and manageable crowds.
  • In summer, schedule outdoor sightseeing for early morning and keep midday hours for shaded courtyards, museums, or ferry rides.
  • Even in dry months, pack a light layer—Istanbul's evenings, especially near the water, can feel cooler than daytime highs suggest.
