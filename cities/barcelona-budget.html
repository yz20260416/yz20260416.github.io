Barcelona Travel Cost Guide

Barcelona is one of Europe's most visited cities, and for good reason: Gothic streets, Gaudí masterpieces, Mediterranean beaches, and world-class food all sit within a compact, walkable city. The good news for budget-conscious travelers is that Barcelona can be surprisingly affordable — especially if you plan around its excellent public transport, set lunch menus, and free cultural attractions. This guide breaks down realistic daily costs across three travel styles — budget, mid-range, and luxury — so you can plan a trip that fits your wallet. All figures are per person, in euros (€), and based on 2025 prices.

Accommodation Breakdown

  • Budget Hostels in El Raval, Gràcia, or near Sants station offer dorms from €20–35. Book 2–3 months ahead in summer. Budget private rooms in guesthouses (hostales) start around €45–70.
  • Mid-Range A 3–4★ hotel in Eixample or Poble-sec runs €90–160. Well-located apartments (minimum-stay rules apply) are great for families and longer stays.
  • Luxury Expect €250–600+ for 5★ properties near Passeig de Gràcia or the Gothic Quarter waterfront. High-design boutique hotels often sit in the €300–450 range.

Tip: Tourist tax (€2.25–7 per person per night, depending on category) is usually added on top of the room rate.

Food & Drink Breakdown

  • Coffee: €1.50–2.50 at a bar; €3–4 at a specialty café.
  • Menú del día (weekday set lunch, 2–3 courses + drink): €12–18 — the single best value in the city.
  • Tapas dinner: €20–30 per person with a drink at a casual bar; €45+ at popular spots in El Born.
  • Fine dining tasting menus: €90–180+ per person (e.g., Michelin-starred restaurants in Eixample).
  • Supermarket picnic: €8–12 for bread, cheese, fruit, and wine — perfect for a beach or park lunch.
  • Drinks: Beer €3–5, glass of wine €3–6, cocktail €10–15 in central bars.

Transport Breakdown

  • T-casual card: 10 rides for €12.55 — valid on metro, bus, and FGC within Zone 1. Best deal for most visitors.
  • Single metro ticket: €2.65 (includes free transfers within 75 minutes).
  • Airport transfer: Aerobús to Plaça Catalunya €7.25 one way; metro L9 Sud €5.50; taxi €30–40.
