Nairobi Top 10 Attractions

Nairobi, Kenya’s vibrant capital, is a city where urban energy meets wild savannah. Known as the “Green City in the Sun,” it surprises travelers with a national park within city limits, rich cultural heritage, and a thriving food and art scene. Whether you’re a wildlife enthusiast, history buff, or curious explorer, these top 10 attractions capture the essence of Nairobi — from elephant orphans and giraffe kisses to colonial-era museums and lively markets. Below you’ll find descriptions, current ticket prices (in USD/KES), and practical tips to make the most of each visit.

Attraction Description Ticket price (approx.) Visiting tips
1. Nairobi National Park
The world’s only national park within a capital city. Spot lions, rhinos, giraffes, and zebras against a skyline backdrop. The park also hosts the Sheldrick Wildlife Trust elephant orphanage and the Nairobi Animal Orphanage.
 $43 (non-resident) / KES 5,600
