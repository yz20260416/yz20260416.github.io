Dubai 3-Day Itinerary

Dubai is a city of superlatives — home to the world's tallest building, sprawling desert dunes, and a skyline that seems to rise straight out of the Arabian Gulf. In just three days you can experience its futuristic skyline, wander through traditional souks, ride the dunes at sunset, and dine at world-class restaurants. This itinerary balances iconic landmarks with cultural immersion and a touch of relaxation, so you get the most out of a short stay. Each day is organized by neighborhood to minimize travel time, with restaurant recommendations for every meal.

Day 1 — Modern Dubai: Downtown & Burj Khalifa

Time Activity Details
9:00 AM Dubai Mall & Aquarium Start at the Dubai Mall (one of the world's largest). Visit the Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo, or simply explore the shops and the waterfall atrium.
12:30 PM Lunch at Souk Al Bahar Dine at Time Out Market Dubai for a curated selection of local and international dishes, or Al Fanar Restaurant for Emirati cuisine with Burj Lake views.
2:00 PM Burj Khalifa — At the Top Book a timed ticket in advance for the 124th/125th floor observation deck. For a splurge, the 148th floor SKY experience includes a guided tour.
4:30 PM Dubai Fountain Show Catch the free fountain show at the base of Burj Khalifa (evening shows run every 30 minutes after sunset; daytime shows at 1 PM and 1:30 PM on weekdays).
7:00 PM Dinner at At.mosphere or Rivington Grill At.mosphere (Level 122, Burj Khalifa) offers a dramatic fine-dining experience. For a more relaxed option, Rivington Grill in Souk Al Bahar serves excellent British fare.

Day 2 — Old Dubai & the Creek

Time Activity Details
9:00 AM Al Fahidi Historical Neighborhood Wander the narrow lanes of this restored 19th-century district. Visit the Coffee Museum and the Arabian Tea House for a traditional breakfast.
11:00 AM Dubai Museum (Al Fahidi Fort) The city's oldest building houses an excellent museum on Dubai's history and Bedouin heritage (entry ~AED 3).
12:30 PM Abra ride across Dubai Creek Take a traditional wooden abra (AED 1) from Bur Dubai to Deira — the cheapest and most authentic crossing in the city.
1:00 PM Gold & Spice Souks Explore the Gold Souk and the aromatic Spice Souk in Deira. Bargaining is expected — start at about half the asking price.
2:30 PM Lunch at Aroos Damascus A legendary Syrian restaurant in Deira known for grilled meats, hummus, and fresh bread. Budget-friendly and beloved by locals.
5:00 PM Dubai Creek Harbour sunset Head to Dubai Creek Harbour for views of the skyline and the Creek Tower area — a beautiful sunset spot away from the crowds.
7:30 PM Dinner at Al Ustad Special Kabab A no-frills Iranian kebab institution in Bur Dubai, famous since 1978. Try the chicken kebab and the saffron rice.

Day 3 — Desert & Beach

Time Activity Details
8:00 AM Jumeirah Beach & Burj Al Arab views Morning walk along Jumeirah Public Beach with iconic views of the sail-shaped Burj Al Arab. Swim or relax before the heat peaks.
10:30 AM Madinat Jumeirah Stroll this Arabian-style resort complex with canals, a souk, and great photo opportunities of the Burj Al Arab.
12:30 PM Lunch at Pierchic or The Beach House Pierchic (overwater fine dining) is a special-occasion spot; The Beach House at Anantara offers relaxed Mediterranean fare with sea views.
