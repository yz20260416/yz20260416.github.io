The data shows a clear split between Huangshan's comfortable shoulder seasons and its demanding extremes. October and November are the sweet spot: highs of 24.8°C and 19.4°C, lows of 15.4°C and 9.0°C, and only 6.6 and 7.6 rain days — meaning mostly dry trails, good visibility, and manageable temperatures for long hiking days. By contrast, June is the wettest month at 19.0 rain days, so expect frequent downpours, slick granite steps, and clouded views; July and August bring punishing highs of 33.6–34.3°C. Plan summit hikes for autumn and pack rain gear if you travel in late spring or early summer.

Practical tips

  • Target October or November for the best balance of mild temperatures, low rain days, and clear summit views.
  • Avoid June if possible — 19 rain days means frequent downpours, slick granite steps, and obscured scenery.
  • In July and August, start hikes at dawn to beat highs above 33°C, and carry extra water.
  • Even in dry months, pack a waterproof shell — mountain weather changes fast and mist is common.
  • For winter visits (December–February), bring insulated layers; lows near 1–3°C make summit nights and early mornings genuinely cold.
