Sydney Travel Weather Guide

Based on 2021–2025 averages, Sydney's weather is most reliably pleasant in September and October, when highs sit around 21–23°C and rain days drop to just 7–12 per month—ideal for coastal walks, ferries and outdoor sightseeing. The wettest stretch runs from January to March, with roughly 15–16 rain days per month, so summer visitors should plan indoor backups and pack a rain jacket despite the warm 25–26°C highs. Winter (June–August) is mild but cooler, with lows near 8°C and far fewer rain days, making it a quiet, budget-friendly time for city exploring.

Practical tips

  • September is the standout month for dry, mild weather—book outdoor-heavy itineraries then.
  • January–March is the wettest period; always carry a compact rain jacket and keep flexible indoor plans.
  • Winter (June–August) offers the fewest crowds and mild daytime temperatures, but pack warm layers for cool nights near 8°C.
  • Even in dry months, UV levels are high—bring sunscreen and a hat for beach and harbour days.
  • Check daily forecasts on arrival, as Sydney's shower patterns can shift quickly between coastal and inland areas.
