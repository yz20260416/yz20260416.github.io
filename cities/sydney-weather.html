Sydney Weather by Month

Sydney enjoys a temperate, oceanic climate with warm summers and mild winters, making it a year-round destination for travelers. The city averages around 2,500 hours of sunshine annually, and while rain falls throughout the year, it rarely disrupts travel plans for long. Understanding Sydney's seasonal patterns—from the balmy beach days of January to the crisp, clear winter mornings of July—will help you pack appropriately and choose the ideal time for your visit, whether you're planning to surf at Bondi, hike the Blue Mountains, or explore the harbour by ferry.

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall: March–April and October–November — mild temperatures, moderate rainfall, and fewer crowds than peak summer.
  • Best for beach lovers: January–February — warmest water and reliable sunshine, though expect crowds and higher prices.
  • Best for budget travelers: June–August — lower accommodation rates, dry, sunny days, and excellent whale-watching.
  • Best for events: January (New Year's Eve, Sydney Festival), February–March (Mardi Gras), May–June (Vivid Sydney).
  • Avoid if crowd-averse: Late December through January, when domestic and international tourism peaks.

Practical Tips

  • Pack in layers: Sydney weather can shift quickly—mornings may be cool even on warm days, and sea breezes cool the coast in the afternoon.
  • Sun protection is essential: Australia's UV index is high year-round; wear sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses even on cloudy days.
  • Rain gear: Rainfall is spread evenly across the year. A compact umbrella or light rain jacket is worth carrying, especially February–June.
  • Summer heat: January and February can bring heatwaves above 35°C (95°F). Plan outdoor activities for early morning and stay hydrated.
  • Winter warmth: Sydney homes and some venues can be drafty; bring a warm jacket for evenings, especially June–August.
  • Book ahead in peak season: Accommodation and popular tours fill quickly around New Year's, Mardi Gras, and school holidays (mid-December to late January).
  • Water temperatures: Ocean swimming is comfortable December–April (around 21–24°C / 70–75°F) but chilly in winter (17–18°C / 63–64°F).
