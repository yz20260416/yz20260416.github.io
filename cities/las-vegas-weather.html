Las Vegas Travel Weather Guide

Las Vegas is a desert city where timing your visit matters enormously. The real data shows April and October as the sweet spots: April averages a comfortable 27.4°C high with just 0.2 rain days, and October delivers a mild 28.4°C high with only 1.0 rain day — near-perfect conditions for exploring the Strip and day-tripping to Red Rock Canyon. July is the toughest month, with a punishing 41.8°C average high and lows that barely dip below 28°C, making midday outdoor activity genuinely risky. Practically, this means booking spring or autumn for sightseeing-heavy trips, and treating summer visits as indoor, pool, and evening affairs.

Practical tips

  • Book April or October for the best balance of comfortable temperatures (27–28°C highs) and almost no rain — these months suit sightseeing, hiking, and pool time alike.
  • Avoid midday outdoor activity in June–August. With highs of 39–42°C, shift walking tours, pool time, and excursions to early morning or after dark, and carry water at all times.
  • Pack layers year-round. Desert nights drop sharply — winter lows sit near 3–5°C and even summer evenings can feel cooler than expected after the extreme daytime heat.
  • Don't over-worry about rain. Even the wettest month (March, 4.0 rain days) rarely brings all-day storms; a light packable rain layer is enough.
  • Watch for monsoon season in August, when rain days rise to 2.8 and brief intense storms can cause flash flooding in washes and canyons — check forecasts before any desert hike.
