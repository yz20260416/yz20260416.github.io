Lima Travel Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Conditions

Lima's weather is famously mild and famously gray. The warmest, sunniest window runs from January to March, when highs reach roughly 24–25°C and lows stay near 20°C — this is peak beach and outdoor season, but also peak crowds and higher hotel prices. February stands out as the warmest month (25.1°C high), while August is the coolest and dampest-feeling (18.6°C high, 14.1°C low) under the persistent garúa mist. Practically, travelers wanting sunshine should book December–March and pack light layers for cool evenings; those visiting April–November should expect overcast skies, cool mornings, and near-zero measurable rain, making it ideal for sightseeing without an umbrella.

Practical Tips

  • Lima receives almost no measurable rain year-round, but winter garúa mist makes June–September feel damp — a light water-resistant layer helps more than an umbrella.
  • Pack for two seasons: summer-weight clothing for December–March (highs 22–25°C) and warm layers for July–September (highs around 19°C, lows near 14°C).
  • Book accommodation early for January–March, when domestic beach tourism and holidays push crowds and prices to their peak.
  • If you want sunshine over gray skies, prioritize December–April; outside that window, plan sightseeing around midday when cloud cover is thinnest.
  • Coastal fog can reduce visibility and chill the air in the mornings during winter — schedule outdoor walks and cliffside views for afternoons.
