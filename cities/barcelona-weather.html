Barcelona Travel Weather Guide

Barcelona enjoys a Mediterranean climate with mild winters and hot, humid summers. The data shows the most reliable travel weather in late spring and early summer: May and June pair pleasant highs of 21–27°C with manageable rain (8.6 and 5.6 days respectively). The wettest months are September (10.2 rain days) and March (9.4), so pack a compact umbrella if visiting then. August is the hottest (29.3°C high, 21.5°C low) and busiest, meaning higher prices and crowded beaches. Conversely, January is coolest (14°C high, 4.5°C low) but driest (3.4 rain days), making it ideal for sightseeing without the crowds.

Practical tips

  • Pack layers year-round: evenings can be cool even in summer, and winters are mild but damp.
  • Carry a compact umbrella or rain jacket in March, May, September, and October, when rain days peak.
  • Visit in May or June for the best balance of warm temperatures and manageable rainfall.
  • Avoid August if you dislike crowds and heat; consider late September or October for milder conditions.
  • Use sunscreen and hydrate during July and August, when highs approach 29–30°C.
