Best Time to Visit Phuket

Phuket is Thailand's largest island and a province in the Andaman Sea, connected to the mainland by the Sarasin Bridge. Once wealthy from tin and rubber, it is now a tourism hub with over 100,000 foreign residents. Visit during the dry season from November to April for calm seas and sunshine.

Local Character

Phuket's name comes from the Malay word 'Bukit', meaning hill, reflecting how the island appears from a distance; the island was historically inhabited by Austronesian Malays before Thai settlement, and the northern district still carries the old name Thalang, derived from the Malay 'Telong' meaning cape. Visitors exploring the north will encounter this older identity beyond the beach resorts. In Western charts the island was long known as Junk Ceylon, a corruption of the Malay 'Tanjung Salang'; Portuguese explorer Fernão Mendes Pinto, who arrived in Siam in 1545, described it as a significant port of call where trading vessels stopped for supplies, though by the mid-16th century piracy and difficult sea conditions had caused its prominence to decline. Knowing this explains why Phuket's historic heart lies in the north rather than on the tourist beaches. The island's position on the India–China trade route brought Portuguese, French, Dutch and English ships to its waters, yet Phuket was never colonized by a European power, a point of local pride worth remembering when touring the old town.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Sarasin Bridge — The bridge linking Phuket Island to Phang Nga province on the mainland, marking the island's northern gateway.
  2. Thalang District — The northern district that was the site of Phuket's old capital and still bears the island's historical Malay-derived name.
  3. Phang Nga Bay — The bay east of Phuket separating it from Krabi province, known for its dramatic limestone scenery.
  4. Old Phuket Town — The historic urban core reflecting the island's trading-route heritage and Sino-Portuguese architecture.
  5. Phuket's west coast beaches — The Andaman-facing shoreline that draws most visitors, best enjoyed during the November-to-April dry season.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit Phuket?
November to April offers the driest weather and calmest seas, with January and February being peak season.
How do I reach Phuket from the mainland?
Phuket Island is connected to Phang Nga province by the Sarasin Bridge, and the nearest other province is Krabi across Phang Nga Bay.
Why is the island called Phuket?
The name derives from the Malay word 'Bukit', meaning hill, describing how the island looks from a distance.
