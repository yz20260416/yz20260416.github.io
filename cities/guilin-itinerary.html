Guilin, in the Guangxi region of southern China, is famous for its surreal karst limestone peaks, winding Li River, and misty rice terraces. This 3-day itinerary balances the classic Li River scenery with the quieter countryside and the nearby Longji Rice Terraces. Days are organized by morning, afternoon, and evening, with restaurant suggestions and practical notes. Distances are moderate—plan on taxis, ride-hailing apps (DiDi), or organized tours for the longer trips.

Day 1 – Guilin City and the Li River Gateway

Theme: Orientation, city landmarks, and iconic karst views.

Time Activity Notes
Morning Visit Elephant Trunk Hill (Xiangbishan) and Fubo Hill Start early (8:00–9:00) to avoid crowds. Elephant Trunk Hill is Guilin's symbol; Fubo Hill offers city and river views. Both are close to the city center.
Afternoon Reed Flute Cave (Ludiyan) and Seven Star Park Reed Flute Cave is a large illuminated limestone cave about 20 minutes by taxi from downtown. Combine with Seven Star Park, a short drive away, for a relaxed walk.
Evening Two Rivers and Four Lakes night cruise; stroll Zhengyang Pedestrian Street The evening boat cruise passes illuminated pagodas and bridges. Zhengyang Street is good for snacks and souvenirs.

Restaurant Recommendations – Day 1

  • Lunch: Chunji Roast Goose (multiple branches) – Cantonese-style roast goose and local dishes.
  • Dinner: Guilin Rice Noodle shops near Zhengyang Street (e.g., Shiyue Rice Noodles) – try the dry-tossed Guilin mifen with pickled beans and chili.
  • Alternative: A riverside restaurant near the Two Rivers and Four Lakes area for Guangxi cuisine.

Day 2 – Li River Cruise to Yangshuo

Theme: The classic karst river scenery and Yangshuo countryside.

Time Activity Notes
Morning Li River cruise from Zhujiang Pier to Yangshuo Book in advance. Boats typically depart around 9:00–9:30 and take about 4 hours. The stretch near Xingping (the scene on the 20-yuan note) is the highlight. Sit on the upper deck for views.
Afternoon Arrive Yangshuo; explore West Street or take a countryside bike/scooter ride West Street is touristy but lively. For quieter scenery, cycle toward the Yulong River area or take a bamboo raft on the Yulong River.
Evening Impression Liu Sanjie show (optional) or riverside dinner The open-air show uses the karst landscape as a backdrop. Book tickets ahead; otherwise enjoy a relaxed dinner in Yangshuo.

Restaurant Recommendations – Day 2

  • Lunch: Onboard the cruise (simple set meal) or a riverside restaurant in Yangshuo after arrival.
  • Dinner: Beer fish (pijiu yu) restaurants in Yangshuo – a local specialty of river fish cooked with beer and tomatoes.
  • Snacks: West Street stalls for stuffed rice cakes and fresh fruit.

Day 3 – Longji Rice Terraces or Yangshuo Countryside

Theme: Choose between the dramatic rice terraces or a slower countryside day.

Time Option A: Longji Rice Terraces Option B: Yangshuo Countryside
Morning Early transfer (about 2–2.5 hours) to Longji; hike between Ping'an and Dazhai terraces Bamboo raft on the Yulong River; visit Moon Hill or the Big Banyan Tree
Afternoon Lunch in a Zhuang or Yao village; explore viewpoints; return to Guilin Cycle through villages and rice paddies; lunch at a countryside farmhouse
Evening Return to Guilin; relaxed dinner near the hotel Return to Guilin by late afternoon; dinner in the city

Restaurant Recommendations – Day 3

  • Longji option: Village restaurants serving bamboo rice and local smoked pork.
  • Yangshuo option: Farmhouse restaurants in the countryside for fresh vegetables and river fish.
  • Dinner in Guilin: A local Guangxi restaurant for dishes such as sour bamboo shoots and stir-fried river snails.

Practical Tips

  • Best time to visit: April–October for green scenery and rice terraces; expect heat and rain in summer. Winter is quieter but cooler and mistier.
  • Getting around: Use DiDi or taxis in Guilin; book Li River cruises and Longji tours through hotels or reputable agencies.
  • Li River cruise: Book ahead, especially in peak season. Confirm departure pier and pickup arrangements.
  • Weather: Bring rain gear and comfortable walking shoes; karst areas can be slippery.
  • Money and connectivity: Mobile payment (WeChat/Alipay) is common; carry some cash. A local SIM or eSIM helps with maps and ride-hailing.
  • Language: English is limited outside tourist areas; have hotel names and destinations written in Chinese.
  • Pacing: Day 2 is long; keep Day 3 flexible. If short on time, prioritize the Li River cruise and Yangshuo.
