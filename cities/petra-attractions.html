Petra Top 10 Attractions

Carved into the rose-red cliffs of southern Jordan, Petra is more than a wonder of the ancient world — it’s a journey through 2,000 years of Nabataean history. With so much to see across its vast 264-square-kilometer site, knowing where to focus is key. This guide highlights the 10 most remarkable attractions, complete with descriptions, current ticket prices (for the main Petra archaeological park), and practical advice to make the most of your visit.
