Best Time to Visit Jiuzhaigou

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are April and May. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

Jiuzhaigou sits at over 2,000 metres in the mountains of northern Sichuan, and the five-year averages (2021–2025) show just how strongly the valley's weather swings across the year. The most comfortable window for most travellers is September and October, when highs of 27.2°C and 19.8°C are paired with falling rain-day counts (13.0 and 11.4) and famously vivid autumn colour — book accommodation early, as this is peak season. The least inviting months are July and August: they tie for the hottest highs at 29.8°C, and July is the wettest month of the year at 19.8 rain days, meaning frequent showers, slippery boardwalks and a higher chance of cloud obscuring the lakes. Winter is cold but quiet, with January lows averaging just 1.3°C and only 9.4 rain days.

Practical Tips

  • Pack for rain year-round: every month averages at least 6 rain days, and April–September exceed 17 — a waterproof jacket and non-slip shoes are essential.
  • Layer for big temperature swings: daily ranges of 7–10°C are normal, and winter lows near 1°C mean warm base layers are needed even on sunny days.
  • Time your visit for September–October: these months combine comfortable highs (19.8–27.2°C) with relatively fewer rain days and spectacular autumn colour — but crowds are high, so book early.
  • Avoid July if rain bothers you: it is the wettest month (19.8 rain days) and among the hottest, with the highest risk of washed-out days.
  • Consider winter for solitude: December has the fewest rain days (6.2) and low crowds, but temperatures hover near freezing — bring proper cold-weather gear.
