Athens Travel Cost Guide

Athens is one of Europe's most affordable capital cities, offering travelers a remarkable blend of ancient history, vibrant street life, and Mediterranean cuisine without the price tag of Paris or Rome. Whether you're a backpacker stretching every euro, a mid-range traveler seeking comfort and convenience, or a luxury visitor wanting five-star experiences, Athens can accommodate your budget. This guide breaks down the real costs of accommodation, food, transport, and activities across three travel styles, helping you plan an accurate daily budget. Prices are given in euros (€) and reflect typical 2024–2025 rates, with a note that Athens is generally more affordable outside the peak summer months of July and August.

Accommodation Breakdown

Budget / Backpacker (€20–45 per night)

  • Hostel dorms: €20–35 in central areas like Monastiraki, Psirri, or Koukaki. Most include free Wi-Fi and breakfast; some have rooftop bars with Acropolis views.
  • Budget guesthouses and pensions: €35–45 for a private single or double in less central neighborhoods (e.g., Victoria Square, Metaxourgeio).
  • Budget tips: Book directly with hostels for the best rates, and consider staying slightly outside the tourist core (Neos Kosmos, Pangrati) where prices drop 15–25%.

Mid-Range (€70–130 per night)

  • 3-star hotels: €70–110 in Plaka, Syntagma, or Koukaki, often including breakfast and air conditioning.
  • Boutique apartments (Airbnb/short-term rentals): €80–130 for a stylish studio or one-bedroom with kitchenette — ideal for longer stays.
  • Mid-range tips: Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best value with pleasant weather and lower rates.

Luxury (€200–500+ per night)

  • 5-star hotels: €250–500+ at properties like the Grande Bretagne or Electra Palace, many with Acropolis-view rooms and rooftop pools.
  • Luxury suites and villas: €300–800+ in Kolonaki, the Riviera (Glyfada, Vouliagmeni), or designer apartments in Plaka.
  • Luxury tips: Book well in advance for Acropolis-view rooms; rates can double during peak summer. Consider the coastal suburbs for resort-style stays with easy metro access.

Food & Drink Costs

Budget (€15–25 per day)

  • Souvlaki and gyros: €3–5 per wrap — Athens' best budget meal.
  • Bakeries (fourno): spanakopita, tiropita, and fresh bread for €2–4.
  • Supermarkets: self-catering breakfast and lunch for €8–12 per day (yogurt, fruit, cheese, olives).
  • Cheap eats: koulouri (sesame bread ring) for €1, loukoumades for €3–4.
