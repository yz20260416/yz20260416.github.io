Cape Town Weather: A Month-by-Month Travel Guide

Cape Town has a Mediterranean climate: warm, dry summers and cool, wet winters. The real data shows the best weather in January and February, when highs sit around 25.8°C and rain falls on only 2–3 days per month — ideal for beaches, Table Mountain hikes and the Winelands. The worst weather arrives in June, July and August, with highs of just 16–18°C and roughly 11–12 rain days per month, so expect wet, windy days and pack accordingly. April and October are pleasant shoulder-season months with moderate rain and comfortable temperatures, offering good value and fewer crowds.

Practical tips

  • Pack for both sun and rain: summer needs sunscreen and light layers, while winter (June–August) requires a waterproof jacket, warm layers and sturdy shoes.
  • Time beach and mountain days for January–March, when rain days are lowest and temperatures are warmest.
  • In winter, build flexibility into your itinerary and use clear mornings for Table Mountain or coastal drives.
  • Shoulder months (April, October, November) balance good weather with lower prices and smaller crowds.
  • Remember the strong southeaster ("Cape Doctor") in summer can be breezy, so plan sheltered activities on windy days.
