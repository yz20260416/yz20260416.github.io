Lima Travel Cost Guide

Lima, Peru's sprawling coastal capital, offers travelers a remarkable range of experiences—from world-renowned ceviche and Michelin-starred dining in Miraflores to pre-Columbian ruins tucked between modern high-rises. While it has a reputation as one of South America's more affordable capitals, costs vary dramatically depending on your travel style. A backpacker can comfortably explore Lima on $30–45 per day, while mid-range travelers should budget $70–120, and those seeking luxury can easily spend $200+ daily. This guide breaks down realistic costs across accommodation, food, transport, and activities so you can plan your Lima visit with confidence. All figures are in US dollars (USD) with approximate Peruvian sol (PEN) equivalents, based on an exchange rate of roughly S/3.7 per USD.
