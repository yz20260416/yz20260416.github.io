Top Attractions in Chongqing

Chongqing, a sprawling megacity on the banks of the Yangtze and Jialing rivers, blends dramatic mountain scenery with rich history and modern spectacle. From riverside stilt houses and bustling historic lanes to Buddhist cave art and striking performance venues, the city offers a wide range of experiences. Here are ten attractions most likely to appeal to international visitors.

  1. Hongya Cave (Hongya Dong) — An 11-story stilt-building complex built into the hillside along the southern bank of the Jialing River in the Jiefangbei district. It is one of Chongqing's most popular attractions, housing retail spaces, hotels, and a food market.
    Tip: Visit after dark for the illuminated views; it is busiest in the evening, so arrive early for photos.
  2. Jiefangbei CBD — Chongqing's ultra-dense downtown core, anchored by a large pedestrian mall with a landmark monument tower and surrounded by shopping and commercial buildings. It is the city's iconic urban center.
    Tip: Combine with a walk to nearby Hongya Cave; evenings are lively but crowded.
  3. Ciqikou — A historic subdistrict in Shapingba District, originally called Longyin Town and once known as "Little Chongqing." Its name traces back to porcelain production during the Ming dynasty, and it is celebrated in the proverb "One flagstone road, and one thousand years' Ciqikou."
    Tip: Go on a weekday morning to avoid crowds and sample local snacks along the old flagstone streets.
  4. Chongqing People's Auditorium — A large hall for major meetings and events in Yuzhong, built between 1951 and 1954 with involvement of early Communist Party leaders. The building includes a large auditorium and three adjoining wings, covering a total area of 66,000 m².
    Tip: The exterior is especially photogenic when lit at night; check for public event schedules.
  5. Chongqing Grand Theatre — A performing arts venue in central Chongqing overlooking the Yangtze River. The 64-metre, six-storey building was constructed from 2005 to 2009 and contains two concert halls.
    Tip: The riverside setting offers great skyline views; check the schedule for concerts and performances.
  6. Fengdu Ghost City — A large complex of shrines, temples, and monasteries dedicated to the afterlife, located on Ming mountain in Fengdu County on the north bank of the Yangtze River. It sits about 170 km downstream of Chongqing.
    Tip: Often visited on Yangtze River cruises; allow several hours to explore the hillside complex.
  7. Dazu Rock Carvings — Mount Baoding Buddhist Sculptures — A Buddhist site on a limestone outcropping at 500 metres elevation, about 15 km north of Dazu District center. Primary construction took place during the Southern Song dynasty, and the site is part of the Dazu Rock Carvings World Heritage Site.
    Tip: Pair with nearby Dazu Lotus Manor; bring comfortable shoes for the hillside paths.
  8. Furong Cave — A karst cave located on the banks of the Furong River, about 20 km from the seat of Wulong District. It is a striking natural attraction known for its dramatic limestone formations.
    Tip: The cave can be cool and damp; bring a light jacket and check tour access from Wulong.
  9. Single Tree Vista — A popular overlook park and tower in Nan'an District, known as one of the best places to see a panorama of central Chongqing. It offers views of the Yuzhong District skyline, the Yangtze and Jialing rivers, and the city's night scene.
    Tip: Visit around sunset to catch both daytime views and the famous night skyline.
  10. Qutang Gorge — The shortest of China's Three Gorges, where the Yangtze passes between Chijia Mountain and Baiyan Mountain at the Kuimen entrance. It lies immediately downstream of the ancient Baidicheng fortress.
    Tip: Best experienced on a Yangtze River cruise; the gorge is a highlight of the Three Gorges route.

Practical Tips

  • Chongqing is hilly and densely built — wear comfortable shoes and expect stairs and steep streets.
  • Evening is prime time for skyline and riverside sights such as Hongya Cave and Single Tree Vista.
  • Many major sites are spread across districts; plan travel time and consider the metro for the downtown core.
  • For Dazu Rock Carvings, Fengdu, Furong Cave, and Qutang Gorge, day trips or cruise connections are practical.
  • Check opening hours and event schedules in advance, especially for the Grand Theatre and People's Auditorium.
