Zanzibar Weather by Month

Zanzibar, the spice island off Tanzania’s coast, enjoys a tropical climate with warm temperatures year‑round. However, rainfall and humidity vary dramatically between the long rains (Masika), short rains (Vuli), and the dry seasons. Use this monthly guide to plan your beach holiday, spice tour, or cultural exploration — with average high temperatures, typical rainfall, and honest advice on the best months to visit.
