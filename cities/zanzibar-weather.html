Zanzibar Travel Weather Guide

Zanzibar's tropical climate divides neatly into distinct travel windows. Based on recent historical data, February stands out as the sweet spot — the hottest highs (33.1°C) but relatively few rain days (11.4) — while July offers the coolest, driest-feeling conditions with highs of just 27.5°C and lows of 22.3°C, ideal for sightseeing and beach days without oppressive heat. The worst period is April, when the long rains peak at 26.8 rain days and temperatures dip to a humid 28.7°C high. Practically, this means booking February for sun-soaked beach holidays and July for comfortable exploration, while avoiding April unless you don't mind frequent downpours.

Practical Tips

  • Best overall window: July–August offers the most comfortable temperatures (highs 27–29°C) with fewer rain days — ideal for combining beach time with Stone Town and spice-tour exploration.
  • Pack for rain year-round: Every month averages 11+ rain days, so bring a light rain jacket, quick-dry clothing, and waterproof bags for electronics regardless of season.
  • Avoid April if possible: With 26.8 rain days, the long rains can disrupt ferries, diving trips, and beach plans — only choose it for deep discounts and indoor-focused travel.
  • Beat the heat: January–March highs exceed 32°C; schedule outdoor activities for early morning and late afternoon, and stay hydrated.
  • Book ahead for peak season: December–February and July–August see high crowds and prices; reserve accommodation and excursions well in advance.
