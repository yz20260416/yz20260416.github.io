Best Time to Visit Zhangjiajie

Zhangjiajie is a prefecture-level city in northwestern Hunan Province, home to the Zhangjiajie National Forest Park within the Wulingyuan Scenic Area, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1992. Visit in spring or autumn for mild weather and clear views of the sandstone pillars, and allow several days to explore the park's trails and cable cars.

Local Character

Zhangjiajie was known as Dayong until 1994, when the city adopted the name of the small village inside the national forest park to raise its profile after the 1992 UNESCO designation — a visitor can still find the original village of Zhangjiajie within the park bounds, which explains the name's layered history. The region's deep past is unusually well documented: in 1986 the Academy of Chinese Social Science unearthed 108 stoneware articles in Cili County, and in 1988 the Hunan Archaeological Institute found further stone tools in Sangzhi County. These finds, plus ten-thousand-year-old pottery from Cili and a uniquely designed black clay pot from Sangzhi, show that local stonework and pottery-firing were among the most advanced in China at the time. A visitor should care because this predates most famous Chinese archaeological sites and reframes the area as an early cultural center, not merely a scenic backdrop. The terrain that preserved these relics also isolated the region: difficult cultivation and poor land and river transport led outside chroniclers to label it the "Land of the Savage Southern Minority" and the home of the "Wuling Rude People" and "Tujia Rude People" — discriminatory labels that reveal how geography shaped outside perceptions of the local Tujia culture.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Zhangjiajie National Forest Park — China's first national forest park, named after a village within its bounds and famed for its towering sandstone pillars.
  2. Wulingyuan Scenic Area — The UNESCO World Heritage Site encompassing the forest park and surrounding quartz-sandstone landscape.
  3. Lishui River — Known as Zhangjiajie's mother river, with banks settled since the Stone Age and now a scenic corridor through the city.
  4. Cili County — Site of 1986 Stone Age stoneware discoveries and of more than 20 pottery-firing relics dating back ten thousand years.
  5. Sangzhi County — Where archaeologists found stone tools and a uniquely designed black clay pot estimated at ten thousand years old.

FAQ

When was Zhangjiajie renamed?
The city was previously called Dayong and adopted the name Zhangjiajie in 1994, after the national forest park and its 1992 UNESCO designation.
How old is human settlement in the area?
Settlement dates back roughly 100,000 years, with Stone Age relics found in Cili and Sangzhi counties and a recorded history from 221 BC.
What is the Lishui River?
It is Zhangjiajie's mother river, along whose banks people have lived since the Stone Age.
