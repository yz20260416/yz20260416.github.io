Cape Town 3-Day Itinerary

Cape Town is one of the world's most spectacular cities, wedged between the Atlantic Ocean and the iconic Table Mountain. With three days, you can experience its dramatic scenery, rich history, world-class food and wine, and beautiful beaches — though you'll likely wish you had more time. This itinerary balances the must-see highlights with local favorites, structured by day and neighborhood to minimize travel time. It's designed for first-time visitors and assumes you have a rental car or use ride-hailing apps (Uber and Bolt are widely available and affordable). Book Table Mountain, Robben Island, and wine estate tastings in advance, especially in peak season (November–March).

Day 1: Table Mountain, City Bowl & V&A Waterfront

TimeActivityNotes
8:00 AM Breakfast at Truth Coffee Roasting (36 Buitenkant St) Steampunk-themed café famous for its house-roasted beans — a great start in the City Bowl.
9:00 AM Table Mountain via the Aerial Cableway Go early to beat crowds and clouds. Check the cableway's live status before heading up — it closes in high wind. Alternatively, hike Platteklip Gorge (2–3 hrs, strenuous).
12:30 PM Lunch at Kloof Street House (30 Kloof St) Charming Victorian house with a garden; excellent burgers and salads.
2:00 PM Company's Garden & Iziko South African Museum Stroll the historic gardens, feed the squirrels, and see the museum's natural history collections.
4:00 PM Bo-Kaap neighborhood walk Colorful Cape Malay quarter; visit the Bo-Kaap Museum and sample koeksisters. Be respectful — people live here.
6:30 PM Sunset at Signal Hill Panoramic views over the city and harbor; popular with paragliders.
8:00 PM Dinner at V&A Waterfront — try Baía (seafood) or Harbour House Book ahead for waterfront tables. Stroll the promenade afterward.

Day 2: Cape Peninsula — Cape Point & Boulders Beach

TimeActivityNotes
7:30 AM Depart city; coffee stop at Ou Meul Bakkery (Muizenberg) Start early — the peninsula loop is a full day (approx. 200 km round trip).
9:00 AM Chapman's Peak Drive One of the world's most scenic coastal roads (toll road). Stop at the lookout points.
10:00 AM Cape of Good Hope & Cape Point (Table Mountain National Park) Walk to the lighthouse, spot baboons (keep food hidden — do not feed them), and photograph the southwestern-most point of Africa.
1:00 PM Lunch at Two Oceans Restaurant (Cape Point) or Seaforth Restaurant (Simon's Town) Seafood with ocean views; reserve in peak season.
2:30 PM Boulders Beach penguin colony, Simon's Town African penguins up close via boardwalks; swim at the sheltered beach. Entry fee applies.
4:30 PM Return via Kalk Bay — browse antique shops and the harbor Optional stop; great for coffee and quirky boutiques.
7:30 PM Dinner at The Pot Luck Club (Woodstock) or Chef's Warehouse Both are celebrated for creative small plates; book well in advance.

Day 3: Winelands & Kirstenbosch

TimeActivityNotes
8:30 AM Breakfast at Jason Bakery (Bree St) or Giovanni's Deli (Green Point) Grab pastries and coffee before heading out.
9:30 AM Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden UNESCO-listed garden; walk the Boomslang canopy walkway. Allow 1.5–2 hours.
11:30 AM Drive to Stellenbosch (approx. 45 min) Historic university town with Cape Dutch architecture.
12:30 PM Lunch at Delaire Graff Estate or Tokara Both offer stunning valley views and refined menus; reservations essential.
2:00 PM Wine tastings: Spier, Boschendal, or Vergelegen Choose 2–3 estates max. Designate a driver or book a private wine tour — do not drink and drive.
5:00 PM Return to Cape Town via the N1 Optional sunset stop at Bloubergstrand for the classic Table
