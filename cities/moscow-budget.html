```html Moscow Travel Cost Guide

Moscow is one of Europe's most fascinating capitals — a city of onion-domed cathedrals, Soviet-era monuments, world-class museums, and a metro system that doubles as an underground art gallery. It also has a reputation for being expensive, but that reputation is only partly deserved. With careful planning, Moscow can be surprisingly affordable, and even mid-range travelers can enjoy a rich, comfortable visit. This guide breaks down realistic daily costs across three travel styles — budget, mid-range, and luxury — covering accommodation, food, transport, and activities, so you can plan your trip with confidence.

Currency note: Prices are shown in Russian rubles (RUB) with approximate US dollar equivalents. Exchange rates fluctuate — at the time of writing, roughly 1 USD ≈ 90–95 RUB. Always check current rates before you travel, and carry some cash for small vendors, though cards are widely accepted in Moscow.
