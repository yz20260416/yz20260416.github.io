Best Time to Visit Maldives

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are April and May. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

The Maldives stays warm year-round, with average highs of 28–29°C and lows rarely dipping below 26°C, so the real variable is rain. February stands out as the driest month, averaging just 6.6 rain days, making it the best bet for uninterrupted beach and dive time. May is the wettest, with around 21 rain days, so expect frequent short downpours and plan flexible indoor options. Note that even "dry" months like January and March still see roughly 13–14 rain days, so brief showers are always possible. The data below shows that no month is truly rain-free, but February and June offer the most reliable sunshine windows.

Practical tips

  • Treat every month as warm: highs sit between 28.1°C and 29.4°C, so pack light, breathable clothing and strong reef-safe sunscreen rather than cold-weather layers.
  • Rain in the Maldives usually arrives as short, intense bursts rather than all-day washouts — carry a compact rain jacket and keep excursions flexible.
  • Target February (6.6 rain days) for the most reliable sunshine, and consider June (10.4 rain days) as an underrated value option.
  • Avoid May (21 rain days) and October–November (18+ rain days) if dry weather is your priority, unless you're chasing lower prices or surf.
  • Book peak-season trips (December–February) months in advance, as high crowds coincide with the driest and most popular conditions.
