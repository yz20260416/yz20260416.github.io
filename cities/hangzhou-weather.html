Hangzhou Weather Guide: When to Visit

Hangzhou's climate data tells a clear story: the sweet spot for travelers is October and November, when highs sit at a comfortable 24.1°C and 18.7°C, lows stay mild, and rain days drop to 10 and 8.8 respectively. By contrast, June is the wettest month with 19.2 rain days, and July–August are punishingly hot, with highs of 34–34.8°C and lows barely dipping below 26°C. Practically, this means planning outdoor sightseeing around West Lake and the tea hills for autumn, while summer visits should be built around early mornings, shade, and indoor attractions. Winter is cold but relatively dry, and spring is pleasant but frequently wet.

Practical tips

  • Target October–November for the best combination of comfortable temperatures and fewer rain days; book hotels early since crowds are high in October.
  • Avoid June if you dislike rain—it averages 19.2 rain days—and treat July–August with caution due to highs of 34°C+ and high humidity.
  • Always carry a compact umbrella or rain jacket between February and June, when rain days consistently exceed 12 per month.
  • In summer, schedule outdoor sightseeing before 10 a.m. or after 4 p.m. and keep indoor options like museums and tea houses as backups.
  • Winter (December–January) is cold but relatively dry, so pack warm layers and plan around shorter daylight hours.
