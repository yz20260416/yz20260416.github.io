```html Beijing 3-Day Itinerary — Travel Guide

Beijing packs five centuries of imperial history, world-class museums, and one of the planet's most exciting food scenes into a single sprawling city. Three days is enough to see the headline sights — the Forbidden City, the Great Wall, the Temple of Heaven — without spending your whole trip in transit. This itinerary is built around a simple logic: Day 1 for the imperial core, Day 2 for the Great Wall plus modern Beijing, Day 3 for the temples, hutongs, and food. Each day clusters sights by neighborhood so you spend more time exploring and less time on the subway. Restaurant picks are a mix of iconic institutions and reliable local favorites, with notes on what to order.

Best seasons: April–May and September–October (mild weather, clear skies). Book Forbidden City and Great Wall tickets online in advance — both sell out, especially on weekends and Chinese public holidays.

Day 1 — Imperial Beijing: Tiananmen, Forbidden City & Jingshan

Start with the big one. Book your Forbidden City slot for the earliest available entry (8:30 a.m.) and arrive at Tiananmen security 20–30 minutes early — lines build fast.

TimeActivityNotes
8:00 Arrive at Tiananmen Square Bring your passport — it's checked at security. The square is free; the Mao Mausoleum (if open) requires a separate timed reservation.
8:30–12:30 Forbidden City (Palace Museum) Enter via Meridian Gate (south), exit via Gate of Divine Prowess (north). Allow 3–4 hours. Rent the audio guide or book a licensed guide; the complex is 72 hectares. Don't miss the Hall of Supreme Harmony, the Clock & Watch Gallery, and the Imperial Garden.
12:30–13:30 Lunch near Jingshan Try Huangcheng Lao Ma (黄城老马) for Beijing-style noodles, or grab dumplings at any small shop on Jingshan West Street.
13:30–14:30 Jingshan Park Climb to the Wanchun Pavilion for the classic panoramic view over the Forbidden City's golden roofs. Small entry fee.
14:45–16:30 Beihai Park Walk south from Jingshan to Beihai's north gate. Circle the lake, see the White Dagoba, and watch locals dance and play cards. Optional: rent a pedal boat.
17:00–18:00 Rest / hotel break You'll have walked 12–15 km. Recharge before dinner.
