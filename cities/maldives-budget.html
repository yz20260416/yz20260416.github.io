Maldives Travel Cost Guide

The Maldives is famously a bucket-list destination, but it's also widely perceived as one of the world's most expensive travel spots. The good news? That reputation is only half the story. While a private overwater villa at a five-star resort can easily exceed $1,500 per night, the Maldives has become increasingly accessible thanks to local guesthouses on inhabited islands, public ferries, and a growing mid-range market. Whether you're planning a once-in-a-lifetime luxury honeymoon or a budget-conscious island-hopping adventure, this guide breaks down realistic costs across accommodation, food, transport, and activities so you can plan with confidence.

Detailed Breakdown by Travel Style

🌴 Budget Traveler — $80–$195 per day

Accommodation ($40–$90/night): Local guesthouses on inhabited islands (e.g., Maafushi, Dhigurah, Fulidhoo, Thoddoo) are the budget traveler's secret. Expect clean, air-conditioned rooms with breakfast included. Dorm beds can start around $25–$35.

Food ($15–$30/day): Local cafés ("hotels" in Maldivian English) serve rice, curry, roshi, and fish for $3–$8 per meal. A "short eats" snack costs $1–$2. Alcohol is not available on local islands, but that keeps costs down.

Transport ($5–$25/day): Public ferries between local islands cost $2–$5 per leg. Speedboats cost $25–$50 per leg. Budget travelers should plan routes around the public ferry schedule (not daily).

Activities ($20–$50/day): Snorkeling trips ($25–$40), sandbank visits ($15–$30), and local island tours ($10–$20). Many guesthouses rent snorkel gear for $5–$10/day.

🏝️ Mid-Range Traveler — $270–$630 per day

Accommodation ($150–$350/night): Options include upscale guesthouses, boutique hotels on local islands, and entry-level resort rooms (often on the "garden view" side). Some 4-star resorts offer half-board packages that improve value.

Food ($40–$80/day): Mix of local cafés and resort/guesthouse dining. A resort buffet dinner runs $30–$50; à la carte mains $15–$30. Sunset cocktails $10–$15.

Transport ($30–$80/day): Domestic flights ($150–$250 one-way) or speedboat transfers ($50–$150 one-way) are common. Many resorts include transfers in packages — always check.

Activities ($50–$120/day): Diving (2-tank dive ~$80–$120), dolphin cruises ($40–$60), sunset fishing ($40–$70), and spa treatments ($60–$120).
