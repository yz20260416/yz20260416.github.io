Best Time to Visit Melbourne

Melbourne is Australia's cultural capital, known for its laneway street art, world-class coffee, major sporting events and vibrant food scene. Visit in autumn (March–May) or spring (September–November) for mild weather and lively festivals, though the city's famous 'four seasons in one day' means layers are always wise.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Royal Botanic Gardens — Sprawling heritage gardens beside the Yarra River offering lakes, lawns and a children's garden.
  2. National Gallery of Victoria — Australia's oldest public art museum, with a vast collection and the famous stained-glass ceiling.
  3. Queen Victoria Market — Historic open-air market selling fresh produce, deli goods and souvenirs since 1878.
  4. Federation Square — The city's civic heart, home to major museums, galleries and riverside events.
  5. Great Ocean Road — A scenic coastal drive west of Melbourne famed for the Twelve Apostles rock formations.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Australian Dollar (AUD, A$)
  • Language: English
  • Time Zone: AEST (UTC+10), AEDT (UTC+11) in summer
  • Visa: Most international visitors need an Electronic Travel Authority or visa, easily applied for online before travel.

FAQ

How do I get around Melbourne?
The CBD is walkable and served by an extensive tram network, including a free tram zone in the city centre.
What is Melbourne famous for?
Coffee culture, laneway street art, Australian Rules football, major events like the Australian Open, and diverse dining.
When is the best time to visit Melbourne?
March to May and September to November offer mild weather, fewer crowds than summer and plenty of festivals.
