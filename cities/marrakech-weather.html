Marrakech Weather by Month

Marrakech enjoys a semi-arid climate with long, hot summers and mild, dry winters, making it a year-round destination with distinctly different characters each season. Sitting at the foot of the Atlas Mountains, the city experiences dramatic temperature swings between day and night — especially in winter and spring — and almost no rain from June through September. Understanding the monthly weather patterns helps travelers choose the right season for sightseeing, souk shopping, pool time, or mountain excursions, and pack accordingly.

Note: Figures are long-term averages and can vary year to year. Marrakech's desert-influenced climate means daytime sun can feel much stronger than the air temperature suggests, and nighttime temperatures can drop sharply, particularly from November to March.

Best Months to Visit

  • March–May (spring): Arguably the best overall window — warm, sunny days (23–29°C / 73–84°F), comfortable evenings, and blooming gardens. Ideal for walking the medina and day trips to the Atlas Mountains.
  • October–November (autumn): Another excellent period with pleasant temperatures (22–28°C / 72–82°F) after the summer heat. October in particular is a traveler favorite.
  • June–September (summer): Very hot (often 35–40°C+ / 95–104°F+). Best for travelers who enjoy pool time and early-morning or late-evening exploration; expect higher prices during European school holidays.
  • December–February (winter): Mild, sunny days but genuinely cold nights (down to ~5°C / 41°F). A great low-season choice for budget travelers and crowd-free sightseeing, provided you pack warm layers.

Practical Tips

  • Layer your clothing year-round: Even in summer, evenings are comfortable, and in winter, days can be sunny and warm while nights are cold. A light jacket or sweater is always useful.
  • Respect the heat in summer: Schedule medina walks and outdoor sights for early morning or after 5 p.m.; drink plenty of water; wear a hat and sunglasses; use high-SPF sunscreen.
  • Rain is rare but real: Most rain falls between November and March in short bursts. A compact umbrella or light rain jacket is worth packing in winter and early spring.
  • Watch for dust and sandstorms: Occasionally in spring and summer, hot, dusty winds (the chergui) can blow in from the desert, reducing visibility and raising temperatures — plan indoor activities (museums, hammams, souks) as backup.
  • Mountain day trips run cooler: If visiting the Atlas Mountains or Ourika Valley, expect temperatures 5–10°C lower than Marrakech — bring a warm layer even in summer.
  • Book ahead in peak seasons: Spring (March–May) and autumn (October–November) are the busiest and most pleasant periods; reserve riads and popular restaurants well in advance.
  • Ramadan considerations: The Islamic holy month shifts each year; daytime opening hours for some shops and restaurants change, but the city takes on a lively evening atmosphere — worth checking dates if timing matters to you.
