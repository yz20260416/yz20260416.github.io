Marrakech Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Travel Planning

Marrakech's climate splits sharply between a mild, occasionally wet winter and a fiercely hot, bone-dry summer. The data shows April and October as the sweet spots: highs of 27.9°C and 30.9°C respectively, with lows near 13-16°C and only around 3 rainy days each — ideal for walking the medina and exploring courtyards without heat stress. July and August are the opposite extreme, with average highs of 40.1°C and 39.2°C and essentially zero rain, meaning sightseeing must shift to early mornings and evenings. Winter (December-February) is comfortable by day (21-22°C) but cool at night, with December the wettest month at 5.8 rain days.

Practical tips

  • Time your visit: April and October offer the best balance of warm days and low rainfall; avoid July-August unless you can tolerate 40°C highs.
  • Dress in layers: Even in hot months, desert-influenced nights drop sharply — winter lows hover around 7-8°C.
  • Plan around the heat: In June-September, schedule outdoor sightseeing before 10am and after 5pm, and keep midday for riads, museums or pools.
  • Carry rain protection in winter: December and March see the most rain days (5.8 and 5.6), so a compact waterproof is worthwhile.
  • Book ahead in peak season: October and the summer months draw the biggest crowds — reserve riads and popular restaurants early.
