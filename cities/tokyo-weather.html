Tokyo Weather by Month

Tokyo has a humid subtropical climate with four distinct seasons. Summers are hot and muggy, winters are cool and dry, and the rainy season (tsuyu) typically runs from early June to mid-July. Spring and autumn are the most comfortable periods, with mild temperatures and lower humidity.
