Tokyo Travel Weather Guide

Tokyo's weather data reveals a city of two distinct travel personalities. The sweet spot is late spring and autumn: May (highs 22.9°C, lows 14.4°C) and October (highs 22.1°C, lows 14.3°C) offer comfortable temperatures, though May carries a high 14.8 rain days, so pack a compact umbrella. The most challenging month is August, with punishing highs of 32.6°C and lows near 25°C — plan outdoor sightseeing for early morning and lean on air-conditioned museums and shopping. January is the coldest (lows near 0°C) but notably dry with just 4.4 rain days, making it a good value month for crisp, crowd-light sightseeing if you dress in layers.

Practical tips

  • Rain days exceed 9 in every month from March through October — a compact umbrella and water-resistant shoes are essentials for most of the year.
  • Book May and October trips furthest in advance: they combine the most comfortable temperatures (highs around 22–23°C) with manageable rain, so they fill up fast.
  • For August and July, schedule outdoor sightseeing before 10 a.m. or after 5 p.m. and build in air-conditioned breaks; heat and humidity are the main risks, not rain.
  • January and February are the driest and least crowded months — ideal if you don't mind lows near freezing, and a good window for lower hotel rates.
  • September sits in typhoon season; keep travel insurance and flexible bookings, and check forecasts daily during your trip.
