San Francisco Weather by Month

San Francisco's climate is famously mild and famously unpredictable. Thanks to its position on a narrow peninsula between the Pacific Ocean and San Francisco Bay, the city enjoys cool, wet winters and dry, cool-to-warm summers — with the famous summer fog ("Karl the Fog") rolling in through the Golden Gate most mornings. Mark Twain supposedly quipped that the coldest winter he ever spent was a summer in San Francisco, and there's truth to it: July and August are often cooler than May and October. Rainfall is concentrated from November through March, while the "summer" months are almost entirely dry. Use this month-by-month guide to plan your wardrobe and itinerary — and remember that neighborhoods vary wildly: the foggy Sunset District can be 10–15°F cooler than sunny Mission Dolores on the same afternoon.

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall: September & October. Warmest temperatures, clearest skies, and the fog finally relents — perfect for Golden Gate Bridge views, Alcatraz tours, and cable-car rides.
  • Best for budget & fewer crowds: January–March. Rain is frequent but rarely all-day, and hotel rates drop significantly. Bring rain gear and enjoy museums, restaurants, and indoor attractions.
  • Best for value with decent weather: April–May. Rain tapers off, days lengthen, and you'll beat the summer tourist surge.
  • Avoid if you want sunshine: June–August near the coast. The fog is thickest then; head inland (Mission, Noe Valley, or across the bay) for warmth.

Practical Tips

  • Dress in layers, always. A morning that starts foggy and 55°F can turn sunny and 70°F by afternoon — and back again. A packable windbreaker or light fleece is essential year-round.
  • Don't trust the calendar for summer. July evenings near the water can feel like 50°F with wind chill. Locals wear scarves in August.
  • Check microclimates before you go. The Sunset, Richmond, and Ocean Beach run 10–15°F cooler than the Mission or SoMa. Plan beach days for September–October.
  • Rainy-season strategy (Nov–Mar): Storms usually pass within a day or two. Build flexibility into your itinerary — save outdoor plans for clear windows and have museum, food, and shopping backups ready.
  • Book Alcatraz and popular restaurants well ahead during peak months (May–October), when demand is highest.
  • Fog is part of the experience. Morning fog often burns off by midday; if the bridge is socked in, try again in the afternoon or head to a sunny neighborhood.
  • UV can be deceptive. Even on cool, overcast days, sun exposure adds up — sunscreen is wise year-round.
