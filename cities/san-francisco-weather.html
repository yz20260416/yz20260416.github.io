San Francisco Travel Weather Guide

San Francisco's real 2021–2025 averages reveal a city of mild temperatures but wildly uneven rainfall. September is the statistical sweet spot: highs near 20.4°C, lows around 14.4°C, and only 1.2 rain days per month. December is the wettest and least forgiving month, averaging 12.8 rain days with highs of just 13.4°C. Practically, this means summer visitors should pack layers for cool, foggy evenings rather than expecting heat, while anyone travelling November through March should bring a waterproof jacket and build flexibility into outdoor plans. Spring and autumn offer the best balance of warmth and dry days.

Practical tips

  • Pack layers year-round: even warm months like August and September have lows around 14°C, and fog can chill the coast quickly.
  • Schedule outdoor highlights (Golden Gate, Alcatraz, coastal walks) for April–October, when rain days are lowest.
  • If visiting November–March, bring a waterproof jacket and shoes — over a third of days can see rain.
  • Book hotels and popular attractions early for June–September, when crowds peak despite modest temperatures.
  • Use rainy winter days for museums, food halls and indoor sights rather than cancelling plans.
