Three Days in Sanya: A Complete Itinerary

Sanya, on the southern tip of Hainan Island, is China's premier tropical beach destination, known for warm water year-round, palm-lined bays, and a growing food scene blending Hainanese, Cantonese, and Southeast Asian flavors. This three-day plan balances beach time, nature, culture, and local dining, with morning, afternoon, and evening schedules for each day.

Day 1: Yalong Bay and Tropical Forest

TimeActivity
MorningStart at Yalong Bay (亚龙湾), widely considered Sanya's best beach for swimming and snorkeling. Arrive early to claim a spot; the sand is fine and the water calm. Optional: snorkeling or a short catamaran trip.
AfternoonVisit the Yalong Bay Tropical Paradise Forest Park (亚龙湾热带天堂森林公园), a hilltop park with canopy walkways and panoramic bay views. Allow 2–3 hours; bring water and sun protection.
EveningDinner at a seafood restaurant near Yalong Bay, then a relaxed walk along the beach or a drink at a resort bar.

Dining: For lunch, try a beachside Hainanese chicken rice spot. For dinner, choose a seafood restaurant where you select live catch by weight — confirm prices before ordering.

Day 2: Wuzhizhou Island and Haitang Bay

TimeActivity
MorningTake an early ferry to Wuzhizhou Island (蜈支洲岛), a small island off Haitang Bay known for clear water, coral, and water sports (diving, jet skiing, parasailing). Book tickets in advance during peak season and arrive at the pier early to avoid queues.
AfternoonReturn to the mainland and relax at Haitang Bay (海棠湾), home to upscale resorts and the large Sanya International Duty Free City for shopping.
EveningDinner at a Haitang Bay resort restaurant or the duty-free complex food court; early night to recover from the sun.

Dining: Pack snacks or eat at the island's simple canteens at lunch. For dinner, Haitang Bay resorts offer reliable seafood buffets; the duty-free mall has varied casual options.

Day 3: Culture, City, and Luhuitou

TimeActivity
MorningVisit the Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone (南山文化旅游区), home to the 108-meter Guanyin statue and Buddhist temples set in landscaped grounds. Allow 3–4 hours; it is a large site, so wear comfortable shoes.
AfternoonExplore downtown Sanya: the First Market (第一市场) area for local snacks and produce, and Sanya Bay's waterfront promenade for a stroll.
EveningHike or take a taxi to Luhuitou Park (鹿回头公园) for sunset views over the city and Sanya Bay, then dinner at a downtown seafood or Hainanese restaurant.

Dining: Try Hainanese specialties such as Wenchang chicken, coconut rice, and coconut chicken hotpot (椰子鸡). At First Market, buy seafood and have a nearby restaurant cook it for a small fee — agree on the cooking charge first.

Practical Tips

  • Best time to visit: October to April is dry and comfortable; May–September is hot, humid, and the typhoon season (July–October).
  • Getting around: Taxis and ride-hailing apps are convenient; distances between bays are long, so budget travel time. The high-speed rail connects Sanya to Haikou and other Hainan cities.
  • Sun safety: The UV index is high year-round — use reef-safe sunscreen, hats, and rash guards, and reapply often.
  • Seafood pricing: Always confirm prices per weight before ordering at seafood markets and restaurants to avoid disputes.
  • Island trips: Book Wuzhizhou Island and Nanshan tickets online in advance during holidays; ferries can sell out.
  • Language: English is limited outside major resorts; translation apps and hotel concierges help with taxis and menus.
  • Cash and payments: Mobile payment (WeChat/Alipay) is dominant; carry some cash for small vendors and market stalls.
  • Dress: Swimwear is fine at beaches; cover up when visiting temples such as Nanshan.
