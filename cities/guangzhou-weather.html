Guangzhou Travel Weather Guide

Guangzhou's subtropical climate splits the year into a mild, dry-ish cool season and a long, hot, soaking wet season. The data shows October and November are the sweet spot: highs of 29.1°C and 25.1°C with just 9.4 and 6.2 rain days, meaning you can sightsee all day with only occasional showers. By contrast, May through August is punishing — highs near 30–33°C combined with 21–27 rain days per month, so plan indoor activities, carry rain gear daily, and expect heavy afternoon downpours. January and December are the coolest and driest, ideal for walking the city comfortably, though evenings near 11–12°C call for a jacket.

Practical tips

  • Best window: October–December offers the most comfortable temperatures with the fewest rain days — prioritize these months for outdoor sightseeing.
  • Rain gear always: From March to September, carry a compact umbrella and quick-dry footwear; showers are frequent and often intense.
  • Beat the heat: In June–September, schedule outdoor activities for early morning, and keep indoor options (museums, malls, teahouses) for afternoons.
  • Typhoon awareness: July–September is typhoon season — check forecasts and keep travel plans flexible during this period.
  • Layer up in winter: January and December days are mild but nights drop to around 11–12°C, so bring a jacket and warm layers.
