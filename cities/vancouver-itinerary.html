Vancouver 3-Day Itinerary

Vancouver is a rare city where you can ski in the morning and kayak in the afternoon — or, more realistically, wander through a temperate rainforest, explore a world-class food scene, and watch the sun set over the Pacific, all in a single day. Nestled between the Coast Mountains and the Salish Sea, this West Coast gem rewards travelers who balance urban exploration with outdoor adventure. This 3-day itinerary is designed to help you experience the best of Vancouver — from Stanley Park's seawall to Granville Island's markets, from Gastown's cobblestones to Grouse Mountain's views — without feeling rushed. Each day includes a suggested schedule, key activities, and restaurant picks to keep you fueled and satisfied.

Day 1 — Downtown, Stanley Park & the Seawall

Start your trip with Vancouver's iconic waterfront and green spaces. This day is mostly walking and cycling, so wear comfortable shoes.

Time Activity Details & Tips
8:00 AM Breakfast at Café Medina Famous for Belgian waffles and lavender lattes. Arrive early — the line gets long after 9 AM. Café Medina, 780 Richards St
9:30 AM Stanley Park & Seawall Rent a bike from Spokes Bicycle Rentals near Denman St. Ride the 9 km Seawall loop, stopping at Prospect Point, Siwash Rock, and the Totem Poles at Brockton Point. Allow 2–3 hours.
12:30 PM Lunch at The Sandbar Seafood with a view on Granville Island. Try the salmon or fish tacos. The Sandbar, 1535 Johnston St
2:00 PM Granville Island Public Market Browse local produce, cheeses, and crafts. Grab a coffee at JJ Bean and walk the waterfront. Optional: take a short Aquabus ride to Yaletown.
4:00 PM Yaletown & False Creek Stroll through Yaletown's converted warehouse district. Walk the False Creek seawall toward Olympic Village. Great photo ops of the city skyline.
6:30 PM Dinner at Miku Aburi-style sushi with waterfront views near Canada Place. Reserve ahead. Miku, 200 Granville St #70
8:30 PM Evening walk at Canada Place See the sails of Canada Place lit up and enjoy views of Burrard Inlet and North Vancouver.
Day 2 — North Vancouver: Mountains, Suspension Bridge & Capilano

Head across the Lions Gate Bridge to North Vancouver for a day of nature and adrenaline. Book Capilano Suspension Bridge tickets online in advance to skip the line.

Time Activity Details & Tips
8:00 AM Breakfast at Jam Café Hearty brunch (try the chicken and waffles). Expect a wait on weekends.
