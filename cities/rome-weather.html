Rome's weather data reveals a city of two seasons. May is arguably the sweet spot: highs near 24°C and lows around 13°C make long days of walking and sightseeing genuinely comfortable. July is the toughest month, with average highs of 34°C and lows of 21.6°C, so plan outdoor sights for early morning and reserve midday for museums, churches, or shaded cafés. November is the wettest month, averaging 12.2 rain days, so pack a compact umbrella and keep indoor options ready. The shoulder months of April, May, September, and October offer the most balanced conditions overall.

Practical tips

  • Book May or September for the best balance of comfortable temperatures and manageable conditions.
  • Avoid midday outdoor sightseeing in July and August, when highs reach 33–34°C; use early mornings and evenings instead.
  • Carry a compact umbrella and waterproof layer from October through May, when rain days regularly exceed 9 per month.
  • July is the driest month (3.2 rain days), making it reliable for outdoor plans despite the heat.
  • November demands the most rain preparation (12.2 rain days); build flexibility into your itinerary.
