Shangri-La Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Temperatures, Rainfall & Best Time to Visit

Shangri-La sits at roughly 3,200 metres on the Tibetan Plateau, so its weather is defined by cool-to-cold temperatures year-round and a sharply seasonal monsoon. The data shows the clearest, driest window is November (just 2.8 rain days, highs near 10°C) and December (1.2 rain days), while the wettest stretch is July with 27.2 rain days — meaning nearly every day brings showers and cloud that can hide mountain views. Practically, travellers chasing clear skies and trekking conditions should aim for late autumn; those visiting in summer must pack waterproofs and build in flexibility for washed-out days.

Practical tips

  • Pack for cold nights year-round: lows fall below freezing from November through March and even summer nights hover around 9–10°C, so warm layers and a good sleeping bag are essential.
  • Time your visit for clear skies: November and December offer the fewest rain days (2.8 and 1.2) and the best chance of mountain views — book these for photography and trekking.
  • Avoid or waterproof July: with 27.2 rain days, July is the least reliable month; if you must go in summer, bring rain jackets, dry bags and flexible itineraries.
  • Watch altitude as much as weather: at ~3,200 m, acclimatise gradually and keep exertion moderate, especially on cold, low-oxygen winter mornings.
  • Layer for big day-night swings: daily ranges of 10–15°C are common, so dress in removable layers and carry sun protection for bright high-altitude days.
