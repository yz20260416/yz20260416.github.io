Paris 3-Day Itinerary

Three days in Paris is enough to fall in love with the city — if you plan smartly. This itinerary balances iconic landmarks with neighborhood charm, world-class museums with leisurely café time, and famous sights with local favorites. Each day clusters attractions by arrondissement to minimize transit, and every day includes restaurant recommendations ranging from budget-friendly to splurge-worthy. Book major attractions (Eiffel Tower summit, Louvre, Versailles) online in advance — especially in peak season — and wear comfortable shoes, because Paris is best explored on foot.

Day 1 — Icons & the Seine Central Paris

Start with the essentials: the Eiffel Tower, the Seine, and the historic heart of the city. This day is walkable and photogenic, with plenty of café stops.

Time Activity Notes
8:30 AM Breakfast at a local boulangerie Croissant + café crème; try Poilâne or any neighborhood bakery.
9:30 AM – 12:00 PM Eiffel Tower (Champ de Mars) Book summit tickets online weeks ahead. Arrive early to beat crowds. Walk the Champ de Mars and Trocadéro for photos.
12:15 PM Lunch near Rue Cler Market street with cafés and cheese shops — great for a casual plat du jour.
1:30 – 3:00 PM Musée d'Orsay Impressionist masterpieces in a converted Beaux-Arts railway station. Closed Mondays.
3:15 – 4:30 PM Walk the Seine & Tuileries Garden Cross Pont Royal toward the Louvre exterior and Tuileries; browse the riverside bouquinistes.
4:30 – 6:00 PM Louvre (exterior & courtyard) or quick visit If you want inside access, pre-book a timed slot; otherwise enjoy the pyramid and Carrousel.
6:30 PM Seine river cruise ~1 hour at sunset; Bateaux Mouches or Vedettes du Pont Neuf. Book online.
8:00 PM Dinner See restaurant picks below.

Restaurant Recommendations — Day 1

  • Le Petit Cler (7th) — Cozy bistro near Rue Cler; classic French plates, moderate prices.
  • Café Constant (7th) — Reliable neighborhood bistro with a good prix-fixe menu.
  • Les Cocottes (7th) — Christian Constant's casual spot; famous cocottes (cast-iron pot dishes).
  • Septime (11th, splurge) — Michelin-starred modern French; reserve well in advance. (Worth the taxi if you want a special meal.)

Day 2 — Museums, Montmartre & Local Life Right Bank

Dive into art and bohemian Paris. Morning at the Louvre (or a smaller museum if you prefer), afternoon in Montmartre, and evening in a lively neighborhood like Le Marais or Canal Saint-Martin.
