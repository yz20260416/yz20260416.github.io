Phuket Travel Budget Guide: Backpacker, Mid-Range & Luxury Costs

Phuket is Thailand's largest island and one of Southeast Asia's most popular destinations, offering everything from dormitory beds on Bangla Road to private pool villas in Cape Panwa. Costs vary dramatically by travel style and location—beachfront areas like Patong and Kata command higher prices than Phuket Town or the quieter north. This guide breaks down realistic daily budgets across three travel styles, using Thai baht (THB) with approximate US dollar equivalents (1 USD ≈ 36 THB). All figures are per person, per day, unless noted.

Accommodation Details

  • Backpacker: Dormitory beds in hostels (Patong, Phuket Town) run 300–600 THB per night. Private fan rooms in guesthouses start around 500–800 THB; air-conditioned budget rooms 800–1,200 THB.
  • Mid-Range: Three- to four-star hotels and resorts (Kata, Karon, Kamala) typically 1,500–3,500 THB per night, with pools and breakfast included at the lower end.
  • Luxury: Five-star resorts and pool villas (Cape Panwa, Surin, Bang Tao) range from 8,000 THB for standard five-star rooms to 30,000+ THB for private villas. High season (November–February) pushes rates up 30–50%.

Food Costs

  • Backpacker: Street food (pad thai, som tam, grilled chicken) 50–80 THB per dish; local restaurants 80–150 THB; convenience store meals under 100 THB.
  • Mid-Range: Sit-down Thai restaurants 150–350 THB per main; Western meals 250–450 THB; a beer at a bar 80–150 THB.
  • Luxury: Resort dining and fine-dining seafood 800–2,500 THB per main; beach club meals and cocktails 400–700 THB each.

Transport Costs

  • Local buses (songthaew): 30–50 THB per trip between beaches and Phuket Town.
  • Airport transfer: Public bus to Phuket Town ~100 THB; shared minivan 150–200 THB; Grab/taxi 600–1,000 THB to Patong.
  • Scooter rental: 250–400 THB per day (international driving permit and helmet required; insurance recommended).
  • Private car with driver: 1,500–2,500 THB per day.

Activity Costs

  • Island-hopping day tours (Phi Phi, Similan): 1,000–2,500 THB including lunch and transfers.
  • Scuba diving: 2,500–3,500 THB per dive trip; PADI Open Water course 10,000–15,000 THB.
  • Muay Thai tickets: 1,000–2,000 THB (Patong stadium); cheaper at local stadiums.
  • Big Buddha and temples: Free entry (donations welcome).
  • Cabaret shows and nightlife: 500–1,200 THB for shows; drinks 100–300 THB.

Practical Tips

  • Seasonality matters: High season (November–February) raises accommodation 30–50%; low season (May–October) brings discounts but also monsoon rain and rougher seas for boat tours.
  • Carry cash: Many street vendors, songthaews, and small shops are cash-only. ATMs charge a 220 THB foreign card fee—withdraw larger amounts less often.
  • Negotiate songthaew fares before boarding, especially at night, and confirm whether the price is per person or per vehicle.
  • Book tours locally in Phuket Town or at beach-side agencies for better prices than hotel desks—but check reviews and insurance coverage.
  • Eat where locals eat: Markets and small shophouses offer the best value and freshest food.
  • Budget a buffer: Add 10–15% to your daily estimate for SIM cards, laundry, and incidental costs.
