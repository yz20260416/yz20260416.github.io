Mexico City Weather by Month

Mexico City sits at an elevation of roughly 2,240 meters (7,350 feet) in a highland valley, giving it a spring-like climate year-round. Temperatures are remarkably stable—daytime highs hover between 21°C and 26°C (70–79°F) in every month—but the city has two distinct seasons: a dry season from roughly November through April and a wet season from May through October, when afternoon thunderstorms are common. Because of the altitude, nights are cool to cold all year, and mornings can be crisp. The following guide breaks down typical conditions month by month to help you plan your visit.

Month Avg. High Avg. Low Rainfall Rainy Days Notes
January 21°C (70°F) 6°C (43°F) ~10 mm 2–3 Dry, sunny days; cold nights. Peak dry season.
February 22°C (72°F) 7°C (45°F) ~10 mm 2–3 Warm afternoons, chilly evenings; very little rain.
March 24°C (75°F) 9°C (48°F) ~15 mm 3–4 Warming up; hazy skies possible as dry season peaks.
April 25°C (77°F) 11°C (52°F) ~25 mm 5–6 Hottest afternoons of the year; occasional early showers.
May 25°C (77°F) 12°C (54°F) ~60 mm 9–11 Wet season begins; afternoon storms build late in the month.
June 24°C (75°F) 13°C (55°F) ~130 mm 15–17 Frequent afternoon and evening thunderstorms.
July 23°C (73°F) 12°C (54°F) ~150 mm 17–19 Wettest month; typically sunny mornings, stormy afternoons.
August 23°C (73°F) 12°C (54°F) ~140 mm 16–18 Similar to July; lush green parks and hillsides.
September 23°C (73°F) 12°C (54°F) ~130 mm 14–16 Rain continues; one of the cloudiest months.
October 23°C (73°F) 10°C (50°F) ~60 mm 8–10 Rains taper off; pleasant, increasingly clear days.
November 22°C (72°F) 8°C (46°F) ~20 mm 3–4 Dry season returns; crisp, clear weather.
December 21°C (70°F) 6°C (43°F) ~10 mm 2–3 Cool nights, sunny days; festive holiday atmosphere.

Figures are approximate long-term averages; actual conditions vary year to year. Rainfall totals represent monthly accumulation, and "rainy days" counts days with measurable precipitation.

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall: March, April, October, and November. These months combine warm, mostly dry days with manageable crowds and comfortable conditions for sightseeing.
  • Best for clear skies and dry weather: December through April. Expect brilliant sunshine by day and cold nights—pack layers.
  • Best for budget travelers and lush scenery: June through September, the heart of the wet season. Mornings are often clear, rains usually arrive mid-afternoon and pass quickly, and hotel rates dip.
  • Busiest periods to avoid if you dislike crowds: Semana Santa (Easter week, late March or April), the December holidays, and Day of the Dead (late October/early November), when the city is vibrant but packed.

Practical Tips

  • Layer your clothing. Days can feel warm and sunny while nights drop to single digits Celsius (40s°F). A light jacket or sweater is essential year-round, and a warmer coat helps in December and January.
  • Plan around afternoon rain in the wet season. Schedule outdoor sights, markets, and pyramids for the morning, and keep museums, galleries, and cafés as afternoon backups from May to October.
  • Carry rain gear from June to September. A compact umbrella or rain jacket is worth the space; storms can be sudden and intense.
  • Use sun protection year-round. The high altitude means stronger UV exposure even on cool or hazy days.
  • Expect cool mornings and evenings. Heating is uncommon in many buildings, so warm sleepwear and layers are useful in winter.
  • Check air quality. The valley can trap pollution, especially in the dry season (roughly March–May). Sensitive travelers should monitor air quality indexes and plan strenuous activities accordingly.
  • Book ahead during peak dates. Reserve accommodations and popular attractions (such as the Anthropology Museum and Frida Kahlo's Casa Azul) well in advance for holiday periods and major festivals.
