Mexico City's high elevation keeps temperatures mild year-round, but rainfall splits the year sharply in two. The dry season from November through April is reliably sunny, and March and April stand out as the best months: highs near 28°C, lows around 10–12°C, and only 2–5 rain days per month. The wet season peaks in July, August and September, when afternoon storms hit on 25–28 days a month, so plan mornings outdoors and carry rain gear. Practically, this means booking spring for sightseeing and hiking, or accepting daily afternoon downpours if you visit in late summer.

Practical tips

  • Book March or April for the best combination of warm highs (28°C) and minimal rain (2–5 wet days).
  • Avoid August if you dislike rain: it averages 27.8 wet days, with storms typically arriving mid-to-late afternoon.
  • During the wet season (May–October), schedule outdoor sights and day trips for the morning.
  • Bring layers year-round: nights drop to 7–13°C even when daytime highs reach the high 20s.
  • Pack a compact rain jacket and quick-dry shoes for any visit between May and October.
