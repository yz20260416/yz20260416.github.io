Dubai Travel Weather Guide

Dubai's weather data tells a clear story: the best months to visit are December through March, when highs sit comfortably between 24°C and 31°C and lows are a pleasant 14–20°C. January is especially appealing, averaging just 1.8 rain days. The worst period is July and August, when average highs reach 42.5–43°C and lows stay near 30°C — meaning even nights offer little relief, and outdoor sightseeing becomes genuinely difficult. Rainfall is negligible year-round (every month averages under 2 rain days), so you can plan outdoor activities without much concern for rain, but summer travel demands early starts, indoor plans, and constant hydration.

Practical tips

  • Book outdoor activities (desert safaris, beach days, walking tours) between November and March, when highs stay below about 32°C.
  • If visiting June–September, schedule sightseeing before 9am or after sunset and plan around air-conditioned malls, museums, and indoor attractions.
  • Rain is rare all year (every month averages under 2 rain days), so an umbrella is optional — but winter visits may see the occasional shower in January or December.
  • Carry water constantly and use high-SPF sunscreen year-round; UV exposure is intense even in the cooler months.
  • Expect higher prices and busier hotels in December–March peak season, and book accommodations well in advance.
