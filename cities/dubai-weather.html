Dubai Weather by Month

Dubai has a hot desert climate, which means travelers can expect plenty of sunshine year-round, very little rain, and temperatures that swing dramatically between summer and winter. Understanding the monthly weather patterns is essential for planning a comfortable trip—whether you're visiting for beach time, desert safaris, shopping festivals, or sightseeing. The best months to visit Dubai are generally November through March, when temperatures are mild and outdoor activities are most enjoyable. Summer (June–September) is intensely hot and humid, but it also brings lower hotel rates and indoor attractions. Below is a detailed month-by-month breakdown of temperatures, rainfall, and travel conditions.

Monthly Weather Overview

August 41°C / 106°F 29°C / 84°F ~1 mm Similar to July; very hot. Evening activities and water parks are popular.

November 30°C / 86°F 19°C / 66°F ~3 mm Excellent weather; one of the best months to visit. Peak tourist season begins.
December 26°C / 79°F 16°C / 61°F ~16 mm Cool and festive; popular for holiday travel. Book accommodations early.

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall: November, December, January, February, and March — mild temperatures, low humidity, and ideal conditions for beaches, desert safaris, and sightseeing.
  • Best for budget travelers: June through September — extreme heat but significantly lower hotel rates and fewer crowds at indoor attractions.
  • Best for beach lovers: April, May, October, and November — warm sea temperatures with manageable air heat.
  • Busiest season: December through March, coinciding with holidays, the Dubai Shopping Festival (typically January), and pleasant weather.

Practical Tips

  • Dress for the season: Light, breathable clothing in summer; layers and a light jacket for cooler winter evenings.
  • Hydrate constantly: Drink plenty of water year-round, especially in summer when dehydration risk is high.
  • Plan around the heat: In summer months, schedule outdoor activities for early morning or after sunset, and spend midday hours in malls, museums, or hotels.
  • Sun protection: Use high-SPF sunscreen, sunglasses, and hats—UV levels are strong even in winter.
  • Rain is rare: Annual rainfall averages only about 100 mm, mostly between December and March. Brief showers can cause localized flooding, so allow extra travel time on rainy days.
  • Book early in peak season: November–March sees high demand for hotels and attractions; reserve in advance for better rates and availability.
  • Check Ramadan dates: If traveling during Ramadan, note adjusted opening hours and dining norms; dates shift each year on the Islamic calendar.
  • Humidity awareness: Summer humidity can make temperatures feel even hotter; choose accommodations with reliable air conditioning and consider indoor attractions during peak afternoon hours.
