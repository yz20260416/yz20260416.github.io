Prague 3-Day Itinerary

Prague, the "City of a Hundred Spires," is a compact yet endlessly rewarding destination where Gothic cathedrals, Baroque palaces, and cobblestone lanes sit side by side with a thriving café culture and world-class beer. This three-day itinerary is designed to balance the essential sights—Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, and Old Town Square—with quieter neighborhoods, local food, and enough breathing room to enjoy the city at a relaxed pace. Each day is walkable, with public transport options noted for longer stretches.

Day 1: Old Town, Charles Bridge & the Jewish Quarter

Theme: Historic center orientation and riverside atmosphere.

Time Activity Notes & Restaurant Recommendations
8:30–9:00 Breakfast at a local café near Old Town Square Café Savoy (Vítězná 5) — elegant, historic café known for breakfast and pastries.
9:00–11:30 Old Town Square & Astronomical Clock Arrive early to beat crowds. Watch the clock's hourly apostles' procession (on the hour, 9:00–23:00). Visit the Church of Our Lady before Týn and the Jan Hus Memorial.
11:30–12:30 Walk to Charles Bridge via Celetná Street Browse shops and stop for a trdelník (chimney cake) if you like.
12:30–14:00 Lunch near the bridge Lokál Dlouhááá (Dlouhá 33) — excellent Czech pub food and fresh Pilsner; or Mlejnice (Kožná 14) for hearty traditional dishes.
14:00–15:30 Charles Bridge & Kampa Island Cross the bridge, admire the 30 statues, then descend to Kampa Park for riverside views and the Lennon Wall.
15:30–17:30 Jewish Quarter (Josefov) Visit the Old Jewish Cemetery, Spanish Synagogue, and Maisel Synagogue. A combined ticket covers several sites.
19:00–21:00 Dinner in Old Town V Zátiší (Betlémské náměstí 5) — refined Czech/Indian fusion; or Kolkovna (V Kolkovně 8) for classic Czech fare and beer.

Day 2: Prague Castle, Malá Strana & Petřín Hill

Theme: Royal history, panoramic views, and the city's most photogenic hillside.

Time Activity Notes & Restaurant Recommendations
8:30–9:00 Breakfast near Malá Strana Bakeshop (Kořenského 1) — excellent pastries and coffee.
9:00–12:30 Prague Castle complex Take tram 22 to Pražský hrad. See St. Vitus Cathedral, Old Royal Palace, Golden Lane, and St. George's Basilica. Buy the circuit ticket online to skip lines.
12:30–14:00 Lunch in Malá Strana U Modré Kachničky (Nebovidská 6) — duck specialties in a romantic setting; or Café de Paris (Velkopřevorské náměstí) for a lighter meal.
14:00–15:30 St. Nicholas Church & Nerudova Street Baroque masterpiece; walk down the charming Nerudova Street toward the river.
15:30–17:30 Petřín Hill & Lookout Tower
