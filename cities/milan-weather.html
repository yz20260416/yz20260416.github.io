Milan Travel Weather Guide

Milan's weather data reveals a city of strong seasonal contrasts. June and September offer the most balanced conditions for sightseeing, with highs near 29°C and 25°C respectively and manageable rain frequency, though summer humidity builds in July and August when highs hover around 30°C. May is the statistical outlier for rainfall, averaging 16.6 wet days—the highest of any month—so pack rain gear if visiting then. January is the coldest and dreariest month, with highs of just 7.7°C, lows near freezing, and nine rain days, making outdoor exploration uncomfortable. Late spring and early autumn are practically the sweet spots, while midsummer demands light clothing and hydration and winter calls for warm layers.

Practical Tips

  • Pack a compact umbrella and a waterproof layer year-round—no month averages fewer than seven rain days, and May peaks at 16.6.
  • Visit in June or September for the best balance of warm temperatures (28.9°C and 24.5°C highs) and moderate crowd levels.
  • Avoid May if you dislike rain, and avoid January if you dislike cold—both are the statistical low points for comfortable weather.
  • In July and August, schedule sightseeing for early morning or evening to avoid peak heat near 30°C, and carry water.
  • Winter visitors should bring insulated, windproof clothing; lows hover near freezing in January and December.
