Local Character

Chongqing's character is shaped by its role as China's wartime capital during the Second Sino-Japanese War (1937–1945), when the Republic of China government relocated here; visitors can still explore historical sites from this era, such as the former government buildings and air-raid shelters, which offer a glimpse into the city's resilience. The city's geography as a major Yangtze River port has fostered a strong maritime and trading culture, evident in the bustling docks and the traditional 'bangbang' porters who carry goods on bamboo poles—a dying trade that reflects the city's hilly terrain and hardworking spirit. Additionally, Chongqing's status as the only inland direct-administered municipality, separated from Sichuan in 1997 to spur development, has created a distinct local identity that blends urban ambition with deep-rooted Sichuanese traditions, from fiery hotpot to the rhythmic sounds of local opera.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Chongqing Monorail — Ride the world's longest and busiest monorail system, with 70 stations, including the famous Liziba Station where trains pass through a residential building.
  2. Yangtze River Economic Belt — Explore the bustling riverfront and docks that have made Chongqing a key connection in the Yangtze River Economic Belt, offering scenic views and local life.
  3. Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport — As China's second-busiest airport and a top-50 global hub, it's a modern gateway worth noting for its scale and efficiency.
  4. Wartime Capital Sites — Visit historical sites from Chongqing's tenure as the wartime capital of the Republic of China (1937–1945), including former government buildings and air-raid shelters.
  5. Chongqing University — Stroll through the campus of one of China's top universities, known for its research output and scenic location.

FAQ

What is the best time to visit Chongqing?
Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer mild temperatures and moderate rainfall, making them ideal for exploring the city.
How do I get around Chongqing?
The city's extensive monorail system, including the famous Liziba Station where trains pass through a building, is the most efficient way to navigate the mountainous terrain.
What local food should I try in Chongqing?
Chongqing is famous for its spicy hotpot, a must-try dish that reflects the city's bold flavors and Sichuanese culinary traditions.
