Best Time to Visit Lima

Lima is Peru's sprawling coastal capital, famed for its cliff-top ocean views, colonial-era center, and world-class culinary scene. Visit December through April for sunny skies and warm beach weather, or anytime for its museums and food. It is a year-round destination, though the gray garúa mist of winter (May–November) mutes its colors.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Miraflores and the Malecón — Cliff-top promenade offering ocean views, parks, and easy access to cafés and shops.
  2. Historic Centre of Lima — UNESCO-listed colonial heart featuring the Plaza Mayor and ornate balconies.
  3. Basilica and Convent of San Francisco — Baroque church famous for its catacombs and colonial art collection.
  4. Huaca Pucllana — Pre-Inca adobe pyramid in Miraflores with guided archaeological tours.
  5. Barranco District — Bohemian neighborhood of murals, galleries, and the Bridge of Sighs overlooking the sea.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Peruvian Sol (PEN, S/)
  • Language: Spanish (English is common in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: PET (UTC-5)
  • Visa: Most Western and many other visitors receive a 90-day entry stamp on arrival, but check current requirements for your nationality.

FAQ

Is Lima safe for tourists?
Tourist districts like Miraflores, San Isidro, and Barranco are generally safe with normal precautions, but avoid unlit areas at night and keep valuables out of sight.
What is Lima's signature food?
Ceviche is the city's flagship dish, alongside lomo saltado and causa, and Lima is widely regarded as Latin America's culinary capital.
How do I get around Lima?
Ride-hailing apps and taxis are the most practical option, while Metropolitano buses serve major corridors; traffic is heavy, so allow extra time.
