Bali 3-Day Itinerary

Bali is a magical island that packs an extraordinary amount of variety into a small area — from ancient Hindu temples and emerald rice terraces to world-class surf breaks and volcanic sunrises. With only three days, you'll need to be strategic: this itinerary focuses on the classic South Bali → Ubud → East Bali loop, hitting the island's signature highlights without spending hours stuck in traffic. Each day balances must-see landmarks with genuine local food, sunset viewpoints, and a little breathing room. Whether you're a first-timer or returning, this plan is built to be flexible — swap activities as your energy (and the weather) allows. Note: Bali traffic is legendary, so early starts are your best friend.

Day 1: South Bali — Beaches, Temples & Sunset

Base area: Seminyak / Canggu / Kuta (pick one; Seminyak is central for this route).

Time Activity Details Food / Notes
07:30 Breakfast at hotel or local warung Start with nasi campur or bubur injin (black rice pudding). Warung Local (Seminyak) — cheap, authentic, opens early.
08:30 Tanah Lot Temple One of Bali's most iconic sea temples, perched on a rock formation. Go early to beat crowds and heat. Entrance ~IDR 60,000. Bring sarong (or rent on-site).
11:00 Drive to Uluwatu area (approx. 1 hr) Scenic coastal drive through Jimbaran and Pecatu. Stop for coconut water along the way.
12:30 Lunch — Jimbaran Bay seafood Grilled fish and prawns right on the sand. Menega Café — legendary grilled seafood, book ahead for a beach table.
14:30 Padang Padang Beach or Bingin Beach Relax, swim, or watch surfers. Padang Padang is small but photogenic. Parking fee ~IDR 10,000. Bring cash for loungers.
17:00 Uluwatu Temple & Kecak Fire Dance Cliffside temple with dramatic ocean views. The Kecak dance at sunset is unforgettable. Temple entry ~IDR 50,000; Kecak ~IDR 150,000. Watch your belongings — monkeys are bold!
19:30 Dinner in Seminyak Modern Balinese or international fare. Merah Putih (upscale Indonesian) or Warung Ibu Made (home-style, budget-friendly).

Day 2: Ubud — Rice Terraces, Monkeys & Culture

Base area: Ubud (stay overnight here to avoid the long drive back south).

Time Activity Details Food / Notes
06:30 Tegallalang Rice Terraces Arrive at sunrise for soft light and empty paths. Walk the terraces and swing bridges.
