Kyoto Travel Weather Guide

Kyoto's weather data reveals a city of sharp seasonal contrast. May and October stand out as the best months to visit, with pleasant highs of 22.8°C and 22.6°C respectively and moderate rain-day counts, meaning you can comfortably explore temples and gardens on foot without oppressive heat or heavy gear. August is the toughest month: highs near 32.6°C, lows of 24.2°C, and roughly 14.8 rain days combine into hot, humid, storm-prone conditions that demand early starts and indoor backup plans. Winter is cold but relatively dry, while June and July bring the heaviest, most persistent rainfall alongside rising heat.

Practical tips

  • Aim for May or October for the most comfortable temperatures, but bring a compact umbrella since both months still see around 10–13 rain days.
  • Avoid late July and August if you dislike heat and humidity; if you must visit, sightsee before 9 a.m. and plan air-conditioned midday breaks.
  • June through September is the wettest stretch (13–15 rain days per month); pack waterproof shoes, a rain jacket, and flexible indoor backup plans.
  • Winter mornings can dip below freezing in January and February, so bring insulated layers, gloves, and warm footwear for temple visits.
  • Book accommodation early for April, October, and November, when pleasant weather coincides with peak blossom and foliage crowds.
