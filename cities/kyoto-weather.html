Kyoto Weather by Month

Kyoto’s climate is humid subtropical, with four sharply defined seasons that transform the city’s temples, gardens, and streetscapes. Summer is hot and muggy, winter is crisp with occasional light snow, and spring and autumn are famously beautiful. Use this monthly guide — with average high/low temperatures, rainfall, and travel notes — to plan your visit around the weather you prefer.
