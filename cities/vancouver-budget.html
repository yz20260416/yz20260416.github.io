Vancouver Travel Cost Guide

Vancouver is a stunning West Coast city where mountains meet the ocean — but it’s also one of Canada’s most expensive destinations. Whether you’re a backpacker watching every dollar, a mid-range traveller seeking comfort and experiences, or a luxury visitor looking for the finest, this guide breaks down realistic daily costs for accommodation, food, transport, and activities. All figures are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and reflect average 2025 prices (excluding peak summer surcharges, which can add 20–40%). Use the table below to plan your budget by travel style.
