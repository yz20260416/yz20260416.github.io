Xi'an Travel Weather Guide

Xi'an's climate rewards careful timing. The data shows April, May and September as the sweet spot, with highs of 24–27°C, lows of 11–18°C and only 6–11 rain days per month — ideal for walking the city walls, climbing the Big Wild Goose Pagoda and exploring the Terracotta Army without heat stress. July and August are the toughest months: highs of 33°C pair with 10–11 rain days, so expect humid heat and afternoon downpours, and plan indoor or early-morning sightseeing. January and December are cold but dry (under 3 rain days), meaning crisp, clear days with thin crowds — just pack serious layers.

Practical tips

  • Best window: April–May and September offer the most balanced temperatures; note that September still carries 11 rain days, so pack a compact umbrella.
  • Avoid the worst: July–August combine 33°C highs with 10–11 rain days — schedule Terracotta Army and museum visits for mornings.
  • Rain strategy: August, September and October are the wettest months (11 rain days each); build in flexible days and prioritize indoor attractions then.
  • Winter layers: January lows near -4°C with only 2.8 rain days mean dry, clear cold — thermal layers and a windproof coat are essential.
  • Air quality and dust: Spring can bring dust and haze; check forecasts and carry a mask if sensitive, especially in March–April.
