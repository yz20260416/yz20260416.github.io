Copenhagen 3-Day Itinerary

Copenhagen is a city that rewards slow travel: compact enough to explore on foot and by bike, yet packed with world-class design, harbor swimming, and a food scene that punches far above its size. This three-day itinerary balances the essential sights — Nyhavn, Tivoli, the Little Mermaid — with the neighborhoods locals actually love, from the street-food halls of Refshaleøen to the candle-lit wine bars of Vesterbro. Each day mixes major attractions with breathing room for a canal-side coffee or an impromptu bakery stop, because in Copenhagen, the hygge is the point. Distances are short, the metro is fast, and almost everything below is reachable by bike — the city's preferred mode of transport.

Day 1 Historic Copenhagen & the Harbor

TimeActivityNotes & Food
9:00 Nyhavn & harbor walk Start early to photograph the iconic 17th-century gabled townhouses before the crowds. Grab a pastry and coffee at Andersen Bakery nearby.
10:00 Amalienborg Palace Four rococo palaces around an octagonal square; the Changing of the Guard takes place daily at noon (12:00) when the royal family is in residence.
11:00 Designmuseum Danmark Denmark's flagship design museum — Danish chairs, ceramics, and fashion. Allow 1–1.5 hours.
12:30 Lunch: Torvehallerne Copenhagen's beloved food halls. Try Hallernes Smørrebrød for open-faced sandwiches, or Grød for porridge if you want something lighter.
14:00 Rundetaarn (Round Tower) Climb the spiral ramp (no stairs!) for a 360° view over the old city. Small entry fee.
15:30 Strøget & the Latin Quarter Wander Europe's longest pedestrian shopping street, then duck into the Latin Quarter for independent boutiques and Lagkagehuset for a cinnamon roll.
18:30 Dinner: Kødbyen (Meatpacking District) Former slaughterhouse district turned food-and-nightlife hub. Try Fiskebaren for seafood or Paté Paté for sharing plates.

Day 2 Christianshavn, Refshaleøen & Tivoli

TimeActivityNotes & Food
9:00 Christianshavn & Church of Our Saviour Climb the external spiral spire (weather permitting) for one of the city's best views. Note: the top section is steep and not for everyone.
10:30 Freetown Christiania Self-governed community with murals and workshops. Respect the no-photography rules in certain areas and keep to marked paths.
12:00 Lunch: Reffen street food Refshaleøen's waterfront street-food market — dozens of stalls, harbor views, and a relaxed vibe. Great for groups with mixed tastes.
14:00 Louisiana-style art stop or harbor swim On warm days, swim at Islands Brygge Havnebad (free harbor bath). Otherwise, browse Copenhagen Contemporary on Refshaleøen.
16:30 Canal boat tour Classic 1-hour boat tour from Nyhavn or Gammel Strand — a relaxed way to see the city from the water.
19:00 Tivoli Gardens One of the world's oldest amusement parks (opened 1843). Go at dusk when the lights come on; ride the wooden roller coaster. Note: Tivoli is open seasonally — check dates.
20:30 Dinner: Vesterbro Mother for sourdough pizza, or Bæst for organic pizza and house-made charcuterie. Book ahead on weekends.
