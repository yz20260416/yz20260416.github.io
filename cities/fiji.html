Best Time to Visit Fiji

Fiji is a South Pacific archipelago of more than 330 islands, with about 110 permanently inhabited, lying roughly 1,300 miles north-northeast of New Zealand. Most visitors base themselves on Viti Levu, home to the capital Suva and the tourism hub of Nadi, or on Vanua Levu. The dry, cooler months from May to October are the most comfortable for travel.

Local Character

Fiji's population is concentrated on Viti Levu and Vanua Levu, with about three-quarters of Fijians living on Viti Levu's coasts, in Suva or in smaller centres such as Nadi, where tourism dominates, and Lautoka, where the sugar-cane industry is dominant. A visitor should care because this coastal concentration means the island's interior remains sparsely inhabited and difficult to reach, so most cultural encounters happen along the coast rather than inland. The islands were largely formed by volcanic activity beginning around 150 million years ago, and geothermal activity still occurs today on Vanua Levu and Taveuni; on Viti Levu the geothermal systems are non-volcanic and produce low-temperature surface discharges of roughly 35 to 60 degrees Celsius. Travellers interested in the islands' natural character should note this distinction, since the visible hot springs on Viti Levu are a different phenomenon from the volcanic systems elsewhere. Human settlement dates to the second millennium BC, first Austronesians and later Melanesians with some Polynesian influences, and Europeans first visited in the 17th century; this layered settlement history shapes the island's place names and local identities.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Suva — Fiji's capital city, on the coast of Viti Levu, where a large share of the country's urban population lives.
  2. Nadi — A smaller urban centre on Viti Levu where tourism is the major local industry and most international arrivals begin their trip.
  3. Lautoka — A Viti Levu urban centre dominated by the sugar-cane industry, offering a glimpse of everyday working life.
  4. Taveuni — An island where geothermal activity still occurs today, one of the few places in Fiji with active volcanic heat.
  5. Vanua Levu — Fiji's second major island, home to geothermal activity and one of the two islands where most of the population lives.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Fijian dollar (FJD, $)
  • Language: English, Fijian and Fiji Hindi (English is widely used in tourism areas)
  • Time Zone: FJT (UTC+12)
  • Visa: Most international visitors receive a visitor permit on arrival for tourism stays, but travellers should confirm current requirements with Fiji's immigration authorities before booking.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit Fiji?
The cooler, drier months from May to October are generally the most comfortable, though this is also the busiest period.
How many islands does Fiji have?
Fiji consists of more than 330 islands, of which about 110 are permanently inhabited, plus more than 500 islets.
Where do most visitors stay?
Most arrivals begin on Viti Levu, either in the capital Suva or in Nadi, where tourism is the major local industry.
