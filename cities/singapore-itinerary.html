Singapore 3-Day Itinerary — Travel Guide

Singapore packs an extraordinary amount into a small island: futuristic skylines, UNESCO-listed gardens, hawker centres serving some of the world's best street food, and three distinct cultural quarters — all connected by one of the most efficient public transport systems on the planet. This 3-day itinerary balances the must-see icons with neighbourhood exploration and food experiences, so you leave feeling you've actually tasted Singapore, not just photographed it. The plan assumes you're staying centrally (Marina Bay, City Hall, or Bugis areas work well) and are comfortable using the MRT.

At a Glance

Day Theme Highlights

Day 1 — Marina Bay & the Modern City

TimeActivity
8:30 AM Breakfast at a local kopitiam — try kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs, and kopi at Ya Kun Kaya Toast (several branches citywide).
9:30 AM Walk the Marina Bay waterfront promenade past the Merlion Park for skyline photos before the heat and crowds build.
10:30 AM – 1:00 PM Gardens by the Bay — the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest conservatories are the highlights (book timed tickets online). The Supertree Grove is free to walk; the OCBC Skyway adds a canopy walk.
1:00 PM Lunch at Satay by the Bay (casual hawker-style in the gardens) or head to Lau Pa Sat hawker centre for satay and local classics.
2:30 – 4:30 PM Escape the midday heat at ArtScience Museum or the air-conditioned Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands. Alternatively, take the lift to the SkyPark Observation Deck (or a cocktail at CÉ LA VI) for panoramic views.
5:00 PM Rest at the hotel or stroll the Helix Bridge toward the Civic District.
7:00 PM Dinner — Jumbo Seafood (Riverside Point) for Singapore chilli crab, or Song Fa Bak Kut Teh for peppery pork rib soup.
9:00 PM Spectra light & water show at Marina Bay Sands Event Plaza (free, nightly) — arrive 15 minutes early for a good spot.

Day 2 — Culture & Heritage

TimeActivity
8:30 AM Breakfast in Chinatown — Tiong Bahru Bakery or dim sum at a traditional teahouse.
9:30 AM – 12:00 PM Chinatown: Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple, and the Chinatown Heritage Centre. Wander Pagoda Street and Trengganu Street for souvenirs.
12:00 PM Lunch at Maxwell Food Centre — the legendary Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice and Zhen Zhen Porridge are worth the queue.
1:30 – 3:30 PM Little India: Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, the colourful shophouses of Buffalo Road, and the bustling Tekka Centre market.
