Lijiang Travel Guide: A Perfect 3-Day Itinerary

Lijiang, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Yunnan Province, blends Naxi minority culture, cobblestone old-town lanes, and views of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. This three-day itinerary balances the atmospheric Old Town with nearby villages, mountain scenery, and local food, with morning, afternoon, and evening schedules plus practical tips for a smooth trip.

Day 1 — Old Town Immersion and Culture

TimePlan
Morning Arrive and settle in. Enter Lijiang Old Town (Dayan) early to beat crowds. Wander Sifang Street (Square Street), the town's central hub, and climb Wangu Tower on Lion Hill for rooftop views over the tiled roofs.
Afternoon Visit the Mu Family Mansion (Mu Fu), the former residence of the Naxi chieftains, then the Black Dragon Pool (Heilongtan) park north of town for classic postcard views of the bridge and pavilion with Jade Dragon Snow Mountain behind (clear days only).
Evening Dinner in the Old Town, then stroll the lantern-lit canals and bridges. Optional: a Naxi ancient music performance (Dongjing or Baisha fine music) at one of the small halls.

Day 2 — Jade Dragon Snow Mountain and Baisha

TimePlan
Morning Depart early (around 7:00–8:00) for Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (Yulong Xueshan). Take the cable car to Glacier Park (about 4,506 m) or the lower Spruce Meadow (Yunshanping). Acclimatize slowly and bring warm layers.
Afternoon Descend and visit Blue Moon Valley (Lanyuegu) for its turquoise terraced pools, then stop at Baisha Old Town to see the Ming-dynasty Baisha murals and the village's quieter lanes.
Evening Return to Lijiang for dinner. Rest early; the altitude and early start make this a full day.

Day 3 — Shuhe, Markets, and Departure

TimePlan
Morning Visit Shuhe Old Town, a smaller, calmer sister village about 4 km northwest of Dayan, with a pleasant stream, old bridges, and tea-horse road history. Browse local crafts and tea shops.
Afternoon Return to Lijiang for a final wander: Zhongyi Market for local produce and snacks, or the Dongba Culture Museum for Naxi script and heritage exhibits. Pick up souvenirs such as Naxi embroidery or Pu'er tea.
Evening Farewell dinner, then depart or transfer to the airport/station.

Restaurant Recommendations

  • Naxi-style home cooking: Look for restaurants serving baba (flatbread), Lijiang cold noodles, and cured pork with vegetables in the Old Town's side lanes.
  • Hotpot and wild mushrooms: Yunnan mushroom hotpot is a regional specialty; choose busy, well-reviewed spots and avoid unfamiliar wild mushrooms unless cooked thoroughly.
  • Breakfast: Small shops near Sifang Street sell rice noodles (mixian) and steamed buns; eat where locals queue.
  • Cafés: The Old Town and Shuhe have courtyard cafés for tea and coffee breaks; prices are higher on main tourist streets.

Practical Tips

  • Altitude: Lijiang sits at about 2,400 m and the glacier cable car reaches over 4,500 m. Hydrate, move slowly, and skip the high cable car if you feel unwell.
  • Old Town access: A maintenance fee may apply for entering the Old Town; keep any ticket or receipt with you.
  • Best season: March–May and September–November offer clear skies and mild weather; summer is rainy, winter is cold but sunny.
  • Getting around: The Old Town is pedestrian-only; wear comfortable shoes for uneven cobblestones. Taxis and buses connect Dayan, Shuhe, and Baisha.
  • Tickets: Book Jade Dragon Snow Mountain cable-car tickets in advance, especially in peak season; morning slots are best for views.
  • Etiquette: Ask before photographing locals, and respect Naxi cultural sites and temple rules.
  • Money and connectivity: Mobile payment is widespread; carry some cash for small vendors. Download a translation app and offline maps.
