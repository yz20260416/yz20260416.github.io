Local Character

Rome's identity is inseparable from its geography as the 'City of Seven Hills' and its status as Caput Mundi, or Capital of the World, a title reflecting its role as the first-ever imperial metropolis. Visitors should care because this hilly terrain shapes daily life and views, from the Palatine to the Aventine. Another specific quirk: Vatican City, the world's smallest country, sits entirely within Rome's municipal boundaries, the only existing example of a country within a city, so a single walk can cross an international border. The city's early population mixed Latins, Etruscans, and Sabines, and this layered heritage appears in local dialects and neighborhood identities. The poet Tibullus first called Rome 'The Eternal City' in the 1st century BC, an expression later taken up by Ovid, Virgil, and Livy; knowing this enriches any visit to the Forum. Rome's papal urban programme from Nicholas V onward made the city the birthplace of Baroque and Neoclassicism, so its streets are an open-air museum of those styles.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Colosseum — The iconic Flavian Amphitheatre, a symbol of imperial Rome and its spectacles.
  2. Vatican City — The world's smallest independent country, home to St. Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel.
  3. Roman Forum — The excavated heart of ancient Rome, where civic and religious life once centered.
  4. Pantheon — A remarkably preserved ancient temple with a famous domed ceiling and oculus.
  5. Trevi Fountain — A Baroque masterpiece where visitors toss coins to ensure a return to Rome.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit Rome?
April, May, and October offer mild weather and lively streets, though spring and early autumn are the busiest; July and August are hot and crowded.
Is Vatican City part of Rome?
Vatican City is an independent country entirely within Rome's municipal boundaries, the only existing example of a country within a city.
How many hills is Rome built on?
Rome is known as the City of Seven Hills, a geography that shapes its views and neighborhoods.
