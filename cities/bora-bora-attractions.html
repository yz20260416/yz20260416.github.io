Bora Bora Top 10 Attractions

Bora Bora is the ultimate South Pacific dream — a crown of emerald peaks encircled by a luminous turquoise lagoon and a string of coral motus. Beyond the overwater bungalows, the island offers world-class snorkeling, dramatic volcanic scenery, and a rich Polynesian culture. To help you plan, we’ve curated the top 10 attractions with honest descriptions, current ticket prices (in USD / XPF), and practical visiting tips so you can make the most of every moment in French Polynesia’s most iconic island.

✨ Essential experiences & landmarks

Attraction Description Ticket price* Visiting tips
1. Mount Otemanu The island’s iconic 727m (2,385 ft) volcanic peak, visible from almost everywhere. Lush rainforest, dramatic ridges, and panoramic views of the lagoon. Hiking is not permitted to the summit, but guided 4x4 safaris and boat tours explore the base and offer spectacular photo stops. 4x4 tour: $85–120
(≈ 9,000–13,000 XPF)		 Book a 4x4 safari with a local guide (no self-drive off-road). Morning tours are cooler and clearer. Wear sturdy shoes and bring water.
2. Lagoonarium A natural open-water aquarium on a private motu, famous for its friendly rays, blacktip reef sharks, and dense coral gardens. Guided snorkeling tours include feeding sessions and a traditional Polynesian lunch. Half-day tour: $95–140
(≈ 10,000–15,000 XPF)
