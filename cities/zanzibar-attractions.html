Zanzibar Top 10 Attractions

Zanzibar, the spice island off Tanzania’s coast, blends Swahili, Arab, Indian and European influences into a captivating travel destination. From the labyrinthine alleys of Stone Town to the powder-white beaches of Nungwi and the rare red colobus monkeys of Jozani Forest, here are the ten attractions that define the island — with current ticket prices (2025) and practical tips to help you plan a smooth, memorable trip.
