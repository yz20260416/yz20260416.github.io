Best Time to Visit Ho Chi Minh City

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are April and May. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

Ho Chi Minh City's data tells a clear story: February and March are the sweet spot, with highs of 33.9–35.3°C and only around 5 rain days per month, meaning reliable sunshine for sightseeing and day trips. September is the toughest month, with roughly 30 rain days and highs near 30.8°C — expect heavy afternoon downpours and plan indoor activities or flexible schedules. The dry season (December–April) is best for outdoor exploration, while the wet season (May–October) brings daily showers but also lush scenery, lower prices, and fewer crowds. Pack breathable clothing year-round, as temperatures rarely drop below 22°C.

Practical Tips

  • Time outdoor sightseeing for mornings. In the wet season (May–October), rain typically arrives in the afternoon, so schedule temples, markets, and walking tours before noon.
  • Pack for heat year-round. Lows rarely fall below 22°C, so light, breathable clothing is essential; add a compact umbrella or rain jacket if visiting between May and November.
  • Choose February or March for the best odds of dry, sunny weather — but note March is the hottest month, so plan shade breaks and hydration.
  • Book early for December–February. These dry, high-demand months coincide with peak tourist season and holiday travel.
  • Embrace the wet season for value. May–October offers lower hotel rates and thinner crowds; just keep itineraries flexible and check forecasts daily.
