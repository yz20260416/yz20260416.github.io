Amsterdam 3-Day Itinerary

Amsterdam is a city of canals, world-class museums, cozy cafés, and a laid-back cycling culture. With three days you can cover the essential highlights — the historic Canal Ring, the great art museums, the Jordaan neighborhood, and a day trip or a deeper dive into local life — without feeling rushed. This itinerary balances must-see sights with time to wander, eat well, and enjoy the city the way locals do. All timings are suggestions; book museum tickets online in advance, especially for the Anne Frank House and the Van Gogh Museum.

Day 1 — Historic Center & the Canal Ring

TimeActivityNotes & Food
09:00 Dam Square & Royal Palace
Start at the city's central square, see the Royal Palace (Koninklijk Paleis) and the Nieuwe Kerk.		 Grab a quick breakfast at a local broodjes (sandwich) shop nearby — try a Dutch cheese sandwich.
10:30 Anne Frank House
Walk 10 minutes to the Prinsengracht. The museum is deeply moving and takes about 1.5–2 hours.		 Book ahead Tickets are released online only, typically 6 weeks in advance. No same-day sales.
13:00 Lunch in the Jordaan
Wander into the Jordaan neighborhood via the Westerstraat and its side streets.		 Café de Reiger (Nieuwe Leliestraat) — classic Dutch stamppot and sandwiches in a brown café. Or Winkel 43 for the famous apple pie.
14:30 Canal Cruise
Board a 1-hour open-boat or covered canal cruise from near the Anne Frank House or Central Station.		 Open boats are best in good weather. Look for smaller operators for a more intimate experience.
16:30 Bloemenmarkt & Kalverstraat
Visit the floating flower market and stroll the shopping street toward the Begijnhof courtyard.		 The Begijnhof is a peaceful hidden courtyard — free to enter, respectful silence requested.
19:00 Dinner near Spui or Rembrandtplein Restaurant
