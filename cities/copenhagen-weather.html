Best Time to Visit Copenhagen

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are April. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

Copenhagen's weather data shows a classic Nordic seasonal split: July is the warmest month (highs of 20.7°C, lows near 15°C) but also the wettest by frequency, with 13 rain days — so pack layers and a compact umbrella for summer visits. February is the coldest and darkest-feeling month (highs of just 4.6°C, lows near 0.6°C), making it the least comfortable for sightseeing on foot. The sweet spot for travelers is late spring and early autumn: May (highs 15.1°C, 10.6 rain days) and September (highs 18.1°C, only 8.2 rain days) offer mild temperatures with relatively manageable rain, ideal for walking, cycling, and harbor exploration.

Practical tips

  • Rain is frequent year-round (8–13 days per month), so always pack a compact waterproof jacket or umbrella, even in summer.
  • Layer clothing: temperatures swing from near-freezing lows in winter to mild summer highs, and coastal winds make it feel cooler.
  • Choose May or September for the best balance of mild temperatures and fewer rain days if you want comfortable sightseeing.
  • Book accommodation and major attractions early for June–August, when crowds peak alongside the warmest weather.
  • In winter months (December–February), plan around short daylight hours and prioritize indoor activities and warm footwear.
