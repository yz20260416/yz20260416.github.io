Lijiang, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in northwestern Yunnan Province, is famed for its cobblestone old town, Naxi minority culture, and dramatic mountain scenery. The historic core, Old Town Lijiang (Dayan), dates back over 800 years and is crisscrossed by canals fed by the Black Dragon Pool. Most attractions cluster around the old town and the nearby Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, making a 3–4 day visit ideal. Below are the top 10 must-see attractions with practical details for planning your trip.

  1. Lijiang Old Town (Dayan Ancient Town)

    The heart of Lijiang and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997, this maze of cobblestone lanes, wooden Naxi courtyard houses, and stone bridges is best explored on foot. Highlights include Sifang Square (Square Street), the Waterwheel at the north entrance, and the nightly Naxi folk dancing. Expect crowds in the evenings and during holidays.

    Tickets: Free entry to the town; a maintenance fee (around ¥50) is sometimes charged and may be checked at certain sites.

    Hours: Open 24 hours; shops and museums typically 9:00–22:00.

    Tips: Visit early morning (7:00–9:00) for quiet streets and good light. Wear comfortable shoes—the lanes are uneven and slippery when wet.

  2. Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (Yulong Xueshan)

    A dramatic 5,596-meter massif about 30 km north of Lijiang, home to the Glacier Park cable car, Spruce Meadow, and Yak Meadow. The mountain is sacred to the Naxi people and offers alpine scenery, though the high altitude (over 4,500 m at the top station) requires caution.

    Tickets: Park entrance around ¥100; cable car tickets range roughly ¥120–¥180 per route (Glacier Park is the most popular).

    Hours: Typically 7:00–18:00 (last cable car up varies by season).

    Tips: Book cable car tickets in advance during peak season. Bring warm layers, sunscreen, and consider oxygen canisters sold locally. Those with heart or respiratory conditions should be cautious at altitude.

  3. Black Dragon Pool Park (Heilongtan)

    A serene park north of the old town famous for its classic postcard view of the Moon-Embracing Pavilion with Jade Dragon Snow Mountain reflected in the water. The park also houses the Dongba Culture Research Institute and a small museum.

    Tickets: Around ¥50 (often bundled with the old town maintenance fee).

    Hours: Roughly 7:00–18:00.

    Tips: Go on a clear morning for the best mountain reflection. The walk from the old town takes about 15–20 minutes.

  4. Mu Palace (Mufu)

    The former residence of the Mu family, hereditary Naxi chieftains who ruled the region for centuries. The reconstructed palace complex showcases Naxi architecture and history, with courtyards, halls, and exhibits on local governance and culture.

    Tickets: Around ¥60.

    Hours: Approximately 8:30–17:30.

    Tips: Combine with a walk up to Lion Hill (Wangu Tower) for panoramic old town views; the two are adjacent.

  5. Lion Hill and Wangu Tower

    A small hill at the edge of the old town topped by the wooden Wangu Tower, offering one of the best elevated views over the tiled rooftops of Dayan. A pleasant spot for photography, especially at sunset.

    Tickets: Around ¥50 for the tower/park.

    Hours: Roughly 8:00–18:00.

    Tips: Climb in the late afternoon for golden-hour photos; the steps are steep, so take your time.

  6. Shuhe Ancient Town

    A quieter, better-preserved old town about 4 km northwest of Dayan, also part of the UNESCO listing. Shuhe retains a more relaxed atmosphere, with tea houses, craft shops, and the historic Qinglong Bridge.

    Tickets: Free entry (maintenance fee may apply as in Dayan).

    Hours: Open 24 hours; businesses roughly 9:00–22:00.

    Tips: A good base if you prefer calm over nightlife; easy taxi or bike ride from Dayan.

  7. Baisha Ancient Town

    An ancient Naxi village about 10 km north of Lijiang, once the capital of the region. Its highlight is the Baisha Mural paintings (14th–17th centuries) blending Naxi, Tibetan, and Han Buddhist art, plus the nearby Baisha Naxi Embroidery Institute.

    Tickets: Around ¥30 for the mural site.

    Hours: Approximately 8:30–17:30.

    Tips: Visit on the way to Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. The village is small and walkable; allow 1–2 hours.

  8. Yulong Snow Mountain Glacier Park (Spruce Meadow & Yak Meadow)

    Sub-areas of the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain scenic zone. Spruce Meadow (Yunshanping) is a gentle alpine grassland with views of the peak; Yak Meadow offers quieter pastures and Tibetan-style scenery. Each has its own cable car.

    Tickets: Included in the mountain park fee; separate cable cars roughly ¥60–¥120 per meadow route.

    Hours: Typically 7:00–18:00.

    Tips: Spruce Meadow suits families and casual walkers; Yak Meadow is less crowded. Check cable car operating status in winter.

  9. Lashi Lake (Lashihai)

    A highland lake about 10 km west of Lijiang, an important wetland and wintering ground for migratory birds. Popular for horse riding along the lake, birdwatching (November–March), and visiting Naxi villages.

    Tickets: Lake access generally free; horse riding and boat trips ¥100–¥200 depending on route.

    Hours: Daylight hours; best visited 8:00–17:00.

    Tips: Agree on horse-riding prices and routes beforehand. Bring binoculars in winter for birdwatching.

  10. Dongba Culture Museum / Dongba Valley

    Dedicated to the Dongba culture of the Naxi people, including their pictographic script—one of the last living pictographic writing systems. Exhibits cover rituals, manuscripts, and traditional crafts; the Dongba Valley site offers performances and demonstrations.

    Tickets: Museum entry often free or low-cost; Dongba Valley performances around ¥100–¥150.

    Hours: Roughly 9:00–17:00 (closed some Mondays—confirm locally).

    Tips: A good rainy-day option and a meaningful introduction to Naxi heritage before exploring the old town.

Practical Tips for Visiting Lijiang

  • Best time to visit: March–May and September–November offer clear skies and mild weather. Winters are sunny but cold at night; summers bring rain.
  • Altitude: Lijiang sits at about 2,400 m; Jade Dragon Snow Mountain exceeds 4,500 m at the top station. Acclimatize first and ascend slowly.
  • Tickets and fees: Prices above are approximate and change seasonally; the old town maintenance fee and mountain park fees are the main costs. Carry cash for small vendors.
  • Transport: The old town is pedestrian-only. Use taxis or local buses for outlying sites; many travelers hire a driver for a day to cover Baisha, Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, and Lashi Lake.
  • Etiquette: Respect Naxi customs and ask before photographing locals; dress modestly at cultural sites.
  • Packing: Sun protection (high UV at altitude), layers, rain gear, and comfortable walking shoes are essential.
