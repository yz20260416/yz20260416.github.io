Lijiang Travel Guide: Top 10 Must-See Attractions

Lijiang, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in northwestern Yunnan Province, China, is famed for its well-preserved Naxi old town, cobblestone lanes, canals, and views of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. The following guide covers the top 10 attractions with descriptions, ticket prices, opening hours, and practical visiting tips. Prices and hours are indicative and can change seasonally or during holidays, so confirm locally before visiting.

  1. Lijiang Old Town (Dayan Ancient Town)

    The historic core of Lijiang, a maze of stone-paved streets, canals, bridges, and Naxi courtyard houses. Highlights include Sifang Street, the Waterwheel at the north entrance, and views from Lion Hill (Wangu Tower).

    • Ticket: Old town entry is free; the Old Town Maintenance Fee (if charged) is around CNY 50. Wangu Tower/Lion Hill is about CNY 50.
    • Hours: Open all day; shops typically 09:00–22:00.
    • Tips: Visit early morning for quiet lanes and photos; wear comfortable shoes for uneven stone streets; expect crowds in the evening.

  2. Black Dragon Pool Park (Heilongtan)

    A scenic park north of the old town known for its clear spring pool and classic postcard view of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain reflected in the water, plus historic pavilions and the Dongba Culture Museum nearby.

    • Ticket: About CNY 50 (often bundled with the old town maintenance fee; check current policy).
    • Hours: Roughly 07:00–19:00 (seasonal).
    • Tips: Best visited on clear mornings for mountain reflections; allow 1–2 hours; combine with a visit to the Dongba Culture Museum.

  3. Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (Yulong Xueshan)

    A dramatic mountain massif reaching 5,596 m at Shanzidou Peak, with cable cars, alpine meadows, and the Glacier Park. Popular stops include Ganhaizi Meadow, Spruce Meadow, and the Glacier Park at around 4,500 m.

    • Ticket: Park entrance about CNY 100; cable car tickets vary (roughly CNY 55–120 per cableway); shuttle bus extra.
    • Hours: Typically 07:00–18:00 (last cable car times vary by season).
    • Tips: Book cable car tickets in advance during peak season; bring warm layers and sun protection; ascend gradually and watch for altitude sickness; consider renting oxygen if sensitive.

  4. Shuhe Ancient Town

    A quieter, more relaxed old town about 4 km northwest of Lijiang, with Naxi architecture, cafés, and a slower pace. It is also a stop along the old Tea Horse Road.

    • Ticket: Free entry (some individual sites may charge).
    • Hours: Open all day.
    • Tips: Good base for travelers seeking calm; walk to the Old Stone Bridge and local markets; easy taxi or bus from Lijiang Old Town.

  5. Baisha Ancient Town

    A historic village north of Lijiang with Naxi murals, traditional courtyards, and a laid-back atmosphere. The Baisha Murals are the main cultural draw.

    • Ticket: Town entry free; Baisha Murals ticket about CNY 30.
    • Hours: Town open all day; mural sites roughly 08:00–18:00.
    • Tips: Visit on a clear day for mountain views; combine with nearby Yuhu Village and Joseph Rock’s former residence; try local Naxi food.

  6. Yuhu Village and Joseph Rock’s Former Residence

    A traditional Naxi village at the foot of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain, known for stone houses and its association with botanist-explorer Joseph Rock.

    • Ticket: Village entry free; residence/museum small fee (about CNY 10–20).
    • Hours: Daytime, roughly 08:00–18:00.
    • Tips: Pair with Baisha and Jade Dragon Snow Mountain; bring cash for small vendors; respect local homes and residents.

  7. Lijiang Mural Sites and Dongba Culture Museum

    The Dongba Culture Museum in Black Dragon Pool Park explains Naxi Dongba script and culture, while historic murals in Baisha and nearby temples reflect Tibetan, Han, and Naxi artistic influences.

    • Ticket: Museum entry often included with park ticket; mural sites vary (about CNY 20–30).
    • Hours: Typically 09:00–17:00; closed some Mondays.
    • Tips: Good for rainy days or cultural context; check opening days in advance; allow 1–2 hours.

  8. Lion Hill (Shizi Shan) and Wangu Tower

    A hill at the edge of the old town offering panoramic rooftop views of Lijiang. Wangu Tower is a landmark viewpoint.

    • Ticket: About CNY 50 for the tower/viewpoint area.
    • Hours: Roughly 08:00–18:00.
    • Tips: Go late afternoon for soft light; climb is moderate—take your time; combine with a walk through the old town.

  9. Tiger Leaping Gorge (Tiger Leaping Gorge)

    A spectacular deep gorge on the Jinsha River between Lijiang and Shangri-La, popular for hiking and viewpoints. It is a day trip or overnight excursion from Lijiang.

    • Ticket: About CNY 45–65 (varies by section).
    • Hours: Daytime; trail access hours vary by season.
    • Tips: Wear sturdy shoes; check weather and trail conditions; the high trail is strenuous—choose the lower trail for easier walks; arrange transport in advance.

  10. Lashi Lake (Lashihai) and Tea Horse Road Area

    A plateau lake and wetland area west of Lijiang, known for birdwatching, horse riding along old Tea Horse Road routes, and rural scenery.

    • Ticket: Lake area generally free; activities and some reserves charge (about CNY 30–100 depending on activity).
    • Hours: Daytime; activity operators typically 08:00–18:00.
    • Tips: Best in winter and spring for migratory birds; agree on prices before horse riding; bring sun protection and water.

Practical Tips for Visiting Lijiang

  • Altitude: Lijiang sits at about 2,400 m; Jade Dragon Snow Mountain exceeds 4,500 m at the cable car stations. Acclimatize, stay hydrated, and avoid rapid ascent if sensitive to altitude.
  • Best seasons: Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer clear skies and mild weather; summer is rainy; winter is cold but sunny.
  • Tickets: Book popular sites (Jade Dragon Snow Mountain cable cars) in advance during holidays; carry cash for small fees and rural vendors.
