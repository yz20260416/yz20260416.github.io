Jerusalem Travel Weather Guide

Jerusalem's weather data reveals a classic Mediterranean pattern with two distinct travel windows. The most comfortable months are April, May, and October, when highs sit between 24–28°C and rain days drop to just 2–3 per month, meaning you can plan outdoor sightseeing in the Old City without carrying rain gear or battling intense heat. In contrast, July and August are the hottest and driest (highs near 33°C, essentially zero rain), so sightseeing is best done early morning or late afternoon. January and February are the wettest and coolest (highs around 14°C, 9–10 rain days per month), so pack layers and a waterproof jacket if visiting then.

Practical tips

  • Best overall windows: April–May and October offer warm, mostly dry conditions with manageable heat—book these early as crowds are high.
  • Avoid peak heat: If visiting July–August, schedule outdoor sites before 10am or after 5pm and carry plenty of water.
  • Rain gear for winter: January–February average 9–10 rain days per month, so bring a waterproof jacket and shoes with grip for stone streets.
  • Layer up: Even in warm months, lows can drop 12–15°C below daytime highs, so a light layer helps for evenings.
  • Shoulder-season balance: March and November are mild but showery—ideal if you prefer fewer crowds and don't mind occasional rain.
