Athens Weather by Month

Athens enjoys one of the sunniest and warmest climates in Europe, with a typical Mediterranean climate characterized by hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. The city receives roughly 2,800 hours of sunshine per year and very little rainfall between June and September, making it an appealing destination across most of the calendar. However, temperatures and conditions vary dramatically by season—summer heat can be intense, while winter brings cooler, rainier days. Understanding Athens' month-by-month weather will help you choose the ideal time to visit, pack appropriately, and plan sightseeing around the heat or rain. The guide below covers average high and low temperatures, approximate rainfall, and the best months for a trip.

Note: Figures are long-term averages and can vary year to year. Summer heatwaves can push temperatures above 38°C (100°F), and winter cold snaps occasionally bring near-freezing nights.

Best Months to Visit Athens

  • Best overall: April–May and September–October — warm, mostly dry, comfortable temperatures, and manageable crowds.
  • Best for sunshine and swimming: June–August — reliably hot and dry, ideal for beaches, though sightseeing requires early starts.
  • Best for budget and quiet sightseeing: November–March — lowest prices and fewest crowds, but expect cooler, rainier weather; winter is still far milder than most of Europe.
  • Peak season to avoid if you dislike heat/crowds: mid-July through August, when temperatures and visitor numbers peak.

Practical Tips

  • Beat the summer heat: In July and August, visit archaeological sites (like the Acropolis) at opening time or late afternoon, carry water, wear a hat, and use sunscreen. Many sites offer little shade.
  • Pack for the season: Light, breathable clothing and sun protection for summer; layers and a waterproof jacket for winter and late autumn. Comfortable walking shoes are essential year-round on Athens' uneven streets and marble paths.
  • Watch for rain in winter: Rainfall is concentrated between November and March. Showers are often short but can be heavy—keep a compact umbrella handy.
  • Check for heatwaves and wildfire risk: Summer heatwaves are common, and strong winds can raise wildfire risk in the surrounding region. Follow local advisories.
  • Shoulder seasons offer the best balance: Spring and autumn provide pleasant temperatures for exploring ruins, neighborhoods like Plaka and Monastiraki, and day trips to Cape Sounion or the islands.
  • Book ahead in peak months: Accommodation and popular attractions fill up in summer and around major holidays (Easter, August 15); reserve tickets and rooms early.
