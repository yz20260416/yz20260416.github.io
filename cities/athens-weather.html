Athens offers a classic Mediterranean climate where timing shapes your trip. The data shows two clear sweet spots: May and October, when highs sit around 23–25°C with only 3.6–5 rain days, meaning warm sightseeing weather without oppressive heat or heavy showers. The hardest months are July and August, when highs of 33–35°C and lows above 23°C make midday exploration genuinely uncomfortable, so plan early mornings, shaded sites, and long siesta breaks. Rain concentrates in late autumn and winter—November and December average 8–9 wet days—so carry a compact umbrella if visiting then. Spring (April–June) is the most reliable all-round window.

Practical tips

  • Avoid midday heat in July–August: schedule outdoor sites before 11am or after 5pm and build in an air-conditioned siesta.
  • Target May, September, or October for the best mix of comfortable temperatures, low rain days, and manageable crowds.
  • In November–December, carry a compact umbrella and plan indoor backups—roughly one in three days sees rain.
  • Wear sturdy shoes for marble sites like the Acropolis, which become slippery when wet in spring and winter.
  • Book major attractions and timed tickets in advance during May–June and September, when crowds peak alongside good weather.
