Warsaw Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Travel Planning

Warsaw's climate rewards travelers who plan around its sharp seasonal swings. The best window is May through September, with July (average high 25.7°C) and August (24.9°C) offering the warmest, liveliest conditions—though both months carry high rain-day counts, so pack a light rain jacket for afternoon showers. The toughest stretch is January (high 2.9°C, low -1.7°C, 14.6 rain days), when short daylight and frequent wet, near-freezing days make sightseeing uncomfortable. September stands out as a sweet spot: still mild at 20.6°C with only 7.8 rain days, the driest month of the year. In practice, summer visitors should book early and expect crowds, while winter travelers get cheaper rates but need warm, waterproof layers.

Practical tips

  • Pack for rain year-round: every month averages at least 7.8 rain days, so a compact umbrella and waterproof jacket are essential in any season.
  • Target September for the best balance: it combines a mild 20.6°C high with the lowest rain-day count (7.8) and thinner crowds than midsummer.
  • Avoid January if comfort matters: it has the most rain days (14.6) and sub-zero lows, making outdoor sightseeing tough—though hotel prices are lowest.
  • Book summer early: July and August bring peak crowds and high accommodation demand, so reserve hotels and major attractions in advance.
  • Use daylight wisely in winter: short days from November to February mean front-loading outdoor plans and saving museums for after dark.
