Warsaw Weather by Month

Warsaw has a temperate continental climate, characterized by cold winters, warm summers, and a distinct swing between seasons. Average highs range from around 1°C (34°F) in January to 25°C (77°F) in July, while rainfall is fairly evenly distributed throughout the year, with slightly wetter summer months. This guide breaks down the weather month by month so you can plan your visit around the conditions that suit you best — whether that's long, sunny days in the parks or crisp winter walks through the Old Town.

Figures are long-term averages and vary year to year. Rainfall totals are modest overall — Warsaw receives roughly 500–550 mm annually — but summer showers and thunderstorms can be intense and brief.

Best Months to Visit

  • May–June and September: The sweet spot for most travelers — mild temperatures (roughly 18–22°C / 64–72°F), longer daylight hours, and fewer crowds than midsummer. Ideal for sightseeing, walking tours, and outdoor cafés.
  • July–August: The warmest and liveliest months, with highs around 24–25°C (75–77°F). Perfect for parks, the Vistula riverfront, and festivals, though this is peak season with higher hotel prices and busier attractions.
  • December: A magical time for Christmas markets and festive atmosphere, but expect cold temperatures and short days. Pack warm layers.
  • January–February: Best for budget travelers and those seeking a quiet, wintry city. Snow is possible but not guaranteed; daylight is limited.

Practical Tips

  • Layer up year-round: Even in summer, evenings can be cool, and spring and autumn temperatures fluctuate significantly between morning and afternoon.
  • Pack for rain: Showers occur in every month. A compact umbrella or waterproof jacket is worth carrying, especially from May to August when thunderstorms are common.
  • Winter essentials (Nov–Mar): Bring a warm coat, hat, gloves, and waterproof boots. Sidewalks can be icy, and temperatures occasionally drop well below freezing.
  • Summer sun: June and July offer up to 16–17 hours of daylight. Sunglasses and sunscreen are useful, and long evenings are perfect for exploring.
  • Check the forecast: Warsaw's weather can shift quickly, particularly in spring and autumn. A daily forecast check helps you plan indoor versus outdoor activities.
  • Book ahead in peak season: July and August, plus holiday periods around Christmas and New Year, see the highest demand for accommodation — reserve early for the best rates.
