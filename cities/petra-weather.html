Petra's desert climate rewards careful timing. The data shows the sweet spot is March–April and October–November, when highs sit comfortably between 18°C and 25°C — ideal for the long walks through the Siq and up to the Monastery. The hardest months are July and August, when average highs of about 33°C and lows near 21°C make midday hiking genuinely draining, so visits must start at dawn. Practical takeaway: book spring or autumn for balanced temperatures and manageable rain, avoid midsummer unless you can handle serious heat, and pack layers for cold desert nights in winter.

Practical Tips

  • Best overall windows: April and October offer the most reliable balance of mild temperatures (highs around 20–25°C) and manageable rain — book these months first.
  • Avoid midsummer midday: In July and August highs average about 33°C; begin hikes at first light, carry 3+ litres of water, and rest in shade during the hottest hours.
  • Rain strategy: March, September and November average 9–10+ rain days; pack a light waterproof shell and grippy footwear for slick stone steps and the Siq floor.
  • Layer up in winter: December–February lows fall to around 7–9°C, so bring warm layers for dawn starts and cold desert evenings.
  • Crowd timing: Spring and autumn are busiest; arrive at the site gate at opening time and consider late-afternoon visits for softer light and thinner crowds.
