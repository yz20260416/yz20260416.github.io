Best Time to Visit Mexico City

Mexico City is the oldest capital city in the Americas, built atop the Mexica city of Tenochtitlan on the high Mexican Central Plateau at 2,240 meters. As North America's most populous city and an Alpha world city, it draws visitors with layered Indigenous and colonial history. Visit during the dry months from November to April for clearer skies and easier exploring.

Local Character

Mexico City began around 1325 as Tenochtitlan, a Mexica city built on islands in Lake Texcoco, and after the 1521 siege it was rebuilt to Spanish urban standards — a visitor walking the centro can still sense this double foundation beneath the colonial street grid. It is one of only two capitals in the Americas founded by Indigenous peoples, which makes its central squares and church sites read differently from other colonial cities. The city is divided into 16 alcaldías, further split into colonias, so address-based exploring rewards travelers who treat neighborhoods as distinct units rather than one continuous downtown. Because it sits at 2,240 meters, the altitude shapes daily life and should shape your first days: plan lighter mornings and hydration rather than a packed arrival schedule.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Zócalo and the historic centro — The main square of the old Spanish city, built over the Mexica center and surrounded by the cathedral and government palaces.
  2. Templo Mayor — Excavated remains of the main temple of Tenochtitlan, the Mexica city founded around 1325 on Lake Texcoco.
  3. Coyoacán — One of the city's historic colonias, offering a slower neighborhood pace away from the centro.
  4. Xochimilco — A southern alcaldía whose canals recall the lake-and-island geography of the Valley of Mexico.
  5. Chapultepec — A large central park and hill area in the city, suited to a half-day of walking and viewpoints.

FAQ

How does the altitude affect a visit?
At 2,240 meters, the city can cause shortness of breath and fatigue, so keep your first day light and drink plenty of water.
When is the best time to visit?
November through April is the drier season, with January and February offering clear, mild days and moderate crowds.
How is the city organized for exploring?
Mexico City is divided into 16 alcaldías, each subdivided into colonias, so plan visits by neighborhood rather than trying to cover the whole city at once.
