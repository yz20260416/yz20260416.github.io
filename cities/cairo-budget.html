```html Cairo Travel Cost Guide

Cairo is one of the most affordable major cities in the world for travelers, offering a remarkable range of experiences—from ancient pyramids and bustling bazaars to Nile-side dining and world-class museums. Whether you're a backpacker watching every Egyptian pound or a luxury traveler seeking five-star comfort, Cairo can accommodate your budget. This guide breaks down estimated daily costs across three travel styles: budget, mid-range, and luxury, covering accommodation, food, transport, and activities.

All prices are listed in Egyptian Pounds (EGP) and approximate US Dollars (USD) based on an exchange rate of roughly 1 USD ≈ 48 EGP (subject to fluctuation). Costs are per person per day unless otherwise noted.

🏨 Accommodation

  • Budget: Hostels in downtown Cairo (near Tahrir Square) offer dorm beds for EGP 400–600/night. Private rooms in budget guesthouses start around EGP 700–1,000. Areas like Downtown and Hadayek El Ahram (near Giza) are popular for budget stays.
  • Mid-Range: 3–4 star hotels and boutique guesthouses in Zamalek, Maadi, or Giza offer comfortable rooms with breakfast for EGP 1,500–3,500/night. Many include pyramid or Nile views at this tier.
