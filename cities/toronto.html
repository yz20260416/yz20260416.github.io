Best Time to Visit Toronto

Toronto is Canada's largest city, a diverse lakeside metropolis known for its multicultural neighbourhoods, world-class museums, and lively food and arts scene. Visit in late spring or early autumn for mild weather and vibrant outdoor life, while summer brings festivals and winter offers skating and holiday markets.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. CN Tower — Iconic 553-metre tower offering observation decks, a glass floor, and EdgeWalk experiences high above the city.
  2. Royal Ontario Museum — Canada's largest museum, showcasing natural history, world cultures, and dinosaurs in a striking crystal-clad building.
  3. Art Gallery of Ontario — Major art museum with a Frank Gehry-designed facade and a strong collection of Canadian and Indigenous art.
  4. St. Lawrence Market — Historic market hall packed with food vendors, fresh produce, and the famous Saturday farmers' market.
  5. Toronto Islands — Car-free island chain reachable by ferry, offering beaches, bike paths, and skyline views back toward downtown.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Canadian Dollar (CAD, C$)
  • Language: English (French is also official nationally; multilingual services are common)
  • Time Zone: EST (UTC-5), with daylight saving in summer (EDT, UTC-4)
  • Visa: Most international visitors need an eTA or visitor visa, so check Canada's official immigration requirements before travelling.

FAQ

How do I get around Toronto?
The TTC subway, streetcars, and buses cover the city well, and a Presto card or contactless payment makes fares easy.
Is tipping expected in Toronto?
Yes, tipping is customary, typically 15-20% at restaurants and for table service.
What is the best time to visit Toronto?
Late May to early October offers the mildest weather and the most festivals, with September being a particularly pleasant shoulder month.
