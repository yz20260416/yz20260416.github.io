Ho Chi Minh City Travel Budget Guide

Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) is one of Southeast Asia's most affordable major cities, but costs vary widely depending on your travel style. From $25-a-day backpacking to luxury stays in District 1's five-star hotels, the city offers options across every budget. This guide breaks down typical daily and per-item costs for accommodation, food, transport, and activities across three budget tiers. Prices are in US dollars (USD) with approximate Vietnamese dong (VND) equivalents, based on 2024–2025 rates. Note that Vietnam's currency is the dong; $1 is roughly 25,000 VND.

Practical Tips

  • Carry cash: Many street vendors, local restaurants, and small shops are cash-only. ATMs are common but charge withdrawal fees ($2–5).
  • Use ride-hailing apps: Grab is the standard for bikes and cars; it avoids haggling and typically costs less than street taxis.
  • Book accommodation in advance during peak season: December to February and around Tet (Vietnamese New Year) see higher rates and full hotels.
  • Eat where locals eat: Street food and com binh dan (rice plate) stalls offer the best value and often the best food.
  • Negotiate at markets: Ben Thanh Market and similar spots expect bargaining; start around half the quoted price.
  • Watch for tourist pricing: Some attractions and services charge foreigners more than locals; this is normal and usually modest.
  • Budget for SIM data: A local SIM with data costs roughly $5–10 and makes navigation and ride-hailing far easier.
  • Tipping: Not mandatory, but rounding up or leaving 5–10% at restaurants and small tips for guides are appreciated.
