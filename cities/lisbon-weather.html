Lisbon Travel Weather Guide

Lisbon's weather data reveals a classic Mediterranean climate with mild, wet winters and hot, dry summers. August stands out as the best month for sunshine-seekers, with virtually no rain (0.0 rain days) and highs near 29°C—practically guaranteeing dry beach and sightseeing days, though you'll share the city with peak crowds. December is the wettest month (10.8 rain days) combined with cooler highs around 16°C, so winter travelers should pack rain gear and plan indoor activities like museums and cafés. Spring and autumn offer a sweet spot: May and September deliver warm temperatures with manageable rainfall, making them ideal for walking tours and day trips without the summer crush.

Practical tips

  • Book May or September for the best balance of warm temperatures, lower rain risk, and manageable crowds.
  • If visiting July or August, expect intense heat and near-zero rain—carry water, seek shade midday, and reserve attractions in advance.
  • From October through April, keep a compact umbrella and a waterproof layer handy; rain days cluster heavily in these months.
  • Winter travelers (December–February) should pack warm layers for cool evenings and plan indoor backups for wet days.
  • Check the daily forecast the week before you travel—rain-day counts are monthly averages, and individual years can vary.
