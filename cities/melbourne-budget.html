```html Melbourne Travel Cost Guide

Melbourne is one of Australia's most vibrant and culturally rich cities, known for its laneway coffee culture, world-class street art, major sporting events, and diverse neighborhoods. While Australia has a reputation for being an expensive destination, Melbourne offers excellent value for travelers who plan wisely. Whether you're a backpacker watching every dollar, a mid-range traveler seeking comfort and experiences, or a luxury visitor indulging in fine dining and premium stays, this guide breaks down typical costs across accommodation, food, transport, and activities so you can budget with confidence.

All figures are in Australian dollars (AUD) and reflect average 2024–2025 prices. Costs are per person per day unless otherwise stated. USD equivalents are approximate (1 AUD ≈ 0.65 USD).

Accommodation Costs

  • Hostels: Dorm beds run $35–$60 per night; private rooms in hostels $90–$140. Book early during peak season (Dec–Feb, major events like the Australian Open).
  • Mid-range hotels & serviced apartments: $150–$280 per night in the CBD or inner suburbs like Fitzroy and South Yarra. Apartments with kitchens can save on food costs.
  • Luxury hotels: $350–$700+ per night for 5-star properties along the Yarra River or in the CBD. Expect higher rates during Grand Prix and Australian Open weeks.
  • Airbnb: Entire apartments typically $120–$250 per night in inner suburbs; cheaper in outer areas with good tram/train access.

Food & Drink Costs

  • Budget: Supermarket self-catering ($10–$15/day), $8–$12 for a banh mi or meat pie, $4–$6 for coffee.
  • Mid-range: Café breakfast $15–$25, casual lunch $18–$30, dinner at a mid-range restaurant $30–$50 per main. A glass of wine $10–$16.
  • Luxury: Fine dining tasting menus $120–$250+ per person; premium wine pairings extra. Cocktail bars $20–$25 per drink.
  • Tips: Tipping isn't expected in Australia, though rounding up or 10% for excellent service is appreciated.

Transport Costs

  • Myki card: The reloadable card for trams, trains, and buses. A full-fare daily cap is around $11 (weekdays) and $7.20 on weekends — unlimited travel once reached.
  • Free Tram Zone: Trams within the CBD and Docklands are free — a huge money-saver for sightseeing.
  • SkyBus: Airport to CBD transfer ~$24 one-way (or ~$46 return).
  • Rideshare/taxi: Short CBD trips $12–$20; airport trips $50–$70. Use rideshare apps for better rates.
  • Bike share: Around $3–$5 per 30 minutes for short city hops.

Activities & Attractions

  • Free: Royal Botanic Gardens, National Gallery of Victoria (NGV), laneway street art tours (self-guided), Queen Victoria Market browsing, St Kilda Beach, Fitzroy Gardens.
  • Low cost ($10–$30): Melbourne Museum, Old Melbourne Gaol (~$30), Eureka Skydeck (~$28), Melbourne Zoo (~$47), guided walking tours (tip-based).
