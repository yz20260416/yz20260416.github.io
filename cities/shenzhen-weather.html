Shenzhen has a subtropical climate with mild, dry winters and hot, humid summers. The data shows the best travel window is October–December, when highs sit comfortably between 21–29°C and rain days drop to just 4–9 per month — ideal for sightseeing, theme parks, and coastal walks. In contrast, June and August are the worst months, each averaging around 26 rain days with highs near 30°C, meaning frequent downpours, high humidity, and possible typhoon disruption. Practically, plan outdoor activities for late autumn and winter, and if visiting in summer, build flexibility into your itinerary and keep rain gear handy at all times.

Practical tips

  • Time your trip for October–December: rain days fall to 4–9 per month and highs stay a comfortable 21–29°C — the sweet spot for outdoor sightseeing.
  • Avoid June and August if you can: both average around 26 rain days with highs near 30°C, bringing downpours, high humidity, and typhoon risk.
  • Carry rain protection year-round in summer: from April to September, afternoon showers are common, so pack a compact umbrella and quick-dry clothing.
  • Dress in layers for winter: January lows average around 11–12°C while daytime highs reach 20°C+, so mornings and evenings feel noticeably cooler.
  • Watch typhoon forecasts from July to September: keep itineraries flexible and check local warnings, as storms can disrupt transport and outdoor plans.
