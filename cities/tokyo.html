Best Time to Visit Tokyo

Tokyo is a vibrant metropolis where ancient temples sit beside neon-lit streets. Spring brings cherry blossoms and mild weather, while autumn offers crisp air and colorful foliage. This guide breaks down each month so you can choose the ideal time for your trip.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Senso-ji Temple — Tokyo's oldest temple, famous for its giant red lantern and lively market street.
  2. Shibuya Crossing — The world's busiest pedestrian scramble, best viewed from a nearby café.
  3. Meiji Shrine — A serene forested shrine dedicated to Emperor Meiji, a peaceful escape from the city.
  4. Tokyo Skytree — A soaring observation tower offering panoramic views of the entire metropolis.
  5. Tsukiji Outer Market — A bustling market packed with fresh seafood, street food, and local delicacies.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Japanese Yen (JPY, ¥)
  • Language: Japanese (English signage is common in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: JST (UTC+9)
  • Visa: Many nationalities enjoy visa-free short stays; check your country's requirements before traveling.

FAQ

What is the best month to visit Tokyo?
April and May are ideal for mild weather and cherry blossoms, while October and November offer crisp air and autumn foliage.
Is Tokyo expensive to visit?
Tokyo can be pricey, but budget travelers can save by eating at local eateries, using public transit, and booking accommodation in advance.
Do I need to speak Japanese to get around?
Not necessarily. Major signs and train announcements are in English, though learning a few basic phrases is always appreciated.
