Best Time to Visit Bora Bora

Bora Bora is a small volcanic island in French Polynesia ringed by a spectacular turquoise lagoon and coral motus, widely regarded as the ultimate South Pacific honeymoon destination. Visit during the drier austral winter (May–October) for sunny skies and calm lagoon waters, though the wet season offers lush scenery and lower prices. It is a place for overwater bungalows, snorkeling with rays and sharks, and dramatic Mount Otemanu views.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Mount Otemanu — This dramatic 727-metre volcanic peak dominates the island's skyline and can be viewed on lagoon cruises or hiking trails.
  2. Lagoonarium — A natural open-water aquarium on a motu where visitors snorkel among rays, sharks, and tropical fish.
  3. Matira Beach — Bora Bora's most famous public beach offers soft white sand and shallow, calm turquoise water.
  4. Coral Gardens — A shallow snorkeling site inside the lagoon known for its vibrant coral formations and abundant reef fish.
  5. Bloody Mary's — A legendary beachside restaurant and bar famed for fresh seafood and its wall of celebrity visitor names.

Practical Information

  • Currency: CFP Franc (XPF)
  • Language: French and Tahitian (English is common in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: TAHT (UTC-10)
  • Visa: Citizens of the EU, US, and many other countries can visit French Polynesia visa-free for up to 90 days with a valid passport.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit Bora Bora?
May through October offers the driest, sunniest weather and calm lagoon conditions, though it is also the busiest and priciest period.
How do I get to Bora Bora?
Fly into Bora Bora Airport (BOB) from Tahiti's Faa'a International Airport, then take a short boat transfer to your resort or the main village of Vaitape.
Is Bora Bora expensive?
Yes, it is one of the world's most expensive destinations, with luxury overwater bungalows and imported goods driving up costs, though guesthouses offer cheaper options.
