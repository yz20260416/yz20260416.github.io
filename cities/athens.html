Best Time to Visit Athens

Athens is the ancient capital of Greece, blending classical ruins like the Acropolis with a lively modern city of tavernas, rooftop bars, and vibrant neighborhoods. Visit in spring or autumn for mild temperatures and manageable crowds, while summer offers hot, dry days ideal for early-morning sightseeing and island escapes.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Acropolis and Parthenon — The iconic hilltop citadel crowned by the 5th-century BC Parthenon, offering sweeping views over Athens.
  2. Acropolis Museum — A sleek modern museum displaying the archaeological treasures of the Acropolis with glass floors revealing excavation sites below.
  3. Ancient Agora — The former heart of civic life, featuring the remarkably preserved Temple of Hephaestus and a shaded archaeological park.
  4. Plaka — A charming old neighborhood of neoclassical houses, bougainvillea-lined lanes, and traditional tavernas beneath the Acropolis.
  5. Mount Lycabettus — Athens' highest point, reached by funicular or a steep walk, with panoramic views of the city and sea.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Euro (EUR, €)
  • Language: Greek (English is widely spoken in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: EET (UTC+2), EEST (UTC+3) in summer
  • Visa: Many international visitors, including EU, US, UK, and Canadian citizens, can enter Greece visa-free for short stays, but other nationalities should check Schengen visa requirements before travel.

FAQ

How many days should I spend in Athens?
Two to three days is enough to cover the Acropolis, major archaeological sites, and central neighborhoods, though longer stays allow day trips to Cape Sounion or nearby islands.
Is Athens walkable?
The historic center is very walkable, with major sights clustered around the Acropolis and Syntagma Square, and the metro is efficient for longer distances.
What should I avoid in Athens?
Avoid peak midday heat in summer, be cautious of pickpockets on crowded metro lines and in tourist areas, and skip overpriced restaurants directly on the main tourist squares.
