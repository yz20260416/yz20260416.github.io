Local Character

Athens is often called the cradle of Western civilisation, and the Parthenon on the Acropolis remains its defining landmark — a key touchstone of early Western culture that rewards an early-morning visit before tour groups arrive. The city's name is tied to a quirk of mythology: legend says it was named after Athena, goddess of wisdom, though modern scholars generally agree the goddess took her name after the city — a detail that adds nuance to every Athena reference you'll encounter. Athens also holds two UNESCO World Heritage Sites, the Acropolis and the medieval Daphni Monastery; the latter sits well off the standard tourist circuit, so a visit offers a quieter counterpoint to the crowded classical core. For museum-goers, the National Archaeological Museum houses the world's largest collection of ancient Greek antiquities, making it worth dedicating a half-day. The historical urban core shows continuity through millennia of history, layering Roman, Byzantine and a smaller number of Ottoman monuments alongside the classical heritage — walking these streets reveals how many civilisations shaped daily life here.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Acropolis of Athens — A UNESCO World Heritage Site crowned by the Parthenon, considered a key landmark of early Western culture.
  2. National Archaeological Museum — Home to the world's largest collection of ancient Greek antiquities.
  3. Acropolis Museum — A modern museum housing finds from the Acropolis site, complementing a visit to the hill itself.
  4. Daphni Monastery — A medieval monastery and UNESCO World Heritage Site located away from the main tourist circuit.
  5. Benaki Museum — One of the city's major cultural institutions, tracing Greek art and history across the centuries.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Euro (EUR, €)
  • Language: Greek (English is widely spoken in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: EET (UTC+2), EEST (UTC+3) in summer
  • Visa: Greece is in the Schengen Area, so many international visitors can enter visa-free for short stays while others need a Schengen visa — check your nationality's requirements before travel.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit Athens?
April, May, September and October offer mild temperatures and moderate crowds, making them the most comfortable months for sightseeing.
How many UNESCO World Heritage Sites are in Athens?
Two: the Acropolis of Athens and the medieval Daphni Monastery.
Which museum should I prioritize for ancient Greek antiquities?
The National Archaeological Museum holds the world's largest collection of ancient Greek antiquities and is the top choice for classical artefacts.
