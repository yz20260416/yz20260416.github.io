Best Time to Visit Los Angeles

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are January and February. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

Los Angeles delivers warm, dry weather for most of the year, but the real data reveals clear winners and losers for travelers. August is the hottest month (avg high 32.0°C, low 17.8°C) with almost no rain (1.0 rain day), making it ideal for beaches but intense for sightseeing. January is the coolest and one of the wettest (avg high 18.7°C, 5.4 rain days), so pack layers and a rain jacket. The sweet spot is April through June: highs of 23.7–28.0°C, lows of 10.8–15.2°C, and just 0.6–1.8 rain days per month. Plan outdoor activities then, and expect peak crowds and prices in July–August.

Practical tips

  • Book April–June or September–October for the best mix of warm temperatures, low rain days, and manageable crowds.
  • Carry layers year-round: coastal mornings and evenings run 5–10°C cooler than inland areas like the Valley.
  • Schedule rain-sensitive plans (hikes, beach days) for April–October, when rain days drop to 0.4–2.2 per month.
  • Visit in January, February, or November for lower prices and lighter crowds, but keep indoor backup plans ready.
  • In July–August, start outdoor activities early, use sunscreen, and plan beach or shaded afternoon breaks during peak heat.
