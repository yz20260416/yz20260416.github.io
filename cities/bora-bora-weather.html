Bora Bora Weather Guide: When to Go, Month by Month

Bora Bora's data shows a clear split between the wetter first half of the year and the drier austral winter. August is the standout month, with the fewest rain days (11.8) and slightly cooler highs of 23.4°C, making it the most reliable window for lagoon activities and beach time. January is the wettest by far, averaging 26.2 rain days, so expect frequent downpours and plan flexible indoor alternatives. Temperatures stay remarkably stable year-round (highs of 23.4–25.6°C), so rainfall, not heat, is the deciding factor. For the best balance of dry weather and comfortable conditions, aim for July through September.

Practical tips

  • Target August or September for the fewest rain days (11.8 and 15.0) and the most reliable outdoor conditions.
  • Pack a light rain jacket and quick-dry clothing year-round—even the driest months still see some showers.
  • Temperatures are stable (highs 23.4–25.6°C), so pack for warm, humid weather rather than seasonal cold.
  • Book July–August and the December holidays well in advance, as these coincide with peak crowds and prices.
  • If visiting January–April, choose refundable or flexible bookings and keep indoor and water-based backup plans.
