Bora Bora Weather by Month

Bora Bora enjoys a tropical climate with warm temperatures year-round, making it a desirable destination in any season. However, the island experiences two distinct seasons: a wet season (November through April) and a dry season (May through October). Understanding these patterns is essential for planning your trip, as rainfall, humidity, and crowd levels vary significantly throughout the year. The table below breaks down average high temperatures, low temperatures, and rainfall by month to help you choose the ideal time for your visit.

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall (weather & activities): May through October, when the dry season brings lower humidity, gentle trade winds, and minimal rainfall. August is the driest and most reliable month for sunshine.
  • Best for budget travelers: November through April (excluding the December holidays). The wet season offers lower resort rates and fewer crowds, though you should expect short, heavy afternoon showers.
  • Best for honeymooners and romance: June, September, and October balance excellent weather with slightly quieter resorts compared to the July–August peak.
  • Busiest months: July, August, and the December holiday period — book accommodations and excursions well in advance.
  • Months to avoid (if rain bothers you): January and February, which are the wettest and most humid, with the highest chance of prolonged showers.

Practical Tips

  • Pack for rain year-round: Even in the dry season, brief tropical showers are possible. Bring a lightweight rain jacket or compact umbrella, and quick-dry clothing.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen: Bora Bora enforces reef-safe sunscreen regulations to protect its lagoon. Choose mineral-based products and reapply often — the tropical sun is intense.
  • Book water activities early in the day: Morning hours typically offer calmer seas, better visibility for snorkeling and diving, and lower chances of afternoon cloud cover.
  • Plan around the wet season: If visiting November–April, schedule boat tours and lagoon excursions in the morning and keep afternoons flexible for spa time or indoor dining.
  • Watch for cyclone season: November through April is also the South Pacific cyclone season. While direct hits are rare, travel insurance with weather-related coverage is strongly recommended.
  • Consider humidity: The wet season brings higher humidity (often 80%+). Lightweight, breathable fabrics and air-conditioned accommodations will keep you comfortable.
  • Check lagoon visibility: Rainfall can temporarily reduce underwater visibility in the lagoon. Dry-season months (especially August–September) offer the clearest conditions for diving and snorkeling.
  • Respect peak-season pricing: July–August and December holidays see the highest rates. Booking 6–12 months ahead secures better prices and availability.
