Local Character

New York's identity is inseparable from Manhattan, which is both a borough and New York County, and which the US Postal Service addresses simply as 'New York' — a quirk that means mail sent to 'New York, NY' never technically names the city. Visitors should care because it explains why locals say they live 'in the city' rather than naming a borough. The city's cultural life has long been chronicled from within: New York magazine, founded in 1968, shaped the voice of urban journalism, while Ric Burns's documentary film 'New York' traced the city's history on screen. Anthony Burgess's 1976 book 'New York' and Paul Morand's 1930 travel book of the same name show how writers have long treated the city as a subject of observation rather than mere backdrop. Edward Rutherfurd's 2009 historical novel 'New York' likewise follows the city across generations. These works give visitors a layered sense of how the city has been imagined and recorded, from the colonial Province of New York through the modern metropolis.

FAQ

Why is Manhattan sometimes addressed simply as 'New York'?
New York County covers the same area as the borough of Manhattan, and the US Postal Service uses 'New York' as the address for Manhattan.
Which boroughs make up New York City?
The city has five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island.
When is the best time to visit New York?
May and September through October offer the most comfortable weather, while December draws crowds for holiday displays.
