Best Time to Visit New York

New York is a dense, endlessly varied city of five boroughs, world-class museums, theatre, and neighborhoods that reward wandering as much as sightseeing. Visit in spring or autumn for mild weather and pleasant days, though the holiday season and summer each offer their own distinct energy.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Central Park — A vast 843-acre green space offering lakes, woodland trails, and iconic views of the surrounding skyline.
  2. The Metropolitan Museum of Art — One of the world's largest art museums, spanning 5,000 years of global culture across its Fifth Avenue galleries.
  3. Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island — Ferry trips from Battery Park reach the famous copper monument and the immigration museum tracing America's immigrant history.
  4. Top of the Rock — The Rockefeller Center observation deck delivers sweeping, unobstructed views of the Empire State Building and Central Park.
  5. Brooklyn Bridge — A landmark 19th-century suspension bridge with a pedestrian walkway offering classic views of Lower Manhattan.

Practical Information

  • Currency: US Dollar (USD, $)
  • Language: English (widely spoken; multilingual services common in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: EST (UTC-5), EDT (UTC-4) in summer
  • Visa: Most international visitors need a US visa or ESTA approval under the Visa Waiver Program, so check requirements well before travel.

FAQ

How do I get around New York?
The subway runs 24/7 and is the fastest way between boroughs; walking and yellow cabs or rideshares work well for short trips.
Is tipping expected?
Yes, tipping is customary: around 15-20% at restaurants and a dollar or two per drink or bag handled.
When is the best time to visit?
Late April to June and September to October offer the most comfortable weather, though these periods are also the busiest.
