Best Time to Visit Jinan

Based on 5 years of historical weather data, the optimal travel windows are March and April. Expect lower rainfall and comfortable temperatures. Check flight prices for these months →

Jinan's weather rewards timing. Based on 2021–2025 averages, the sweet spots are April–May and September–October, when highs sit comfortably between 20°C and 27°C and rain days stay moderate, making it ideal for exploring Baotu Spring and Daming Lake on foot. The hardest months are July and August: highs hover around 31–32°C, lows stay above 23°C, and July alone averages nearly 14 rain days — expect heat, humidity, and sudden downpours. January and December are the coldest and driest (highs near 5–6°C, lows below freezing, under 2 rain days), which means crisp, quiet sightseeing but a real need for warm layers.

Practical tips

  • Aim for April–May or September–October for the best balance of mild temperatures and manageable rain.
  • Avoid July–August if you dislike heat and humidity; if you must go, schedule outdoor sights before 10am and keep indoor backups for afternoon storms.
  • Carry a compact umbrella or rain shell from June through September, when rain days climb sharply (July peaks at ~14 days).
  • Winter visits (December–February) need proper cold-weather gear — lows drop below freezing — but reward you with thin crowds.
  • Spring (March–May) and autumn bring large day–night temperature swings (often 10°C+), so dress in layers.
