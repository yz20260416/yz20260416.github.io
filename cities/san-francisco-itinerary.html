San Francisco packs an extraordinary amount into 49 square miles: iconic bridges, historic cable cars, world-class museums, diverse neighborhoods, and some of the best food in the country. This 3-day itinerary balances the must-see landmarks with neighborhood exploring and great eating, organized by geography so you spend less time in transit and more time enjoying the city. It works for first-time visitors and assumes you'll use a mix of walking, public transit (Muni), and ride-shares. Book Alcatraz and any museum tickets in advance, and dress in layers—Karl the Fog is real.

Day 1: Fisherman's Wharf, Alcatraz & the Northern Waterfront

Time Activity Notes
8:00 AM Breakfast at Boudin Bakery (Fisherman's Wharf) Try the clam chowder in a sourdough bread bowl—the original 1849 bakery.
9:00 AM Ferry to Alcatraz Island Book the official Alcatraz City Cruises tickets weeks ahead. The Cellhouse audio tour (included) is excellent. Allow 2.5–3 hours round trip.
12:30 PM Lunch at Pier 39 or the Ferry Building Pier 39 has sea lions and casual eats; the Ferry Building Marketplace (Hog Island Oyster Co., Gott's Roadside) is the better food choice.
2:00 PM Walk the Embarcadero to the Ferry Building Flat, scenic waterfront walk with bay and Bay Bridge views.
3:30 PM Ride a cable car (Powell–Hyde line) to Union Square Board at the Hyde & Beach turnaround near Ghirardelli Square; hang off the outside rail if you dare.
5:00 PM Explore Union Square & shop Optional detour to Chinatown via the Dragon Gate on Grant Avenue.
7:00 PM Dinner in Chinatown Try Z&Y Restaurant (Sichuan) or House of Nanking. Finish with a drink at Li Po Cocktail Lounge.

Day 2: Golden Gate Bridge, Presidio & Golden Gate Park

Time Activity Notes
8:00 AM Breakfast at Arsicault Bakery (Inner Richmond) Widely regarded as one of the best croissants in the U.S.
9:00 AM Walk or bike the Golden Gate Bridge Start at Crissy Field or the Welcome Center; the 1.7-mile crossing takes ~45 min each way. Bike rentals available near Fisherman's Wharf.
11:30 AM Explore the Presidio Visit the Palace of Fine Arts and grab coffee at Equator Coffees.
12:30 PM Lunch at The Mill or Nopa The Mill ( Divisadero) for excellent toast and sandwiches; Nopa for a sit-down brunch.
2:00 PM Golden Gate Park: California Academy of Sciences or de Young Museum Pick one—both are superb. The Academy has a living roof and planetarium; de Young has fine art and the Observation Tower (free).
4:30 PM Stroll the Japanese Tea Garden & Stow Lake Peaceful, photogenic, and close together within the park.
6:30 PM Sunset at Ocean Beach or Sutro Baths Dramatic coastal views; bring a windbreaker.
7:30 PM Dinner in the Inner Sunset San Tung (famous dry-fried chicken wings) or Nopa for California-Mediterranean.

Day 3: Neighborhoods, Views & Culture

Time Activity Notes
8:30 AM Breakfast at Tartine Bakery (Mission District) Legendary morning buns and croissants; go early to beat the line.
9:30 AM Mission District street art walk See the murals of Balmy Alley and Clarion Alley; grab a burrito at La Taqueria or El Farolito for an early lunch.
12:00 PM Ride to Dolores Park Classic city views and people-watching; optional Bi-Rite Creamery ice cream.
1:30 PM Painted Ladies at Alamo Square The postcard view of Victorian homes with downtown behind them.
2:30 PM Haight-Ashbury & Golden Gate Park edge Vintage shops, record stores, and Summer of Love history.
4:00 PM Ride to Lombard Street & North Beach See the famous crooked block, then coffee at Caffè Trieste and a walk through Washington Square.
5:30 PM Sunset at Coit Tower or Pioneer Park
