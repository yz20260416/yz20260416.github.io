Zhangjiajie Travel Weather Guide

Zhangjiajie's five-year averages show a classic subtropical monsoon pattern: hot, humid summers and cool, damp winters. The standout month is October, with comfortable highs near 23°C, lows around 16°C, and only about 9 rain days — ideal for hiking the sandstone pillars with clear views. The toughest month is June, combining highs near 30°C with roughly 16 rain days, meaning frequent downpours, slippery trails, and cloud-shrouded viewpoints. In practice, aim for autumn (September–November) or late spring for the best balance of visibility, comfort, and manageable crowds, and pack rain gear year-round since even "dry" months still see regular showers.

Practical tips

  • Pack for rain year-round: even the driest months average several rain days, so bring a waterproof shell, dry bag, and quick-dry layers.
  • Target autumn for views: September–November offers the best mix of comfortable temperatures, fewer rain days, and clearer mountain visibility.
  • Avoid June for hiking: it is the wettest month (about 16 rain days), with slippery trails and frequent cloud cover hiding the peaks.
  • Start early in summer: July and August hit 33°C with high humidity — hike at dawn and rest during the hottest midday hours.
  • Layer up in winter: January and February lows hover near 3–4°C with damp conditions; warm, waterproof clothing and grippy footwear are essential.
