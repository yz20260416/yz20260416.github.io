Paris offers a temperate climate with four distinct seasons, and the data reveals clear windows for visiting. August stands out as the best-weather month, pairing the year's highest average highs (25.1°C) with its lowest rain-day count (9.4), meaning warm, relatively dry days ideal for strolling the Seine and lingering in parks. November and October are the wettest (12.8 rain days each), while January is the coldest (highs of just 7.1°C, lows of 1.6°C). Practically, this means packing light layers and a compact umbrella for spring and autumn trips, and choosing August or early summer if you want the greatest odds of dry, comfortable sightseeing weather.

Practical tips

  • Rain is a near-constant in Paris—every month averages 9+ rainy days—so always pack a compact umbrella or waterproof jacket, even in summer.
  • Layer your clothing: mornings and evenings run 6–10°C cooler than daytime highs, especially in spring and autumn.
  • Target August or the June–September window for the warmest, driest conditions, but book accommodations and major attractions well in advance due to peak crowds.
  • Choose January, February, or November for the lowest crowds and cheapest rates if you can tolerate cold and wet weather.
  • Build flexibility into your itinerary—keep a list of indoor backups (museums, galleries, covered passages) for rain-affected days.
