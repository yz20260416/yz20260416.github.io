Paris Weather by Month

Paris enjoys a temperate oceanic climate with four distinct seasons, but its weather is famously changeable — a sunny morning can turn into a drizzly afternoon. Temperatures rarely drop below freezing or soar above 30°C, making the city pleasant year-round. Rainfall is moderate and fairly evenly distributed, though late spring and summer bring the most sunshine.
