Best Time to Visit Seoul

Seoul is South Korea's dynamic capital, where ancient palaces, bustling markets, and cutting-edge technology coexist. Visit in spring (April–May) for cherry blossoms or autumn (September–October) for crisp air and colorful foliage.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Gyeongbokgung Palace — The largest and most iconic royal palace of the Joseon Dynasty, offering changing of the guard ceremonies and beautiful architecture.
  2. Bukchon Hanok Village — A picturesque neighborhood of traditional Korean houses (hanoks) nestled between two palaces, ideal for a cultural stroll.
  3. N Seoul Tower — Located on Namsan Mountain, this landmark offers panoramic city views and a famous love-lock terrace.
  4. Myeongdong Shopping Street — A vibrant shopping district packed with cosmetics stores, fashion boutiques, and street food vendors.
  5. Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) — A futuristic architectural landmark by Zaha Hadid, hosting exhibitions, design shops, and events.

Practical Information

  • Currency: South Korean Won (KRW, ₩)
  • Language: Korean (English signage is common in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: KST (UTC+9)
  • Visa: Most international visitors can enter visa-free for short stays, but check requirements based on your nationality.

FAQ

What is the best time to visit Seoul?
Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer the most pleasant weather and beautiful scenery.
How do I get around Seoul?
The subway system is extensive, affordable, and English-friendly; taxis and buses are also convenient.
Is English widely spoken in Seoul?
English is not widely spoken but is common in tourist areas, hotels, and major attractions.
