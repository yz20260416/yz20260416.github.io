Best Time to Visit Seoul

Seoul is South Korea's capital and largest city, a mountainous metropolis where Joseon-era palaces and fortress walls sit beside towers like Lotte World Tower and the Dongdaemun Design Plaza. Visit in spring or autumn for the mildest weather, when the hills and palace grounds are at their best.

Local Character

Seoul's character is shaped by geography and dynasty alike: the city is set in mountainous, hilly terrain with Bukhansan on its northern edge, so hiking trails and granite peaks are part of everyday urban life rather than a day-trip afterthought — visitors should pack walking shoes and take a ridge hike for views that explain the city's layout. The Joseon dynasty designated Seoul as its capital and ringed it with the Fortress Wall of Seoul, segments of which still trace the old city boundary; walking a stretch of the wall lets you read the city's pre-modern shape against the modern skyline. The city's origins reach back to 18 BC, when it was founded by the people of Baekje, one of the Three Kingdoms of Korea, a detail that deepens any visit to its older neighborhoods. Within the capital area lie UNESCO World Heritage Sites including Changdeokgung, Jongmyo, and the Royal Tombs of the Joseon dynasty, each rewarding visitors who want the ceremonial and architectural layers behind the modern city.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Changdeokgung — A UNESCO World Heritage Joseon palace known for its harmonious layout and rear garden.
  2. Bukhansan — A granite mountain on the city's northern edge offering hiking trails and sweeping views over Seoul.
  3. N Seoul Tower — An iconic landmark on Namsan with observation decks overlooking the city.
  4. Dongdaemun Design Plaza — A striking modern architectural landmark and design hub in the Dongdaemun area.
  5. Jongmyo — A UNESCO World Heritage royal ancestral shrine preserving Joseon ceremonial architecture.

FAQ

When is the best time to visit Seoul?
Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the mildest weather and the best conditions for sightseeing and hiking.
How many days should I spend in Seoul?
Three to five days allows time for major palaces, a mountain hike, and modern districts such as Gangnam and Dongdaemun.
Is Seoul walkable for visitors?
Many central areas are walkable, and the extensive subway system makes it easy to reach attractions across the city.
