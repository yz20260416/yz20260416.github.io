Yangshuo Travel Guide: Top 10 Must-See Attractions

Yangshuo, a small county town in Guangxi, China, is famous for its dramatic karst limestone peaks, the winding Li River, and its relaxed countryside cycling routes. Once a quiet backpacker stop, it now offers a mix of natural scenery, outdoor activities, and cultural experiences. The attractions below are listed roughly in order of popularity and are best explored over three to five days. Ticket prices and hours can change seasonally, so confirm current details on official channels before visiting.

  1. Li River Cruise (Yangshuo Section)

    The classic Yangshuo experience is a boat trip on the Li River, passing the famous karst peaks depicted on the 20-yuan banknote near Xingping. Most travelers take the cruise from Guilin's Zhujiang Pier to Yangshuo (about 4 hours), while shorter bamboo raft segments operate from Xingping and Yangdi.

    TicketGuilin–Yangshuo cruise: roughly CNY 210–450 depending on boat class; Xingping bamboo raft segments: about CNY 120–220 per person
    HoursDepartures typically 8:30–10:00 AM; rafting roughly 8:00 AM–5:00 PM
    TipsBook a day ahead in peak season, sit on the upper deck for views, and bring sun protection. The Xingping–Nine Horse Fresco Hill raft route offers the best scenery for a shorter trip.

  2. West Street (Xi Jie)

    Yangshuo's oldest and liveliest street, lined with cafes, bars, souvenir shops, and restaurants. It is the town's social hub by day and night and a convenient base for exploring the surrounding area.

    TicketFree
    HoursOpen 24 hours; shops generally 9:00 AM–11:00 PM
    TipsVisit early morning to see the street without crowds. Bargain politely at souvenir stalls, and expect higher prices at riverside bars.

  3. Yulong River (Yulong He)

    A quieter tributary of the Li River, ideal for bamboo rafting and cycling through rice paddies and villages. The stretch between Jinlong Bridge and Gongnong Bridge is the most scenic.

    TicketBamboo raft (2 persons): roughly CNY 240–280 per raft; cycling is free aside from bike rental (about CNY 30–50/day)
    HoursRafting generally 8:00 AM–5:30 PM
    TipsRaft prices are per boat, not per person. Go in the morning for calmer water and softer light. Rent a bike in town and follow the marked riverside paths.

  4. Xingping Ancient Town

    A 1,700-year-old riverside town about 25 km from Yangshuo, known for its old streets and as the viewpoint for the 20-yuan note scenery. It is also the departure point for Li River raft trips.

    TicketFree to enter town; Laozhai Hill viewpoint about CNY 20–30
    HoursTown open all day; viewpoint roughly 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
    TipsClimb Laozhai Hill in the late afternoon for sunset over the river bend. Buses from Yangshuo take about 45 minutes.

  5. Moon Hill (Yueliang Shan)

    A karst peak with a natural arch near the top, reached by a steep stairway of about 800 steps. The reward is panoramic views over the countryside.

    TicketAbout CNY 30–40
    HoursRoughly 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
    TipsAllow 1–1.5 hours round trip and bring water. Early morning or late afternoon is cooler and better for photos.

  6. Reed Flute Cave (Ludi Yan)

    A large limestone cave with colorful lighting and dramatic stalactites and stalagmites. It is a popular stop for travelers combining Yangshuo with Guilin.

    TicketAbout CNY 90
    HoursRoughly 8:00 AM–5:30 PM
    TipsThe cave is about 1 hour from Yangshuo by road, so plan it as part of a Guilin day trip. The interior can be slippery; wear sturdy shoes.

  7. Xianggong Hill (Xianggong Shan)

    One of the best sunrise and sunset viewpoints in the region, overlooking the Li River's most photographed bend. The climb is short but steep.

    TicketAbout CNY 60
    HoursRoughly 6:00 AM–6:30 PM
    TipsArrive 30–45 minutes before sunrise for a good spot on the viewing platforms. Bring a flashlight for the pre-dawn climb.

  8. Fuli Ancient Town

    A small, less commercialized old town on the Li River, known for its traditional painted fans and quiet lanes. It offers an authentic contrast to West Street.

    TicketFree
    HoursOpen all day; shops generally 9:00 AM–5:00 PM
    TipsVisit on a weekday for a quieter experience. Painted fans make good souvenirs; buy directly from workshops.

  9. Silver Cave (Yinzi Yan)

    A multi-level karst cave about 20 km south of Yangshuo, featuring large chambers, underground pools, and elaborate lighting displays.

    TicketAbout CNY 65–90
    HoursRoughly 8:30 AM–5:30 PM
    TipsTours are guided and take about 1 hour. Combine with a countryside cycling route for a half-day trip.

  10. Impression Sanjie Liu Night Show

    An open-air performance staged on the Li River, directed by Zhang Yimou, with hundreds of performers and dramatic lighting against the karst backdrop.

    TicketAbout CNY 200–700 depending on seat class
    HoursUsually one or two evening shows, around 7:30–9:30 PM; schedule varies by season
    TipsBook tickets through your hotel or official channels. Bring a light jacket for the riverside seating and arrive early for the best entry flow.

Practical Tips

  • Best time to visit: April–May and September–October offer mild weather and green scenery; July–August is hot and rainy, and winter is cooler but quieter.
  • Getting around: Rent a bicycle or e-bike in Yangshuo town (about CNY 30–80 per day) for countryside routes; taxis and ride-hailing apps cover longer trips.
  • Budget: Attraction tickets add up quickly; budget roughly CNY 300–600 per person for the main sights, excluding transport and the Li River cruise.
  • Weather: Carry rain gear year-round; karst scenery is often misty, which can be atmospheric but may obscure viewpoints.
  • Booking: Reserve Li River cruises and the Impression show in advance during holidays; smaller sites can be visited on arrival.
  • Etiquette: Bargaining is common at markets but not in fixed-price shops; carry cash for small vendors and raft operators.
