Yangshuo Top 10 Must-See Attractions

Yangshuo, a small county town in Guangxi, China, is famous for its karst limestone peaks, the winding Li River, and relaxed countryside cycling routes. It serves as a base for exploring some of the most photographed scenery in the country. Below are the top 10 attractions, with practical details to help you plan your visit. Prices and hours can change seasonally, so confirm locally before you go.

  1. Li River Cruise (Xingping Section)

    The classic Yangshuo experience: a scenic boat ride past dramatic karst peaks, bamboo groves, and the famous landscape featured on the 20-yuan banknote near Xingping. Boats typically run from Zhujiang Pier or Yangdi to Xingping.

    Ticket price: About CNY 210–270 for the main cruise (varies by boat class and route); shorter Xingping–Yangdi bamboo raft trips around CNY 120–200.

    Opening hours: Departures generally 9:00–14:00, with morning departures most common.

    Tips: Book a day ahead in peak season, arrive early, and sit on the upper deck for views. Bring sun protection and cash for snacks.

  2. West Street (Xi Jie)

    Yangshuo's oldest and liveliest street, packed with cafes, bars, souvenir shops, and guesthouses. It blends traditional architecture with a busy tourist atmosphere and is best explored in the evening.

    Ticket price: Free.

    Opening hours: Open 24 hours; shops generally 9:00–23:00.

    Tips: Visit early morning for quieter photos; expect higher prices and crowds at night. Bargain politely in souvenir shops.

  3. Yulong River

    A quieter tributary of the Li River, ideal for bamboo rafting, cycling, and countryside walks. The scenery includes rice paddies, water buffalo, and old stone bridges such as the Dragon Bridge.

    Ticket price: Free to visit; bamboo raft rides about CNY 200–240 per raft (2 people).

    Opening hours: Rafting generally 8:00–17:00; riverside paths open all day.

    Tips: Rent a bicycle or e-bike in town (about CNY 30–80/day) and ride the riverside route. Go early to avoid heat and crowds.

  4. Xianggong Hill

    One of the best viewpoints in the region, overlooking the Li River's grand bend. A short but steep climb of roughly 15–20 minutes rewards you with panoramic karst scenery, especially at sunrise and sunset.

    Ticket price: About CNY 60.

    Opening hours: Roughly 6:00–18:00.

    Tips: Bring water and wear sturdy shoes; the steps can be slippery after rain. Arrive early at sunrise for the best light and fewer people.

  5. Xingping Ancient Town

    A historic riverside town with old streets, traditional houses, and the iconic 20-yuan note viewpoint nearby. It is a convenient base for Li River scenery and photography.

    Ticket price: Free to enter the town; some viewpoints and boats charge separately.

    Opening hours: Open all day; shops roughly 8:00–20:00.

    Tips: Visit the 20-yuan viewpoint at Laozhai Hill (small fee) for classic photos. Weekday visits are quieter.

  6. Moon Hill

    A limestone peak with a natural arch near the top, offering a short climb and views over the countryside. It is a popular stop on cycling routes south of town.

    Ticket price: About CNY 30.

    Opening hours: Roughly 8:00–17:30.

    Tips: The climb takes 30–45 minutes; bring water and avoid midday heat. Combine with nearby Yulong River cycling.

  7. Reed Flute Cave (Ludi Yan)

    A large karst cave with colorful lighting and impressive stalactites and stalagmites. It is a popular rainy-day activity and a short drive from Yangshuo town.

    Ticket price: About CNY 90.

    Opening hours: Roughly 8:00–17:30.

    Tips: Bring a light jacket; the cave is cool inside. Guided tours are included and last about an hour.

  8. Fuli Ancient Town

    A small, less commercial town known for its traditional painted fans and riverside setting. It offers a quieter glimpse of local life and crafts.

    Ticket price: Free.

    Opening hours: Open all day; fan workshops roughly 9:00–17:00.

    Tips: Visit on market days for local produce; buy fans directly from workshops for better prices.

  9. Butterfly Spring (Yulong River Area)

    A scenic spot combining a cave, spring, and hillside views, often paired with Yulong River cycling. The site includes a short cave walk and lookout points.

    Ticket price: About CNY 55–75.

    Opening hours: Roughly 8:00–17:30.

    Tips: It is a modest attraction; go if you are already cycling the area. Skip if time is short.

  10. Impression Sanjie Liu Night Show

    An outdoor evening performance directed by Zhang Yimou, staged on the Li River with mountains as the backdrop. It features hundreds of performers and dramatic lighting.

    Ticket price: About CNY 198–680 depending on seating.

    Opening hours: Evening shows, typically 19:30–21:00 (seasonal).

    Tips: Book tickets in advance, especially in peak season; bring insect repellent and a light layer for cool evenings.

Practical Tips

  • Best time to visit: April–October for green scenery and river activities; spring and autumn offer the most comfortable weather. Avoid major Chinese holidays if you prefer fewer crowds.
  • Getting around: Rent a bicycle or e-bike for countryside routes; taxis and ride-hailing are available for longer trips. Many attractions are within 10–30 minutes of town.
  • Money: Carry some cash for small vendors and raft operators; larger businesses accept mobile payments.
  • Weather: Summers are hot and humid with occasional rain; pack sun protection, rain gear, and comfortable walking shoes.
  • Etiquette: Bargain politely at markets, ask before photographing locals, and respect countryside property when cycling.
  • Verify details: Ticket prices and opening hours change seasonally—confirm at your hotel or the official ticket office before traveling to an attraction.
