Hangzhou Travel Budget Guide: Costs for Backpackers, Mid-Range, and Luxury Travelers

Hangzhou, the capital of Zhejiang Province and one of China's most celebrated scenic cities, rewards visitors with West Lake, ancient temples, tea plantations, and a polished modern infrastructure. Because it sits on a fast rail line just an hour from Shanghai, prices are moderate by Chinese big-city standards but higher than in inland provinces. The breakdown below covers daily costs for three travel styles, assuming one person and a mix of shared and private spending. Figures are in Chinese yuan (CNY) with approximate USD equivalents (1 USD ≈ 7.2 CNY).

Accommodation Notes

  • Backpackers: Hostels cluster near West Lake's east shore and around Wushan Square. Dorms run CNY 60–90; private rooms in guesthouses start around CNY 150.
  • Mid-range: Book 3–4 star hotels near Longxiangqiao or along the lake's southern edge. Weekend rates rise sharply, so reserve ahead.
  • Luxury: Lakeside five-star properties (Amanfayun, Four Seasons, Grand Hyatt) charge premium rates, especially during holidays and the autumn osmanthus season.

Food Costs in Detail

  • Street and casual: A bowl of noodles or a steamed bun costs CNY 10–25; a full street-food meal CNY 30–50.
  • Local restaurants: Hangzhou classics such as Dongpo pork or West Lake fish run CNY 40–90 per dish; a two-person meal with tea is roughly CNY 150–250.
  • Fine dining: Top restaurants and hotel tables charge CNY 300–800 per person; signature tea-house experiences add CNY 100–300.

Transport Costs

  • Metro fares: CNY 2–8 per ride; a transportation card or mobile payment app speeds up entry.
  • Public buses: CNY 2–5; shared bikes: about CNY 1.5 per 30 minutes.
  • Taxis and DiDi: base fare around CNY 11, typical cross-town trip CNY 25–45.
  • High-speed rail from Shanghai: CNY 73–120 one way; Hangzhou East Station connects to the metro.
  • Airport shuttle to downtown: about CNY 20; taxi from Xiaoshan Airport: CNY 100–130.

Activity Costs

  • West Lake walking and cycling: free; boat rides CNY 55–150 depending on route.
  • Lingyin Temple: CNY 45 temple entry plus CNY 30 for the Feilai Feng grottoes.
  • Leifeng Pagoda: CNY 40; Six Harmonies Pagoda: CNY 20.
  • Longjing tea village: free to wander; tea tastings CNY 50–150.
  • Impression West Lake show: CNY 260–680 per seat.
  • Xixi Wetland Park: CNY 80 entry plus optional boat fees.

Practical Tips

  • Best value season: Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the best weather; summer is hot and humid, winter is quiet and cheapest.
  • Book ahead for holidays: Rates triple during Golden Week (early October) and Chinese New Year; reserve accommodation and trains weeks in advance.
  • Payments: Alipay and WeChat Pay are near-universal; link an international card before arrival. Carry some cash for small vendors.
  • Free sights: The West Lake loop, Hefang Street, and many temples' outer grounds cost nothing and are among the city's highlights.
  • Getting around: The metro is fast and cheap; bikes are ideal for the lake's causeways, but avoid cycling on crowded weekend paths.
  • Budget saver: Eat where locals do, stay slightly off the lake, and use public transport—backpackers can comfortably manage on under US$50 per day.
