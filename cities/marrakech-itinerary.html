Marrakech 3-Day Itinerary

Marrakech is a sensory explosion of colour, scent, and sound — a city where ancient medina alleys open onto bustling souks, where the call to prayer mingles with the beat of Gnawa drums, and where every rooftop offers a view of the snow-capped Atlas Mountains. This carefully crafted 3-day itinerary balances the must-see highlights with hidden corners, and includes hand-picked restaurant recommendations so you can taste the very best of Moroccan cuisine. Whether it's your first visit or a return, these three days will leave you enchanted.

Day-by-Day Schedule & Activities

Day Time Activity Restaurant / Food Stop
Day 1
Medina & Souks		 8:30 AM Breakfast at a riad terrace, then start at Jemaa el-Fnaa before the crowds. Watch the square wake up with fresh orange juice stalls. Café des Épices (spiced coffee & msemen)
10:00 AM Explore Koutoubia Mosque gardens (exterior only for non-Muslims) and stroll through the Mellah (old Jewish quarter). —
12:30 PM Lunch in the medina, then dive into the souks — start at Souk Semmarine for textiles, then navigate to Souk Haddadine (blacksmiths) and Souk Sebbaghine (dyers). Nomad (rooftop, modern Moroccan)
3:30 PM Visit Le Jardin Secret (restored 19th-century garden) or Bahia Palace for intricate zellige tilework. —
7:30 PM Return to Jemaa el-Fnaa at dusk — the square transforms into a carnival of food stalls, musicians, and storytellers. Dinner overlooking the spectacle. Café Argana (terrace view) or Chez Chegrouni (budget, local)
