Best Time to Visit Zanzibar

Zanzibar is a semi-autonomous Tanzanian archipelago whose main island, Unguja, is famed for powder-white beaches, turquoise Indian Ocean waters, and the UNESCO-listed Stone Town. Visit during the dry seasons of June to October or January to February for the best beach weather and clear diving conditions.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Stone Town — A UNESCO World Heritage maze of narrow alleys, carved wooden doors, and historic Swahili architecture.
  2. Nungwi Beach — A lively northern beach known for calm tide-free swimming, dhow sailing, and sunset bars.
  3. Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park — Zanzibar's only national park, home to the endemic red colobus monkey and mangrove forests.
  4. Prison Island (Changuu) — A small islet off Stone Town where visitors can see giant Aldabra tortoises and snorkel nearby reefs.
  5. The Rock Restaurant — An iconic restaurant perched on a rock in the sea off Michamvi, reached by boat or foot at low tide.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Tanzanian Shilling (TZS); US dollars are widely accepted
  • Language: Swahili and English (English is widely spoken in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: EAT (UTC+3)
  • Visa: Most international visitors need a Tanzanian visa, obtainable online as an e-visa or on arrival, and Zanzibar requires valid yellow fever certification if arriving from an endemic country.

FAQ

Is Zanzibar safe for tourists?
Yes, Zanzibar is generally safe, though travellers should use common sense, avoid isolated beaches at night, and keep valuables secure in Stone Town.
What is the best time to visit Zanzibar?
The dry seasons from June to October and January to February offer the best weather, with June to September being cooler and less humid.
Do I need malaria precautions in Zanzibar?
Yes, malaria is present year-round, so consult a travel doctor about antimalarial medication and use repellent and mosquito nets.
