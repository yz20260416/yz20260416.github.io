Lijiang Travel Costs: Budget Breakdown for Every Traveler

Lijiang, a UNESCO World Heritage town in Yunnan Province, China, draws visitors with its cobblestone old town, Naxi culture, and proximity to Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. Costs vary widely depending on travel style: backpackers can get by on dorm beds and street food, while luxury travelers can enjoy boutique courtyard hotels and private guides. The table below summarizes typical daily costs per person in Chinese yuan (CNY), with approximate US dollar equivalents at roughly 7.2 CNY = 1 USD. Prices are estimates for the main tourist seasons and may rise during Chinese public holidays.

Key Cost Notes

  • Old Town Maintenance Fee: Lijiang previously charged an 80 CNY fee for entering the old town; enforcement has varied over the years, so check current rules on arrival.
  • Jade Dragon Snow Mountain: Entrance, cable car, and shuttle bus typically total 300–500 CNY per person; book ahead in peak season.
  • Accommodation: Courtyard guesthouses are a Lijiang specialty; mid-range options offer the best value. Prices spike during Spring Festival and Golden Week (early October).
  • Food: Naxi specialties such as baba flatbread and mushroom hotpot are affordable at local spots; tourist-facing restaurants in the old town charge more.
  • Transport: Lijiang Sanyi Airport is about 30 km from town; airport shuttle buses cost around 20 CNY, taxis 100–150 CNY.

Practical Tips

  • Carry cash for small vendors and buses; mobile payment (WeChat/Alipay) is widely used but requires a Chinese bank setup or international card support.
  • Book accommodation and mountain tickets in advance during holidays, when prices can double.
  • Stay in Shuhe or Baisha instead of the main old town for quieter, often cheaper guesthouses.
  • Altitude is around 2,400 m; budget for a rest day and plenty of water if you feel the effects.
  • Compare tour prices at official ticket offices rather than street touts to avoid markups.
