Best Time to Visit Nairobi

Nairobi is Kenya's capital and East Africa's financial hub, set at high elevation in the south-central part of the country. Nicknamed the "Green City under the Sun," it is the only capital city in the world with a national park inside its boundaries. Visit during the cooler, drier months for game drives and city exploring.

Local Character

Nairobi's name comes from the Maasai phrase Enkare Nyirobi, meaning "place of cool waters," referring to the cold stream that once flowed through the swampy site — a reminder that today's concrete capital sits on what was once Maasai pastureland. Visitors should care because this origin story explains the city's enduring relationship with water and greenery, visible in its climate and nickname. The city was founded in 1898 as a rail depot on the Uganda–Kenya Railway, chosen by chief engineer Sir George Whitehouse as a resting place because of its high elevation, temperate climate, and adequate water supply before the steep ascent of the Limuru escarpments. A visitor should care because this railway origin shaped the city's layout and explains why Nairobi grew so quickly to replace Mombasa as capital in 1907. The site was originally occupied by Maasai pastoralists, Akamba long-distance traders, and Kikuyu agriculturalists — layers of local life that predate the colonial grid and still inform the city's diverse character.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Nairobi National Park — The only national park within a capital city's boundaries, offering game drives for lions, giraffes, and rhinos against a skyline backdrop.
  2. Kenyan Parliament Buildings — The seat of Kenya's national legislature and a landmark of the capital's civic architecture.
  3. State House — The official residence of Kenya's head of state, set in the capital's historic government quarter.
  4. Supreme Court Building — Home to Kenya's highest judicial authority and a key stop for understanding the city's role as national capital.
  5. Nairobi Securities Exchange — One of Africa's largest and second-oldest stock exchanges, reflecting Nairobi's status as East Africa's financial hub.

FAQ

Why is Nairobi called the "Green City under the Sun"?
The nickname reflects its sunny high-elevation climate and unusual greenery, including the national park within the city.
What does the name Nairobi mean?
It comes from the Maasai phrase Enkare Nyirobi, meaning "place of cool waters," referring to a cold stream that once flowed through the area.
When is the best time to visit Nairobi?
The drier months of January, February, June, and September offer the most comfortable conditions for city sightseeing and wildlife viewing.
