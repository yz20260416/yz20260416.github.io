Best Time to Visit Nairobi

Nairobi is Kenya's vibrant capital, a fast-growing metropolis that doubles as the gateway to East Africa's safari country, with Nairobi National Park sitting right on its doorstep. The city blends world-class wildlife experiences, museums, and lively markets with a cosmopolitan food and arts scene. Visit during the dry seasons from January to February or June to October for the best game viewing, clear skies, and comfortable temperatures.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Nairobi National Park — A unique wildlife reserve just minutes from downtown where lions, rhinos, and giraffes roam against a skyline backdrop.
  2. Sheldrick Wildlife Trust — An elephant orphanage where visitors can watch rescued calves being fed and learn about conservation efforts.
  3. Giraffe Centre — A breeding centre for endangered Rothschild's giraffes where visitors can hand-feed the animals from a raised platform.
  4. Karen Blixen Museum — The beautifully preserved former home of Out of Africa author Karen Blixen, set in lush gardens at the foot of the Ngong Hills.
  5. Kibera and Maasai Markets — Explore vibrant local markets such as Maasai Market for crafts and souvenirs, or take a guided community walk through Kibera.

Practical Information

  • Currency: Kenyan Shilling (KES, KSh)
  • Language: Swahili and English (English is widely spoken in tourist areas)
  • Time Zone: EAT (UTC+3)
  • Visa: Most international visitors need an e-visa or electronic travel authorisation obtained online before arrival, though some nationalities qualify for visa-free entry.

FAQ

Is Nairobi safe for tourists?
Nairobi is generally safe in tourist areas and on organised tours, but visitors should avoid walking alone at night, keep valuables out of sight, and use reputable taxis or ride-hailing apps.
What is the best time to visit Nairobi?
The dry seasons from January to February and June to October offer the best weather and wildlife viewing, while April to May brings heavy rains and fewer crowds.
Do I need a yellow fever vaccination for Nairobi?
A yellow fever certificate is required for travellers arriving from countries with risk of yellow fever transmission, and routine vaccinations plus malaria precautions are recommended for Kenya.
