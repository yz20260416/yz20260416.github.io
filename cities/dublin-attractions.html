Dublin Top 10 Attractions

Dublin is a city where Viking history, literary giants, and warm pub culture collide. From the hallowed halls of Trinity College to the hallowed (and haunted) walls of Kilmainham Gaol, the Irish capital packs centuries of story into a walkable centre. Whether you’re chasing the Book of Kells, a perfect pint of stout, or a windswept coastal view, these ten attractions capture the essence of Dublin. Below you’ll find honest descriptions, current ticket prices (as of 2025), and practical tips to help you skip queues, save money, and make the most of every stop.

Attraction Description Ticket & Tips
1. Guinness Storehouse Ireland’s most visited attraction tells the story of the black stuff across seven floors shaped like a giant pint glass. You’ll learn about brewing, advertising, and the Guinness family, finishing with a complimentary pint in the Gravity Bar – Dublin’s best 360° view. €26–€32 (adult, online saver)
Book online for a 10–15% discount and skip the ticket queue. Go early (10am) or after 5pm to avoid crowds. Gravity Bar pint included.
