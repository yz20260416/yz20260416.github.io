Prague Travel Weather Guide

Prague's weather data reveals a clear split between the city's best and most challenging travel months. June through September delivers the most comfortable conditions, with highs of 21–26°C and lows of 11–15°C, though July stands out as the wettest month at 13.6 rain days—worth packing a compact umbrella even in summer. The toughest stretch is December through February, when highs hover near 4–6°C and lows dip below freezing, with January the coldest at -1.2°C. Practically, this means summer travelers should book early and expect peak crowds, while winter visitors get cheaper rates and quieter sights but need warm layers and waterproof footwear for damp, grey days.

Practical tips

  • Rain is a year-round reality in Prague—every month averages 8+ rain days, so always pack a compact umbrella or waterproof jacket regardless of season.
  • For the best balance of mild weather and manageable crowds, aim for late May or September, when highs sit around 20–22°C and rain days ease slightly.
  • Avoid July if you dislike crowds and storms: it is both the warmest and wettest month, with peak tourist numbers.
  • Winter travelers (December–February) should bring insulated layers, gloves, and waterproof boots, as lows drop below freezing and damp cold feels sharp.
  • Summer visitors should book accommodation and major attractions well in advance, and plan indoor alternatives for afternoon thunderstorm risk.
