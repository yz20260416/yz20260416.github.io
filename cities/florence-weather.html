Florence Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Travel Planning

Florence's climate rewards careful timing. Based on 2021–2025 averages, the sweet spots are April–May and September–October, when highs sit comfortably between 19°C and 27°C and rain days hover around 10–12 per month—pack a light rain layer but expect plenty of dry sightseeing hours. The toughest months are July and August, when highs near 33°C and humidity makes midday walking exhausting, so plan museums for the afternoon and outdoor sights for early morning. November and December are the wettest (12.8 and 13.6 rain days), meaning short showers are likely on most trips—bring waterproof shoes and keep indoor backup plans.

Practical Tips

  • Pack for rain year-round: Every month averages at least 5 rain days, and autumn–winter months exceed 10—a compact umbrella and waterproof shoes are essential.
  • Beat the summer heat: In July and August (highs near 33°C), schedule outdoor sights before 10am and reserve air-conditioned museums for the afternoon.
  • Target shoulder seasons: April–May and September–October offer the best balance of mild temperatures and sightseeing comfort, though rain gear remains wise.
  • Book ahead in high season: Crowds peak from April through September—reserve Uffizi and Accademia tickets online well in advance.
  • Embrace winter value: January–February bring cold mornings and rain but low crowds and cheaper rates, ideal for museum-focused trips.
