Rome 3-Day Itinerary

Rome wasn’t built in a day, but you can experience its greatest hits in three unforgettable days. This balanced itinerary blends ancient wonders, Renaissance art, and neighborhood charm—with just enough gelato stops to keep you energized. Each day includes a morning, afternoon, and evening schedule plus hand-picked restaurant recommendations for lunch and dinner. Whether you’re a first-timer or returning, these three days will leave you saying “Roma, non basta una vita” (Rome, a lifetime is not enough).

Day 1 – Ancient Rome & Trastevere

TimeActivityRestaurant / Details
8:30 AM Colosseum & Roman Forum — pre-booked combo ticket (skip-the-line). Start at the Colosseum, then stroll through the Forum and Palatine Hill. Caffè Capitolino (quick espresso & cornetto before entry)
12:30 PM Lunch near Monti — explore the charming Monti neighborhood, known for boutiques and trattorias. Trattoria da Enzo al 29 (carbonara, cacio e pepe) — arrive early or book.
2:30 PM Pantheon & Piazza Navona — walk to the Pantheon (free entry), then Piazza Navona with Bernini’s Fountain of the Four Rivers. Gelato at Giolitti (near Pantheon) — classic Roman gelateria.
5:00 PM Campo de’ Fiori & Trastevere stroll — cross the Tiber into Trastevere. Wander cobblestone alleys and visit Santa Maria in Trastevere. Optional aperitivo at Bar San Calisto (cheap, local vibe) or Freni e Frizioni (trendy).
8:00 PM Dinner in Trastevere — classic Roman dishes in a lively setting. Da Tonino (off-the-beaten-path, authentic) or Osteria der Belli (near Piazza del Popolo, but Trastevere branch is cozy).

Tip: Book Colosseum tickets online at least 3–4 weeks in advance. The combined ticket includes the Forum and Palatine Hill and is valid for 24 hours.
