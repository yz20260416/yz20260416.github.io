Dubai Travel Cost Guide

Dubai has a reputation as a luxury destination, but the reality is far more nuanced. The city offers everything from budget-friendly backpacker hostels and street-side shawarma to ultra-luxury suites and Michelin-starred dining. Your daily spend depends heavily on your travel style, the season you visit, and how far in advance you book. This guide breaks down realistic costs across three travel styles—budget, mid-range, and luxury—so you can plan a trip that matches your wallet. All figures are in U.S. dollars (USD) unless otherwise noted, with the UAE dirham (AED) referenced where helpful (roughly 3.67 AED = 1 USD).

Accommodation Breakdown

  • Budget: Hostels in Bur Dubai and Deira offer dorm beds from $25–$45 per night. Basic private rooms in older districts start around $50–$70. Look near Al Rigga or BurJuman metro stations for convenience.
  • Mid-Range: Three- and four-star hotels in Al Barsha, Deira, and Business Bay typically run $90–$200 per night. Apartments with kitchenettes (great for families) are common in this range, often around $120–$180.
  • Luxury: Iconic properties like Atlantis The Palm, Burj Al Arab, and Armani Hotel start around $350–$500 per night and climb steeply. Beachfront resorts on Jumeirah and Palm Jumeirah average $500–$1,000+, with villas and suites far exceeding that.

Food & Dining Breakdown

  • Budget: Shawarma ($2–$4), falafel wraps, karak chai ($0.50–$1), and supermarket groceries keep daily food costs at $15–$30. Food courts in malls (e.g., Al Ghurair, Ibn Battuta) offer meals for $5–$10.
  • Mid-Range: Casual restaurants and cafes serve mains for $10–$20. A sit-down dinner for two at a mid-tier restaurant runs $40–$70. Friday brunch at mid-range venues starts around $50–$80 per person.
  • Luxury: Fine dining at celebrity-chef restaurants typically starts at $100–$150 per person (often without alcohol, which is expensive and only served in licensed venues). Iconic Friday brunches range from $100 to $250+ per person.

Transport Breakdown

  • Metro: The Dubai Metro is clean, cheap, and efficient. A single trip costs roughly $1.50–$2.50 depending on zones; a day pass is around $5.50.
