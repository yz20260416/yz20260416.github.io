New York's weather data reveals two clear sweet spots: September and May, when highs of 25.0°C and 21.5°C pair with comfortable lows and moderate rain-day counts, meaning you can plan long walking days and outdoor sightseeing with minimal backup plans. The toughest months are July, with 30.4°C highs and 13.6 rain days—the wettest month—bringing both heat and frequent downpours—and January, when lows of -3.2°C demand serious winter layers. Practically, this means booking spring or early autumn for the most reliable sightseeing conditions, and packing rain gear plus breathable clothing if you visit in summer.

Practical tips

  • Aim for September or October for the best balance of mild temperatures, fewer rain days, and manageable crowds.
  • July is the wettest month (13.6 rain days) and the hottest—if visiting then, plan outdoor activities for mornings and keep indoor options ready.
  • Rain days are high most of the year, so always pack a compact umbrella or waterproof jacket, even in summer.
  • Winter months (January–February) combine sub-zero lows with frequent rain—insulated, waterproof footwear is essential.
  • Book major attractions in advance during high-crowd months (May–August, December) to avoid long queues.
