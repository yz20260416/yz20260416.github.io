New York Weather by Month

New York City experiences a humid subtropical climate with four distinct seasons — from crisp, snowy winters to hot, humid summers. Monthly weather varies significantly, so packing and planning depend heavily on when you visit. Below is a detailed breakdown of average high/low temperatures, typical rainfall, and the best months for sightseeing, festivals, and outdoor activities.
