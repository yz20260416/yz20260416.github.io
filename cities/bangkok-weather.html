Bangkok Weather by Month

Bangkok has a tropical savanna climate, with three distinct seasons: hot (March–May), rainy (June–October), and cool (November–February). Temperatures remain warm year-round, but rainfall and humidity vary significantly. This monthly guide helps you plan the perfect trip—whether you seek sunshine, festival vibes, or fewer crowds.
