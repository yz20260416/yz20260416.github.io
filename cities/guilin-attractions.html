Top Attractions in Guilin

Guilin, in China's Guangxi region, is famous for its surreal karst peaks, winding rivers, and vast cave systems. From the iconic Elephant Trunk Hill in the city to the dragon-backed rice terraces of Longsheng and the limestone wonders around Yangshuo and Lipu, the region offers a remarkable mix of natural landmarks, sacred sites, and underground scenery that rewards a few days of exploration.

  1. Elephant Trunk Hill
    This hill is Guilin's signature landmark and a popular tourist attraction, famed for the rock formation that resembles an elephant dipping its trunk into the river. It is one of the most photographed sights in the city.
  2. Reed Flute Cave
    Also known as "the Palace of Natural Arts," this landmark cave is filled with stalactites, stalagmites, and other dramatic rock formations. It takes its name from the reeds growing outside, which can be fashioned into flutes, and contains more than 70 ink inscriptions of poems and travelogues dating back to 792 AD.
  3. Longsheng Rice Terraces (Longji Rice Terraces)
    Located about 100 kilometres from Guilin in Longji town, these terraced fields wind from the riverside up the mountain slopes. Known as the "Dragon's Spine," they are among the region's most spectacular cultural landscapes.
  4. Moon Hill
    This hill just outside Yangshuo features a wide, semicircular natural arch, the remnant of a limestone cave formed in the phreatic zone. Like most formations in the region it is karst, and it remains a popular tourist attraction.
  5. Silver Cave
    A national AAAA-level scenic spot in Lipu County, about 85 km from Guilin and 18 km from Yangshuo, this floor-type cave runs through 12 hills across three layers. Its crystal-clear stalactites sparkle like silver, giving the cave its name.
  6. Fengyu Cave
    This cave in Dongli Village, Lipu, stretches 5.3 kilometres and includes a 3.3-kilometre subterranean river running through nine mountains. It features a hall of 25,500 square metres and is inhabited by red blind fish.
  7. Jianshan Temple
    This Buddhist temple in Yangshuo County includes a shanmen, Mahavira Hall, meditation room, and dining room. It has a building area of about 5,589 square metres and covers roughly 14,333 square metres.

Practical tips

  • Visit Elephant Trunk Hill and Reed Flute Cave early in the morning to avoid peak crowds, especially in summer.
  • Allow a full day or an overnight stay for the Longsheng Rice Terraces, since they lie about 100 km from Guilin.
  • Silver Cave and Fengyu Cave are both in Lipu County and pair well with a Yangshuo-based itinerary.
  • Wear sturdy shoes for Moon Hill and the terraces, as both involve steep walking and steps.
  • Dress respectfully when visiting Jianshan Temple, an active Buddhist site.
