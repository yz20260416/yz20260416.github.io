Vienna 3-Day Itinerary

Vienna — the city of imperial palaces, coffeehouse culture, and world-class music — rewards travelers who slow down and savor it. This 3-day itinerary balances the must-see landmarks (Schönbrunn, the Hofburg, St. Stephen's Cathedral) with the city's quieter pleasures: a slice of Sachertorte in a historic café, a stroll through the Naschmarkt, and an evening at the opera or a Heuriger wine tavern. The plan is walkable and public-transport-friendly, with restaurant recommendations ranging from quick Würstelstand sausages to refined Viennese classics.

Day 1 — Imperial Vienna: The Inner City (Innere Stadt)

Time Activity Notes & Food
9:00 St. Stephen's Cathedral (Stephansdom) Start at the city's heart. Climb the South Tower (343 steps) for rooftop views, or take the lift up the North Tower to see the Pummerin bell. Book the catacombs tour if interested.
10:30 Graben & Kohlmarkt shopping streets → Hofburg Palace Walk the elegant pedestrian streets past the Plague Column. At the Hofburg, visit the Imperial Apartments, Sisi Museum, and Silver Collection (combined ticket, ~1.5–2 hrs).
12:45 LUNCH Café Central (Herrengasse 14) Historic coffeehouse where Trotsky and Freud once lingered. Order Wiener Schnitzel or a bowl of goulash, then a Melange coffee and slice of cake. Arrive early or expect a queue.
14:00 Spanish Riding School (morning exercise or tour) or Albertina Museum Check the Lippizaner training schedule in advance (morning exercises usually ~10:00; adjust Day 1 timing if you prefer this). Otherwise, the Albertina's Old Masters and Monet collection plus the Habsburg staterooms are excellent.
16:00 St. Peter's Church (Peterskirche) & Pestsäule area stroll A Baroque gem with free entry; step inside for the frescoed dome. Wander toward the Danube Canal for a breather.
18:30 DINNER Figlmüller (Wollzeile 5) The classic address for a plate-sized Wiener Schnitzel. Reserve ahead or try the Bäckerstraße branch. Vegetarians: try Tafelspitz alternatives nearby or the salad plates.
20:00 Optional: Concert at Musikverein or Konzerthaus Book tickets in advance for a Mozart/Strauss program. Standing tickets are budget-friendly. Alternatively, a nightcap at Loos American Bar (Kärntner Durchgang).

Day 2 — Schönbrunn, Museums & the Naschmarkt

Time Activity Notes & Food
8:30 Schönbrunn Palace (U4 to Schönbrunn) Arrive at opening to beat crowds. Book the Grand Tour (40 rooms) online. Allow 2 hrs inside, then explore the gardens, Gloriette, and Neptune Fountain — free to roam.
11:30 Gloriette Café (inside Schönbrunn gardens) Coffee and a pastry with a panoramic view over the palace and city. Light bites available; good spot before lunch.
12:30 LUNCH Naschmarkt (U4 Kettenbrückengasse) Vienna's famous market street. Graze on falafel, oysters, cheese, and produce; sit-down options include Neni (Middle Eastern) and Zur Eisernen Zeit for Austrian classics.
14:00 MuseumsQuartier — choose one: Leopold Museum (Egon Schiele, Klimt) or mumok (modern art) or Kunsthalle Wien Don't try to do them all. The Leopold is the strongest single pick for Austrian art. The MQ courtyards are great for a coffee break at Café Leopold or the rooftop bar at mumok.
16:30 Kunsthistorisches Museum (KHM) or Belvedere If you have energy: KHM
