Petra, the ancient Nabataean capital carved into the rose-red cliffs of southern Jordan, is far more than the Treasury snapshot most visitors see. Covering roughly 264 square kilometers—with over 800 monuments spread across valleys, high places, and desert wadis—Petra rewards those who slow down. This 3-day itinerary is designed to let you experience the iconic highlights without rushing, then venture beyond the main trail to discover monasteries, royal tombs, and panoramic viewpoints that most day-trippers miss. Each day blends active exploration with strategic rest, and every evening ends with a rewarding meal in Wadi Musa, the gateway town. Plan for early starts, comfortable walking shoes, and plenty of water—Petra is best experienced on foot.

Day 1: The Classic Route – Siq, Treasury, and the Heart of the City

Time Activity Details
6:30 AM Breakfast & departure Eat at your hotel or grab a quick falafel sandwich near the visitor center. Arrive at the Petra gate by 7:00 AM to beat the crowds and heat.
7:00–8:00 AM Walk the Siq The 1.2 km sandstone gorge is the dramatic entrance to Petra. Look for the water channels and cultic niches carved into the walls. The Treasury reveal at the end is unforgettable in morning light.
8:00–9:00 AM The Treasury (Al-Khazneh) Spend time at the iconic facade before tour groups arrive. Climb to the unofficial viewpoint on the ridge opposite for a elevated perspective (ask a local guide for the path).
9:00–11:30 AM Street of Facades & Theatre Walk the main colonnaded street past the Nabataean Theatre (capacity ~8,500) and the Royal Tombs viewpoint. Detour to the Urn Tomb for its sweeping valley views.
11:30 AM–1:00 PM Lunch at Basin Restaurant The only full-service restaurant inside Petra, near the museum. Buffet-style Jordanian and international dishes; expect higher prices but convenient location. Alternatively, pack a picnic.
1:00–3:30 PM Qasr al-Bint & Great Temple Explore the free-standing temple and the excavated Great Temple complex. The Petra Museum near the exit is worth 30–45 minutes for context on Nabataean culture.
4:00 PM Return & rest Walk back through the Siq (or take a golf cart if available). Rest at your hotel; hydrate and stretch.
7:00 PM Dinner at My Mom's Recipe A beloved family-run restaurant in Wadi Musa serving home-style Jordanian cooking—mansaf, maqluba, and fresh mezze. Book ahead in high season.

Day 2: The High Places – Monastery, Sacrifices, and Panoramas

Time Activity Details
6:30 AM Early entry Enter at opening and move briskly through the Siq to the trailhead for the Monastery.
7:00–9:00 AM Monastery Trail (Ad-Deir) An 800-step climb (about 45–60 min) to Petra's largest monument, the 45m-tall Monastery. The morning light is superb and crowds are thin. Rest at the café near the top.
9:00–10:30 AM Best viewpoint in Petra Continue 10 minutes past the Monastery to the "Best View in Petra" ledge—a dramatic panorama over the Wadi Araba desert. Optional: the Lion Triclinium detour.
10:30 AM–12:00 PM Descend & High Place of Sacrifice Return to the basin and take the steep but rewarding trail up to the High Place of Sacrifice (al-Madbah), an ancient altar with 360° views. Descend via the Roman Soldier Tomb route.
12:30–1:30 PM Lunch Snack at the Basin Restaurant or the small café near the Theatre. Carry energy bars and plenty of water regardless.
1:30–3:30 PM Royal Tombs & Silk Tomb Explore the Urn, Silk, Corinthian, and Palace Tombs along the eastern ridge. The colors of the Silk Tomb's stone are striking in afternoon light.
4:00 PM Optional: Petra by Night Held Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday evenings (check current schedule). The Siq and Treasury are lit by hundreds of candles—magical but crowded. Book tickets at the visitor center.
7:30 PM Dinner at Al-Wadi Restaurant Reliable Jordanian and Middle Eastern fare near the main street; generous portions and fair prices. Try the grilled chicken and hummus.

Day 3: Beyond the Main Trail – Lesser-Known Treasures

Time Activity Details
7:00 AM Entry & Little Petra transfer Arrange a taxi or tour to Little Petra (Siq al-Barid), about 15 minutes from Wadi Musa. This mini-Petra has painted biclinia and a charming Siq—quiet and free of crowds.
8:00–10:00 AM Little Petra exploration Wander the narrow gorge, see the "Painted House" frescoes, and climb to the Neolithic village site nearby. Great photography without the crowds.
10:30 AM–12:30 PM Back to Petra: Wadi Farasa Enter Petra again and take the Wadi Farasa trail past the Roman Soldier Tomb, Garden Temple, and Lion Monument—a lush, under-visited valley with fascinating rock-cut tombs.
12:30–1:30 PM Lunch Picnic near the Garden Temple or return to the Basin Restaurant. Stock up on snacks from Wadi Musa before entering.
1:30–3:30 PM Petra Museum & final highlights Visit the excellent Petra Museum (free with ticket) for artifacts and context, then revisit your favorite spots—the Treasury is quieter in late afternoon.
