Singapore Travel Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Planning

Singapore is hot and humid year-round, with average highs of 28.7–30.4°C and lows of 24.0–26.0°C, so heat is a constant rather than a seasonal variable. What really changes is rain: every month averages well over 9 rain days, meaning "High" rainfall by any measure. February is the standout month, with the fewest rain days (17.8) and warm highs near 30°C, making it the best window for outdoor sightseeing. April, November and December are the wettest (26–26.8 rain days), so plan indoor attractions, carry rain gear daily, and expect short, intense afternoon downpours rather than all-day washouts.

Practical tips

  • Treat rain as a daily certainty: every month averages 17.8+ rain days, so carry a compact umbrella or poncho and choose shoes that handle wet pavement.
  • Front-load outdoor activities (Gardens by the Bay, MacRitchie, Sentosa beaches) into mornings, when thunderstorms are less likely than mid-afternoon.
  • Aim for February or July if outdoor time matters most — they have the fewest rain days (17.8 and 19.8) — and avoid April, November and December for beach-heavy trips.
  • Dress for constant heat: highs stay near 30°C all year, so pack light, breathable clothing and plan air-conditioned breaks during the hottest hours.
  • Keep a wet-weather backup list (museums, malls, hawker centers) ready each day, since even the "driest" months still see rain roughly two days in three.
