Dali Travel Weather Guide: When to Go

Dali's weather data tells a clear story: the dry, bright months from November through April are the sweet spot, with March and April standing out for warm highs of 22–25°C, cool nights, and only about 5–6 rain days per month—ideal for exploring the old town, Erhai Lake, and Cangshan Mountain. The worst weather arrives in the summer monsoon, peaking in July and August with roughly 25 rain days each month, so expect frequent downpours, lush green landscapes, and higher humidity. Practically, plan sightseeing for spring and autumn, and if you visit in summer, build flexibility into your itinerary and carry rain gear daily.

Practical Tips

  • Best window: March–April and October–November offer the best balance of warm days, low rain, and manageable crowds.
  • Avoid the monsoon peak: July and August bring roughly 25 rain days per month—if you visit then, keep plans flexible and carry waterproofs daily.
  • Layer for big day–night swings: Even in dry months, lows can drop 10–15°C below daytime highs, so pack a warm jacket for evenings.
  • Dry-season sun: Winter and spring days are bright but the plateau sun is strong—bring sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat year-round.
  • Shoulder-season value: May and October are transitional—expect some rain but fewer tourists and better prices than midsummer.
