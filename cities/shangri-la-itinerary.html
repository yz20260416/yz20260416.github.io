Shangri-La, known in Chinese as Zhongdian (香格里拉), sits at around 3,200 meters on the Tibetan Plateau in northwestern Yunnan. The town is the capital of Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture and the gateway to Tibetan culture, alpine lakes, monasteries, and the dramatic gorges of the upper Yangtze. This 3-day itinerary balances the essential sights with realistic pacing for altitude acclimatization, plus restaurant picks and practical advice for a smooth trip.

Day 1: Old Town, Songzanlin Monastery, and Acclimatization

Time Activity Notes
Morning Arrive and settle in; gentle walk through Dukezong Old Town Take it slow on arrival day. Explore the cobbled lanes, Tibetan shops, and the giant prayer wheel on Guishan Hill (龟山公园).
Afternoon Songzanlin Monastery (松赞林寺) Yunnan's largest Tibetan Buddhist monastery, about 5 km north of town. Allow 2–3 hours. Dress modestly; walk clockwise around the complex.
Evening Dinner in the old town and early rest Keep the evening light. Hydrate and avoid alcohol on the first night at altitude.

Restaurant picks: Compass Café (Western and Tibetan dishes, popular with travelers), Raven's Café (comfort food and coffee), and local Tibetan eateries in the old town serving momo (dumplings), thukpa (noodle soup), and yak butter tea.

Day 2: Pudacuo National Park and Tibetan Villages

Time Activity Notes
Morning Pudacuo National Park (普达措国家公园) About 22 km east of town. Explore Shudu Lake and Bita Lake via boardwalks and shuttle buses. Bring layers — mornings are cold even in summer.
Afternoon Visit a Tibetan village or the Napa Lake grassland Napa Hai (纳帕海) is a seasonal lake and grassland ideal for photography and birdwatching (spring and autumn). Village visits should be arranged respectfully through local guides.
Evening Return to town; dinner and rest Optional: Tibetan cultural show or a quiet evening at a teahouse.

Restaurant picks: Shenying Tibetan Restaurant (traditional Tibetan fare), Flying Tigers Café (mixed menu, good for families), and Karma Café for light meals and coffee.

Day 3: Balagezong or Gorge Excursion and Departure

Time Activity Notes
Morning Balagezong Scenic Area (巴拉格宗) or Tiger Leaping Gorge day trip Balagezong offers canyons, Tibetan villages, and mountain views (allow a full day if going deep into the area). Tiger Leaping Gorge is roughly 2 hours south — a shorter viewpoint visit is possible but tight.
Afternoon Return to Shangri-La; last shopping and old town stroll Pick up Tibetan handicrafts, yak dairy products, or local honey. Confirm transport for departure.
Evening Departure or final dinner If flying out, note that flights can be affected by weather; build in buffer time.

Restaurant picks: Old Town Tibetan Restaurant for a farewell meal, or a hotel restaurant for convenience before departure.

Practical Tips

  • Altitude: At 3,200 m, altitude sickness is a real risk. Take the first day easy, stay hydrated, avoid alcohol, and consider a doctor's advice before travel if you have heart or respiratory conditions.
  • Best season: May–October is warmest and greenest; autumn offers clear skies. Winters are cold and some high areas may be inaccessible. Avoid the week-long National Day holiday (early October) if you dislike crowds.
  • Getting there: Diqing Shangri-La Airport (DIG) has flights from Kunming, Chengdu, and other Chinese cities. Buses connect from Lijiang (about 4 hours) and Kunming (long-distance overnight).
  • Permits: No special permit is needed for Shangri-La town, but some border areas (e.g., toward Deqin) may require checks — carry your passport.
  • Money: ATMs are limited; bring cash. Mobile payments (WeChat Pay, Alipay) are widely accepted but require a Chinese bank setup or international card support.
  • Etiquette: Walk clockwise around monasteries and prayer wheels. Ask before photographing monks or locals. Dress modestly at religious sites.
  • Packing: Sun protection (high UV), warm layers, rain gear, sturdy shoes, and lip balm. Evenings are cold year-round.
  • Language: Mandarin is common; Tibetan is spoken locally. English is limited outside tourist cafés — a translation app helps.
