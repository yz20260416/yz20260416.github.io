Lhasa Travel Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Conditions

Lhasa's high-altitude climate delivers cold, dry winters and mild, wetter summers, with dramatic day-to-night temperature swings year-round. The data shows the best window is October, when highs still reach 16.9°C and rain days drop to just 6.4 — meaning bright, stable skies and comfortable sightseeing. August is the wettest month at 20.4 rain days, so expect frequent afternoon showers and cloudier mountain views. Practically, this means planning major monastery and Potala Palace visits for spring or autumn, packing layers regardless of season, and scheduling outdoor excursions in the morning during summer months before clouds build.

Practical Tips

  • Pack for big temperature swings: Even in summer, lows hover near 11–12°C, so bring layers you can add and remove through the day.
  • Time outdoor plans for mornings: From May to September, rain days are frequent (14–20 per month), and showers typically build in the afternoon.
  • Choose October for the best balance: Mild highs near 17°C and only 6.4 rain days make it the most reliable month for clear mountain views.
  • Book ahead in summer: June–August brings both the warmest weather and peak crowds, so reserve hotels and permits early.
  • Don't underestimate winter sun: December–February is very dry (1–3 rain days) with bright skies, but nights drop below freezing — pack insulated layers and sun protection.
