Amsterdam Weather by Month

Amsterdam has a temperate maritime climate, meaning weather here is famously changeable — you can experience sunshine, wind, and a sudden rain shower all in the same afternoon. Winters are cool and damp rather than freezing, summers are mild and pleasant, and rain falls year-round with no truly dry season. The city sits close to the North Sea, so breezes are constant and temperatures rarely swing to extremes. Below is a month-by-month breakdown of average highs, lows, and rainfall to help you plan your trip, followed by practical tips for packing and timing your visit.

June 21°C / 70°F 12°C / 54°F ~65 mm ~13 Warm, long daylight hours (sunset after 10 pm near midsummer)
July 23°C / 73°F 14°C / 57°F ~75 mm ~14 Warmest month; busiest tourist season, occasional heat spikes

October 15°C / 59°F 8°C / 46°F ~85 mm ~16 Cooler and wetter; autumn colours along the canals
November 10°C / 50°F 5°C / 41°F ~85 mm ~18 Grey, blustery and damp; one of the wettest months
December 7°C / 45°F 2°C / 36°F ~85 mm ~18 Cold and dark (sunset ~4:30 pm); festive lights and cosy cafés

Figures are long-term monthly averages and vary year to year. Amsterdam receives roughly 800–850 mm of rain annually, spread across 170+ days — light drizzle is more common than heavy downpours.

Best Months to Visit

  • April–May (best overall): Mild temperatures, the driest stretch of the year, blooming tulips and fewer crowds than midsummer. May offers the best balance of pleasant weather and manageable tourism.
  • June–August (warmest, busiest): Ideal for outdoor cafés, canal cruises and long evenings, but expect peak prices, packed museums and the highest rainfall of summer in August.
  • September–October (shoulder season): Comfortable temperatures and lighter crowds; a good value option if you don't mind occasional rain.
  • November–February (cheapest, quietest): Cold, grey and wet, but hotel rates drop sharply and museums are calm. December's festive atmosphere is a draw despite the short days.

Practical Tips

  • Pack for all seasons in one day: Layers are essential year-round. A waterproof jacket or compact umbrella is worth carrying even in summer — showers arrive fast and pass quickly.
  • Don't over-pack heavy winter gear: Amsterdam rarely drops far below freezing. A warm coat, hat, gloves and waterproof shoes cover most winter needs.
  • Book accommodation early for April–May and July–August: These are peak periods, and prices rise significantly. Shoulder-season travel (late September, early March) offers better value.
  • Embrace the wind: Cycling is the local way to get around, but headwinds off the North Sea can be fierce. Rent a bike with good brakes and lights, and dress for the breeze.
  • Check daylight hours: In December the sun sets around 4:30 pm, while in June it stays light until nearly 11 pm — plan museum visits and outdoor activities accordingly.
  • Rain rarely ruins a day: Most precipitation is light drizzle. Locals simply keep moving — a hooded raincoat beats an umbrella in the wind.
  • Watch for summer heat spikes: July and August can occasionally reach 30°C+ (86°F+), and many older buildings lack air conditioning — check your hotel's cooling options if travelling in midsummer.
