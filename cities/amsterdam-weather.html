Amsterdam Travel Weather Guide

Amsterdam's weather is mild but famously changeable, and the data shows two clear patterns travelers should plan around. August is statistically the best month to visit, pairing the year's warmest highs (22.0°C) with comfortable lows (14.3°C) and the fewest rain days of the summer (11.0), making it ideal for canal walks, terrace dining, and day trips. Conversely, October and November are the wettest stretch, with 16.2 and 15.8 rain days respectively; expect frequent showers and pack waterproof layers. Winter is cold but rarely freezing, while spring warms steadily. Practically, book accommodation early for July–August crowds, and favor late spring or early autumn for thinner crowds at reasonable temperatures.

Practical tips

  • Rain is frequent year-round (every month exceeds 9 rain days), so always pack a compact waterproof jacket and umbrella regardless of season.
  • Target August for the warmest, driest summer conditions, or late spring/early autumn for milder temperatures with fewer crowds.
  • Avoid October and November if you dislike rain—they are the wettest months, so prioritize indoor sights or flexible itineraries.
  • Book accommodation and major attractions well in advance for June–August, when crowds peak.
  • Layer clothing for spring and autumn, when cool mornings and mild afternoons alternate with passing showers.
