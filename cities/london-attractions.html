London Top 10 Attractions

From the historic Tower of London to the modern London Eye, the capital offers an extraordinary mix of royal heritage, cutting‑edge culture, and free world‑class museums. Whether you're a first‑time visitor or a seasoned Londoner, these ten iconic attractions capture the city's spirit. Below you'll find descriptions, current ticket prices (2025), and essential visiting tips to help you plan a smooth, memorable trip.

🏛️ The 10 Must‑See Attractions

Attraction Description Ticket price (approx.) Visiting tips
1. Tower of London UNESCO site, home to the Crown Jewels, medieval fortress, and 1,000 years of history. See the White Tower and meet the Yeoman Warders (Beefeaters). £33.60 (adult)
£16.80 (child 5–15)		 Book online in advance (save ~£2). Arrive at opening (9am) to beat crowds. The Crown Jewels queue is shortest early or late afternoon. Allow 3–4 hours.
2. British Museum World‑famous collection spanning 2 million years: Rosetta Stone, Parthenon sculptures, Egyptian mummies. Free entry to permanent galleries. Free (donation suggested)
Special exhibitions £15–£25		 Free timed tickets recommended (book online). Weekdays are quieter. Don’t miss the Great Court; start with the Rosetta Stone early. 3–5 hours.
3. London Eye Iconic 135m observation wheel on the South Bank. 30‑minute rotation offers panoramic views over Parliament, St Paul’s, and beyond. £29–£38 (adult)
£24–£33 (child)		 Book online for cheaper rates and fast‑track. Sunset or early evening rides are magical. Standard ticket vs. fast‑track: standard is fine if flexible.
4. Westminster Abbey Gothic masterpiece, coronation church since 1066, resting place of monarchs
