Fiji 3-Day Itinerary

Fiji is an archipelago of more than 330 islands, and while a week or two is ideal for island-hopping, a well-planned 3-day trip can still deliver the essence of this South Pacific paradise. This itinerary is designed for travelers basing themselves in the Nadi–Denarau–Mamanuca corridor, which offers the best balance of accessibility, cultural experiences, and world-class beaches. You'll spend Day 1 exploring the mainland and its markets, Day 2 on a pristine outer island with snorkeling and beach time, and Day 3 immersing yourself in Fijian village life and natural wonders before departure. The pace is active but realistic — and every day includes memorable meals at some of Fiji's best-loved restaurants.

At-a-Glance Daily Schedule
