Jiuzhaigou Valley, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Sichuan Province, China, is famous for its turquoise lakes, waterfalls, and colorful forests. The main entry point is Jiuzhaigou Huanglong Airport (JZH) or the town of Zhangzha (also called Jiuzhaigou town), about 40 km from the park gate. Budgets below are per person per day in Chinese yuan (CNY), based on a 3-day visit during shoulder season. Peak season (July–October) and holidays raise prices by 30–100%, especially accommodation. Park entry is a fixed cost regardless of travel style.

Typical 3-Day Trip Totals

  • Backpacker: roughly 1,200–1,900 CNY (about US$170–270)
  • Mid-range: roughly 2,400–4,200 CNY (about US$340–590)
  • Luxury: roughly 5,300–12,800 CNY (about US$740–1,800)

Practical Tips

  • Buy park tickets online in advance; daily visitor numbers are capped and peak-season tickets sell out.
  • Stay in Zhangzha town for the widest range of budget and mid-range options; luxury resorts cluster closer to the park entrance.
  • Bring cash — smaller guesthouses and eateries may not accept cards or mobile payment from foreign accounts.
  • Visit in late April–June or September for better prices and fewer crowds than the July–October peak.
  • Altitude reaches 3,000+ m in parts of the park; budget for a rest day if you fly in directly.
  • The park shuttle bus is included in the entry ticket and is the only way to move between the major valleys.
  • Combine with Huanglong Scenic Area (about 2 hours by road) for a full itinerary; add 200–400 CNY for transport and entry.
