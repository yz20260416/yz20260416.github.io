Budapest Travel Cost Guide

Budapest is one of Europe’s most affordable capitals, offering grand architecture, thermal baths, ruin bars, and a rich culinary scene without the sticker shock of Paris or Vienna. Whether you’re a backpacker counting every forint or a luxury seeker looking for five‑star pampering, your daily budget can flex dramatically. This guide breaks down real‑world costs for accommodation, food, transport, and activities across three travel styles: Budget Mid‑range Luxury. All prices are in Hungarian forints (HUF) with approximate EUR equivalents (1 EUR ≈ 390 HUF, as of 2025) to help you plan.

💱 1 EUR ≈ 390 HUF · 1000 HUF ≈ 2.56 EUR · Prices are per person, per day unless noted.
