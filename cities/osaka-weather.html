Osaka Weather by Month

Osaka sits in Japan's Kansai region on the Seto Inland Sea coast, giving it a humid subtropical climate with four sharply defined seasons. Summers are hot and muggy, winters are cold but rarely freezing, and the rainy season (tsuyu) arrives in early summer while typhoon season peaks in late summer and early autumn. Understanding the monthly weather helps you pack correctly, choose the right festivals, and avoid the crowds (or embrace them). Below is a month-by-month breakdown of typical temperatures and rainfall, followed by guidance on the best times to visit.

Figures are long-term climate averages and will vary year to year. Rainfall totals are monthly; "rainy days" counts days with measurable precipitation.

Seasonal Summary

  • Spring (March–May): Mild and increasingly sunny. Cherry blossoms typically peak in late March to early April in Osaka Castle Park and Expo '70 Commemorative Park. May is arguably the single most comfortable month.
  • Summer (June–August): Hot, humid, and rainy at the start. The tsuyu rainy season usually runs early June to mid-July, then gives way to intense heat (often 33–35°C / 91–95°F) with high humidity. Pack breathable clothing and stay hydrated.
  • Autumn (September–November): September can still be hot and is the height of typhoon season; October and November bring some of the year's clearest, most pleasant weather and vibrant foliage.
  • Winter (December–February): Cold but generally dry and sunny. Snow is possible but rarely accumulates. Mornings and evenings drop near freezing, so layers are essential.

Best Months to Visit

  • Best overall: April, May, October, and November. Comfortable temperatures, lower humidity, and reliable sunshine make these ideal for sightseeing, day trips to Kyoto and Nara, and outdoor dining.
  • Best for cherry blossoms: Late March to early April.
  • Best for autumn colors: Mid-to-late November.
  • Best for budget and fewer crowds: January and February (excluding Lunar New Year travel surges) and early December.
  • Avoid if you dislike heat and humidity: Late July and August. Avoid early-to-mid June if you want to minimize rain, and keep an eye on typhoon forecasts in September.

Practical Tips

  • Pack for humidity in summer: Light, quick-dry fabrics, a portable fan, and a compact umbrella. Osaka's summers feel significantly hotter than the thermometer suggests due to high humidity.
  • Carry a folding umbrella year-round: Even in the "dry" months, brief showers happen. During tsuyu (June–mid-July), a sturdy umbrella and waterproof shoes are worth the luggage space.
  • Layer in winter: Osaka's winters are dry and sunny but chilly, especially near the waterfront and in the evenings. A warm coat plus scarf and gloves covers most days; indoor heating can be strong, so removable layers help.
  • Watch typhoon season (August–September): Check forecasts before traveling, keep your accommodation flexible if possible, and know that trains and flights may be suspended during a typhoon. Most storms pass within a day.
  • Book early for peak seasons: Cherry blossom season (late March–early April), Golden Week (late April–early May), and autumn foliage (November) are the busiest and priciest periods. Reserve hotels and popular restaurants well in advance.
  • Dress for shrine and temple visits: Comfortable walking shoes are essential year-round — Osaka's sights involve plenty of steps and pavement, and summer heat makes breathable footwear especially important.
  • Stay hydrated and seek shade in summer: Osaka's urban heat island effect makes midday temperatures intense. Plan indoor activities (museums, shopping arcades like Tenjinbashisuji) during the hottest hours.
