Osaka Travel Weather Guide: Month-by-Month Conditions

Osaka's real 2021–2025 averages show a city of strong seasonal swings. The most comfortable months are April, May and October, when highs sit between 19.8°C and 23.6°C with moderate rain-day counts — ideal for walking between temples, markets and Dotonbori without heat stress. The hardest months are July and August: highs of 32.1°C and 33.2°C combine with lows near 25°C and 15 rain days each, meaning muggy nights and frequent downpours. Practically, book spring or autumn for sightseeing, and if you visit midsummer, plan indoor or early-morning activities and carry rain gear daily.

Practical tips

  • Book April, May or October for the best balance of mild temperatures and manageable sightseeing, but always pack a rain jacket — those months still average 10+ rain days.
  • Avoid July and August if you dislike heat and humidity; if you must visit, schedule outdoor sights before 10 a.m. and after 5 p.m.
  • Carry a compact umbrella year-round: every month averages at least 5 rain days, and May through September exceed 13.
  • Winter (January–February) is the driest window and good for budget travel, but lows near 0–1°C mean warm coats and layers are needed.
  • Use rain-day counts to plan indoor backups — Osaka's covered arcades, museums and food halls are ideal on wet days.
