Kunming Weather Guide: When to Visit the "Spring City"

Kunming earns its "Spring City" nickname honestly — the data shows highs hovering between 15°C and 26°C year-round. For travelers, March and April are the sweet spot: highs of 23–26°C, lows of 9–13°C, and only 5–7 rain days per month make these the driest, most comfortable months for sightseeing. In contrast, June through August brings the rainy season, with 24–26 rain days per month — expect daily afternoon showers, lush green scenery, and the need for waterproof gear. Winter (December–February) is dry and mild by day but chilly at night, with lows near 4–6°C and occasional frost.

Practical tips

  • Pack layers year-round: Large day–night temperature swings (10°C+) are common in every month, so bring a light jacket even in summer.
  • Time your visit for March–April: These months combine the warmest dry weather with moderate rain days — the best overall window for outdoor sightseeing.
  • Rainy season (May–September): Showers typically arrive in the afternoon; plan outdoor activities for mornings and always carry a compact umbrella or rain shell.
  • Winter nights are cold: December–February lows of 3–6°C mean warm evening clothing is essential, especially for higher-altitude day trips.
  • Book ahead in July–August: Summer holidays bring peak domestic crowds; reserve accommodation and popular attractions in advance.
