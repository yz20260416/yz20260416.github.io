Madrid Travel Budget Guide: Backpacker, Mid-Range, and Luxury Costs

Madrid is one of Europe's most affordable major capitals, especially compared with London or Paris, but costs still vary widely depending on how you travel. This guide breaks down realistic daily and per-trip budgets for backpackers, mid-range travelers, and luxury visitors, covering accommodation, food, transport, and activities. Figures are per person per day unless otherwise noted, and reflect typical 2024–2025 prices in euros.

Accommodation

Type Typical Price (per night) Notes
Hostel dorm bed €20–35 Central areas (Sol, La Latina, Malasaña) cost more; book early in peak season
Budget private room / guesthouse €55–90 Often outside the historic center or near metro lines
Mid-range hotel (3–4★) €90–160 Rates rise sharply during fairs, holidays, and summer weekends
Luxury hotel (5★) €300–700+ Gran Vía, Salamanca, and Retiro-area properties command the highest rates
Apartment rental (mid-range) €100–180 Good for stays of 3+ nights; check licensing and cancellation terms

Food and Drink

Style Typical Cost Examples
Backpacker €20–30/day Supermarket meals, menú del día (€12–15), tapas bars, café con leche (€1.50–2.50)
Mid-range €45–75/day Sit-down lunches, a dinner with wine (€25–40), market food halls like Mercado de San Miguel
Luxury €120–250+/day Fine dining tasting menus (€100–200+), Michelin-starred restaurants, cocktail bars
  • Menú del día: A fixed multi-course lunch (€12–18) is the best value for budget and mid-range travelers.
  • Tapas culture: Many bars offer free or cheap tapas with drinks; a "caña" (small beer) runs €2–3.50.
  • Vermouth hour: Weekend vermut (€3–5) with a small snack is a classic Madrid ritual.

Transport

Option Cost Notes
Single metro/bus ticket €1.50–2.00 Metro surcharge applies to airport stations
10-ride ticket (Metrobús) €12.20 Shared across metro and EMT buses; best value for short stays
Tourist travel card (1–7 days) €10–35 Unlimited zone A travel; good for heavy sightseeing days
Airport transfer (Metro) ~€5–7 Includes airport supplement; slower than taxi
Taxi / rideshare €10–20 city trips; €30–35 airport Fixed airport fare applies from the official taxi rank
Bike rental (BiciMAD or private) €5–15/day Good for central and Retiro-area exploration

Practical Tips

  • Book accommodation early: Madrid hosts major trade fairs and events that can double hotel prices; check the calendar before booking.
  • Eat lunch like a local: The menú del día at lunchtime is significantly cheaper than dinner for the same quality of food.
