Chengdu Travel Weather Guide

Chengdu's climate is humid subtropical, and the data shows a clear split between a cool, dry winter and a long, wet summer. The best months to visit are October and November: October still averages a pleasant 21.1°C high, but November dries out dramatically to just 5.8 rain days, making it the sweet spot for comfortable sightseeing. The worst weather comes from July through September, when highs of 28–32°C combine with 16+ rain days per month, meaning you should pack rain gear, plan indoor backups, and expect muggy conditions. Winter is mild but grey, with December being the driest and least crowded month.

Practical tips

  • Always carry a compact umbrella or rain jacket between March and October, when rain days regularly exceed 13 per month.
  • Visit in November or December for the best balance of mild temperatures, low rainfall, and smaller crowds.
  • Avoid July–September if you dislike heat and humidity; if you must go, book air-conditioned hotels and start activities early.
  • Winter days are cool but rarely freezing, so a warm coat plus layers is enough for January and December sightseeing.
  • Keep indoor backup plans (museums, teahouses, malls) ready, since sudden showers are common in Chengdu's wetter months.
