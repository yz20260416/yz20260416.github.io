Here is a comprehensive travel guide section for Budapest, formatted as HTML. Budapest 3-Day Itinerary – Travel Guide

Budapest, the “Pearl of the Danube,” blends imperial history, thermal baths, ruin bars, and a vibrant culinary scene. This 3-day itinerary covers the essential highlights — from the Buda Castle District to the Jewish Quarter — with realistic daily schedules, top activities, and hand-picked restaurant recommendations. You’ll experience both the classic and contemporary sides of Hungary’s capital without feeling rushed.
