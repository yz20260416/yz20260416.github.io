Bora Bora Travel Cost Guide

Bora Bora is the crown jewel of French Polynesia — a volcanic island ringed by a turquoise lagoon and dotted with overwater bungalows. It is undeniably a bucket-list destination, but it also has a reputation as one of the most expensive places in the world. The good news? With careful planning, Bora Bora can fit a range of budgets, from a modest guesthouse stay to an ultra-luxury overwater villa. This guide breaks down real-world costs for accommodation, food, transport, and activities across three travel styles — budget, mid-range, and luxury — so you can plan with confidence. All figures are in U.S. dollars (USD) and reflect typical 2024–2025 prices, with the local currency being the French Pacific Franc (XPF).

At-a-Glance Daily Budget Estimates

These estimates cover one traveler per day, excluding international airfare. Prices assume double occupancy for accommodation (per-person share) and include meals, local transport, and activities.
