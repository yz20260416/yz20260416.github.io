Top Attractions in Sanya

Sanya, on the southern tip of Hainan Island, blends tropical beaches, Buddhist monuments, and rainforest scenery into one of China's most rewarding warm-weather destinations. Whether you come for the surf at Dadonghai, the scale of the Nanshan statue, or the glass walkways above Yalong Bay, the city offers a compact mix of natural and cultural highlights for international visitors.

  1. Guanyin of Nanshan — A 108-metre statue of the bodhisattva Guanyin, one of the tallest in the world, standing on the south coast atop the Nanshan Temple grounds. The sheer scale and seaside setting make it Sanya's most iconic landmark.
  2. Nanshan Temple (Sanya) — A Buddhist temple in Sanya that anchors the Nanshan cultural complex around the great Guanyin statue. Visitors come for the temple halls, gardens, and coastal views.
  3. Tianya Haijiao — Meaning "Edges of Heaven, Corners of the Sea," this popular attraction 24 km west of the city is celebrated as China's symbolic southernmost point. On clear days, offshore islets are visible from the cape.
  4. Yalong Bay — A 7.5-kilometre beach in southeast Sanya, also known as the Yalong Bay National Resort. Its clean sand and calm water make it the area's premier resort beach.
  5. Yalong Bay Forest Park Glass Bridge — A 400-metre glass bridge in the Yalong Bay Tropical Paradise Forest Park, the first of its kind in Hainan. The tempered-glass walkway reaches 100 metres above the ground below.
  6. Dadonghai — The urban beach about two kilometres east of the city centre, where Sanya's beach culture began. It is a busy strip of sand with plenty of water sports on offer.
  7. Yanoda Rainforest Cultural Tourism Zone — A protected rainforest near Sanya in Baoting County, rated a AAAAA scenic spot in 2012. It offers a taste of Hainan's tropical interior and Li and Miao cultural displays.
  8. Atlantis Sanya — An ocean-themed luxury hotel and resort that doubles as a visitor draw with its marine-themed attractions. It is a convenient base for families exploring the coast.
  9. Phoenix Island — An artificial island resort dubbed the "Oriental Dubai," built just offshore from Sanya. Its striking skyline and waterfront setting make it a photogenic stop.
  10. Luobi Cave — A karst cave beneath Yin Ridge, about 15 km from Sanya city, whose name means "hanging pen cave." It appeals to travellers interested in geology and off-the-beach exploring.

Practical tips

  • Visit Nanshan and Tianya Haijiao on the same day — both lie west of the city and pair well for a full-day excursion.
  • Go to Dadonghai and Yalong Bay in the early morning or late afternoon to avoid peak sun and crowds; water sports are busiest midday.
  • Book the Yalong Bay glass bridge and Yanoda rainforest in advance during holidays, as AAAAA-rated sites draw large domestic crowds.
  • The dry, cooler months from November to March are the most comfortable for sightseeing; summer brings heat, humidity, and occasional typhoons.
  • Sanya's sights are spread out — arrange transport or join a tour for the western cluster (Nanshan, Tianya Haijiao) and the eastern beaches separately.
