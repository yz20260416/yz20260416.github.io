Sanya Travel Guide: Top 10 Must-See Attractions

Sanya, on the southern tip of Hainan Island, is China's premier tropical beach destination, known for palm-lined bays, warm turquoise water, and year-round sunshine. Beyond the beaches, the city offers cultural sites, mountain viewpoints, and family-friendly parks. This guide covers the top 10 attractions with ticket prices, opening hours, and practical tips. Prices and hours can change seasonally, so confirm details before visiting.

  1. Yalong Bay (亚龙湾)

    A 7-kilometer crescent of fine white sand and calm, clear water, widely regarded as Sanya's best beach. The bay is lined with resort hotels, and the public beach area is free to access.

    • Tickets: Free (public beach); sunbed and umbrella rentals typically CNY 50–100
    • Hours: Open 24 hours; swimming recommended during daylight
    • Tips: Arrive before 10 a.m. to secure shade; the eastern end near the public access point is less crowded. Strong sun—bring reef-safe sunscreen.

  2. Wuzhizhou Island (蜈支洲岛)

    A small offshore island northeast of Sanya famed for coral reefs, snorkeling, and diving. The island has a visitor cap, and ferry tickets include admission.

    • Tickets: Approximately CNY 144 including round-trip ferry (diving and water sports extra)
    • Hours: Typically 8:00–17:30; last ferry back around 17:00–17:30
    • Tips: Book online in advance during holidays—daily visitor numbers are limited. Go early for calmer water and shorter queues. Water sports packages are cheaper booked as bundles.

  3. Nanshan Buddhism Cultural Park (南山文化旅游区)

    Home to the 108-meter Guanyin statue standing on a man-made island in the sea, one of the tallest Buddhist statues in the world. The sprawling park also includes temples and gardens.

    • Tickets: Approximately CNY 129–150 (park shuttle extra, around CNY 30)
    • Hours: Typically 8:00–17:30
    • Tips: The park is large—buy the shuttle pass to save walking. Dress modestly for temple areas. Allow at least half a day.

  4. Tianya Haijiao (天涯海角, "Edge of the Sky, Corner of the Sea")

    A coastal scenic area famous for its engraved boulders at the symbolic southern edge of China, with a long beach and landscaped paths. A classic photo stop and popular with couples.

    • Tickets: Approximately CNY 68–81
    • Hours: Typically 7:30–18:00
    • Tips: Visit in late afternoon for softer light and fewer tour groups. Combine with Nanshan, which is nearby on the same western route.

  5. Luhuitou Park (鹿回头公园)

    A hilltop park overlooking Sanya Bay and the city, named for the legend of a deer turning its head. Best known for sunset and night views of the skyline and harbor.

    • Tickets: Park admission around CNY 25–42; cable car extra
    • Hours: Typically 7:30–22:00
    • Tips: Go about an hour before sunset for the best light. The walk up takes 30–40 minutes; the cable car saves time.

  6. Sanya Bay and Coconut Dream Corridor (三亚湾·椰梦长廊)

    A long public beach stretching along Sanya Bay, backed by a palm-lined promenade ideal for walking, cycling, and sunset viewing. Free and accessible, it is the city's most convenient beach.

    • Tickets: Free
    • Hours: Open 24 hours
    • Tips: Sunsets here are excellent; the corridor is about 20 kilometers long, so rent a bike or use a taxi to cover sections. Swimming is less ideal than at Yalong Bay due to boat traffic.

  7. Daxiaodongtian Scenic Area (大小洞天)

    A coastal Taoist scenic area with rock formations, ancient inscriptions, and seaside gardens, often paired with Nanshan on the western circuit. Quieter than the major parks.

    • Tickets: Approximately CNY 75–98
    • Hours: Typically 8:00–17:30
    • Tips: Combine with Tianya Haijiao and Nanshan for a full western-route day; hire a car or join a day tour to avoid long taxi waits.

  8. Yanoda Rainforest Cultural Tourism Zone (呀诺达雨林文化旅游区)

    A tropical rainforest park about 35 kilometers north of Sanya in Baoting County, with boardwalk trails, waterfalls, and zipline activities. A good escape from beach heat.

    • Tickets: Approximately CNY 125–170 depending on package (shuttle included; activities extra)
    • Hours: Typically 8:00–17:00
    • Tips: Wear grippy shoes—trails are wet and slippery. Bring insect repellent. It is cooler and rainier than the coast; a light rain jacket helps.

  9. Atlantis Sanya Aquaventure Waterpark (亚特兰蒂斯水世界)

    A large waterpark at Atlantis Sanya on Haitang Bay, with slides, a lazy river, and family areas. Popular with families and open to non-hotel guests.

    • Tickets: Approximately CNY 300–400 for adults; child rates lower (varies by season)
    • Hours: Typically 10:00–18:00; confirm seasonal changes
    • Tips: Book online for discounts and arrive at opening. Lockers cost extra. The adjacent Lost Chambers Aquarium is a good rainy-day alternative.

  10. Haitang Bay (海棠湾) and Duty-Free City

    A newer resort bay east of the city with quieter beaches and the large Sanya International Duty Free Shopping Complex nearby, making it a beach-and-shopping combination.

    • Tickets: Beach free; duty-free mall free entry
    • Hours: Beach open 24 hours; mall typically 10:00–22:00
    • Tips: Bring your passport for duty-free purchases and check allowance rules. The bay is windier and wavier than Yalong Bay—better for strolling than calm swimming.

Practical Tips

  • Best time to visit: November to April offers dry, comfortable weather. Avoid the Lunar New Year period for peak crowds and prices; summer is hot and humid with typhoon risk (June–October).
  • Getting around: Taxis and ride-hailing apps are convenient; agree on meter use or fares. The high-speed rail connects Sanya with Haikou. Attractions on the western route (Nanshan, Tianya Haijiao, Daxiaodongtian) are best combined in one day with a hired car or tour.
