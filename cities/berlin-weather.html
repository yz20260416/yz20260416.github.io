Berlin Travel Weather Guide

Berlin's weather data shows a clear split between a short, mild-to-warm summer and a long, cool winter. July is the warmest month (avg high 24.7°C, low 15.1°C) but also one of the wettest, with 12.6 rain days, so pack layers and a rain jacket even in midsummer. January is the toughest month for visitors: highs average just 4.6°C, lows hover near freezing, and 13 rain days make it the wettest of the year. The sweet spot is May–June and September, when temperatures are comfortable (highs 19–25°C) and rain days drop to 6.4–10.2, meaning fewer washouts and more time outdoors.

Practical tips

  • Rain is frequent year-round in Berlin — even the driest month (September, 6.4 rain days) sees showers, so always pack a compact waterproof jacket or umbrella.
  • Layer clothing between April and October, as daytime highs can be 10–15°C warmer than nighttime lows.
  • For the best weather-to-crowd balance, aim for late May, June or September, when temperatures are comfortable and rain days are relatively low.
  • Winter visitors (December–February) should bring insulated, waterproof footwear and expect highs near 4–6°C with rain rather than reliable snow.
  • Book major indoor attractions in advance during July and August, when peak crowds coincide with the highest chance of summer showers.
