Lisbon Top 10 Attractions

Lisbon, the sun-drenched capital of Portugal, blends historic charm with a vibrant modern edge. From Moorish castles to world-class museums and iconic yellow trams, the city offers endless discoveries. Below are the 10 must-visit attractions — with descriptions, current ticket prices (2025), and insider tips to help you make the most of your trip.

Attraction Description Ticket price (adult) Visiting tips
1. Belém Tower 16th-century fortified tower and UNESCO site, symbol of Portugal's maritime discoveries. Stunning views from the terrace. €8 (tower only)
€12 combined with Jerónimos		 Go early (9am) or late afternoon to avoid queues. Combine with Jerónimos Monastery. Closed Mondays.
2. Jerónimos Monastery Manueline masterpiece, final resting place of Vasco da Gama. The cloister is breathtaking. €12 (cloister + church)
€18 combined with Belém Tower		 Buy the combined ticket online. The church is free but separate entrance. Closed Mondays.
3. Castelo de São Jorge Moorish castle on the highest hill, panoramic views over the city and Tagus River. Peacocks roam the grounds. €15 (adult)
€7.5 students/seniors		 Wear comfortable shoes – cobblestones. Sunset is magical but crowded; arrive 1h before.
4. Alfama & Tram 28 Lisbon's oldest district, a maze of narrow streets, fado houses, and viewpoints. Tram 28 is a historic ride through it. Tram ride: €3.10 (single)
Alfama: free to wander		 Tram 28 gets packed – board at Martim Moniz or Graça early. Watch for pickpockets. Walk instead for hidden gems.
