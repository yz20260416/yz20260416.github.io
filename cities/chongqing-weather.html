Chongqing Travel Weather Guide

Chongqing's real 2021–2025 averages reveal a city of extremes: August is the worst month to visit, with highs near 34.7°C and lows around 26.3°C, creating relentless heat and humidity that make midday sightseeing exhausting. Rainfall is also heaviest in spring and autumn—April averages 17.2 rain days and October 16.2—so pack waterproof layers if you travel then. The sweet spot is late October to November or March to early April, when highs sit comfortably between 18°C and 24°C. December and January are the driest and quietest months, though cooler and often overcast.

Practical Tips

  • Avoid August if possible: highs near 35°C plus humidity make outdoor sightseeing draining; if you must go, schedule activities for early morning and evening.
  • Pack rain gear year-round: every month averages at least 7.6 rain days, and spring/autumn months exceed 16—a compact umbrella and waterproof shoes are essential.
  • Target November or December for comfort: mild highs of 12–18°C, lower rain counts, and low crowds offer the best balance for walking and photography.
  • Dress in layers: spring and autumn swing from cool mornings to warm afternoons, and Chongqing's hills mean lots of walking.
  • Plan indoor backups: museums, hotpot restaurants, and covered markets are perfect during the rainy April–June and September–October stretches.
