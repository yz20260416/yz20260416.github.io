Budapest Weather by Month

Budapest enjoys a temperate continental climate, with four distinct seasons. Summers are warm and occasionally hot, winters are chilly and often grey, while spring and autumn bring mild, pleasant conditions. Rainfall is fairly evenly distributed throughout the year, though late spring and early summer tend to be slightly wetter.
