Budapest Travel Weather Guide

Budapest's climate data reveal a city of two travel personalities. June through August brings the most reliable warmth, with July peaking at a 29.5°C average high and only 7.6 rain days — ideal for outdoor sightseeing, river cruises and thermal baths, though you'll share the city with peak crowds. The trade-off comes in late autumn and winter: November and December are the wettest months (9.0 and 8.8 rain days) with highs of just 9.2°C and 4.9°C, meaning short daylight, layered clothing and indoor-focused itineraries. For a sweet spot, aim for May or September, when mild temperatures and moderate rain days balance comfort against smaller crowds.

Practical tips

  • Pack layers year-round: Budapest's continental climate swings sharply between daytime highs and nighttime lows, especially in spring and autumn.
  • April, May and September are the rainiest months (9+ rain days) — bring a compact umbrella and waterproof shoes.
  • For the best balance of weather and crowds, target late May, early June or October, avoiding both peak summer heat and winter damp.
  • In July and August, sightsee in the early morning and use afternoons for shaded thermal baths or indoor attractions to avoid peak heat.
  • Winter travelers should plan around short daylight: book indoor highlights such as the Parliament, baths and museums for afternoons.
